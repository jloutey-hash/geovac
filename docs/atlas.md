# The GeoVac Atlas: the price list of released axes

*One row per door out of the compact skeleton. For each: what the door releases, what survives it, the number class that enters as the price, the verdict, and where the record lives.*

Started 2026-10-15 (PI-directed). This is the door-indexed view of the mission statement's deliverable (CLAUDE.md §1: "the atlas of that boundary"). It is an **index, not a record**: every row points at the seam row (`docs/forced_free_seam.md`, the per-entry catalogue behind Paper 57), the owning paper section, and the frozen falsifier. Mechanism, numbers and tiers live at those pointers (one record per fact, CLAUDE.md §13.11 rule 8). A row with a stale pointer fails `tests/test_atlas_register.py`.

## How to read it

The organizing observation (Paper 18 `sec:pattern`, with its attribution paragraph): a compact group has a discrete spectrum, so the compact regime is where the skeleton is rational and bit-exact. That mechanism is Peter–Weyl's and the physics reading is Rovelli's and Liu–Noui's; the converse is false (a confining potential on non-compact space also gives a discrete spectrum, Maz'ya–Shubin; the Paper 24 oscillator is the corpus's own instance), so the operator-level statement is "discrete spectrum with finite multiplicities iff compact resolvent". What the corpus adds is the **accounting**: each axis released toward the continuum carries an identifiable number class as its price, and those prices can be classified. The table below is that accounting.

Three kinds of door appear:

- **Geometric doors** release a label of the skeleton (a second nucleus releases $l$; bending releases $m$). The price is set by the geometry alone.
- **Observer doors** are projections an observer makes to read the skeleton (a measure on the sphere, a heat kernel, a finite time window, a Born measure). The price is a volume slot, a Mellin slot, or an imported measure.
- **Calibration doors** are where a number is consumed, not generated (the value of $\alpha$; the window $\beta$).

**The period ladder.** Read the chemistry rows top to bottom: one centre, rationals and $\pi$ (Paper 58, one-centre classes are exact rationals; the K3 record's one-centre limit is rational); two centres, weight-one logarithms and $E_1$, $\pi$-free (Paper 18 `sec:level2_seed_set`); three centres, an elliptic period, and the class leaves the polylogarithm tower entirely (Paper 18 `sec:level2_genus`, Paper 59 `sec:genus`); a fourth centre adds nothing new (J4); a three-electron chain over two-centre densities with two momenta, a K3 whose holomorphic period is a sum of two products of elliptic periods (J7); four linked densities, the first period not built from elliptic ones (J10). The class is set by the number of distinct two-centre densities a kernel couples, and a second kernel sharing a vertex lifts the curve to a surface (J9, an Observation across the tested instances); neither is the number of nuclei or electrons. The four-body $r_{12}$ bridge (J8) reduces, for $s$ leaves, to a two-electron integral of geminal-dressed densities; over two-centre densities its unreduced form is a four-density tree, so it sits on door 20's rung, and the exact reduction changes the cost, not the class.

**Verdict vocabulary** (CLAUDE.md §1 mission statement): FORCED (derived from the construction, with a frozen falsifier); FREE (consumed as external input, projection chain named, number class classified); WALL (a proven obstruction with the mechanism pinned); OBS (a matched or measured fact with no derivation bridge, including pre-registered results that have not yet earned a paper home). The seam-row status (F / A / Obs / C / W) is the finer grading; the atlas verdict is the coarse one.

## Owed

- **Door 20 landed 2026-10-16** with its home in Paper 59 `sec:four_density`, a guard and seam row J10 (F). Owed: whether the real three-dimensional four-density integral reduces into the one-dimensional moment family.
- **Doors 14 and 17 landed 2026-10-15.** Both now have their home in Paper 59 `sec:beyond` and reduced-precision guards; the seam rows J4 and J7 were promoted to F. Still unguarded: the chain's equal-scale collapse and the generic null (long runs; the registration record carries them).
- **Walls register entries for the $l$ seam and $m$ seam.** Discharged by pointer in `docs/walls/register.md` (PERIODS-TRANSCENDENCE, crystallized 2026-10-16): the $l$-seam wall is the two-kinds-of-sparsity crystallization, and bending's release of $m$ is a door, not a wall.
- **The interval-versus-circle hole (door 7).** Paper 35 states the identification as adopted, not derived, with both caveats (restated 2026-10-16); whether a real-time window forces the circle stays the open falsifier target (CLAUDE.md §1.7 WH7).

## What this is not

It is not a new paper, and it carries no numbers of its own. It does not rank doors, and it does not say which rung comes next: that is the open question the ladder leaves (whether the chain's generic fibre carries content beyond the K3 and elliptic periods, and whether five linked densities go beyond four; Paper 59 `sec:four_density` finds four the first rung not built from elliptic periods). Papers remain under the CLAUDE.md §1.5 rhetoric rule: "forced" and "price" are atlas vocabulary, not ontology language for publication.
