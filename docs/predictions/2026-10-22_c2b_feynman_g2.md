## The question

Y(t) = <rho_i rho_j rho_k f(r_ik) f(r_jk) / r_ij>, f(r) = exp(-gamma r), scales (1, 1, t), t on the
apex, at the fingerprint fibre, as in records QG and g32, now at gamma = 2. Does each composition
factor of Y's minimal annihilator come from one set of lines, with the geminals as imaginary
momenta, as the reading says?

## What I know before computing (disclosed)

- At gamma = 3/2 (records g32, EC, CF): pieces of orders 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 3. The order-three piece is
  F_3's operator twisted by t, singular at t = 0, +-2, oo. The order-two piece C2b is the operator of
  C(t) above. The order-one pieces are algebraic: a rational solution; exponent -5/2 at t = 2;
  exponent -7/2 at t = 3; t^5 (t^2-9)^(-9/2) times a sextic in t^2.
- At gamma = 1 (record QG): the annihilator has order four, R3 R1, with no order-two piece. The reading
  does not explain why C2b is absent there. This is disclosed as unexplained.
- Paper 59: the singular points of Y's annihilator move with gamma, to t = |1 - gamma|, 2, 1 + gamma,
  2 gamma.
- **Derived by hand while drafting, nothing computed:**
  - (i) The bubble form. With the 2-D propagator G_m(x) = K0(m|x|)/(2 pi) and the 2-D bubble
    B(p) = int d^2x exp(i p.x) G_t(x) G_1(x), integrating over a line of external momentum gives
    int dp_2 B((c, p_2)) = (1/pi) int_0^oo K0(t x) K0(x) cos(c x) dx. So C(t) is pi times the 2-D bubble
    with masses t and 1, integrated over a line of external momentum, at c = i gamma. At gamma = 0
    it is F_2, the n = 2 tree of Paper 59.
  - (ii) At gamma = 2: z = (t^2 - 3)/(2t), z^2 + 3 = (x + 3)^2/(4x), 1 - z^2 = -(x - 1)(x - 9)/(4x), so
    j = 64 (z^2+3)^3/(1-z^2)^2 = 16 (x + 3)^6 / (x (x - 1)^2 (x - 9)^2), x = t^2. The fibre count of the
    CF record gives I1*, I2, I2, I1 at x = 0, 1, 9, oo.
  - (iii) One density line with momentum c: int_0^oo K0(t x) cos(c x) dx = pi / (2 sqrt(t^2 + c^2)). For
    the pair of geminals, c = 2 i gamma, this is pi / (2 sqrt(t^2 - 4 gamma^2)): algebraic, with a
    square-root branch at t = 2 gamma.
  - (iv) The pair of density lines of scale 1 at zero momentum gives F_3's twisted K3 piece at t = 2.
    It does not depend on gamma.
- Cap known in advance: at gamma = 2, |1 - gamma| = 1 is also the equal-scale point t = 1. At
  gamma = 3/2, t = 1 was not singular, so a coincidence there may change the local data.

## Predictions

**CF5 (main: the Legendre piece; this is CF4).** Y's annihilator at gamma = 2 has an order-two
composition factor equivalent, over Q(t), to L_Leg at gamma = 2: the Legendre operator of degree -1/2
in z = (t^2 - 3)/(2t), twisted by t^(1/2). Its curve has j = 16 (x+3)^6 / (x (x-1)^2 (x-9)^2).
- Falsifier: no order-two factor, or one not equivalent to L_Leg at gamma = 2, while the map search
  recovers the gamma = 3/2 map as a control.

**CF6 (the density pair).** Y's annihilator has an order-three factor equivalent to F_3's operator
twisted by t, with genuine singular points t = 0, +-2, oo, as at gamma = 3/2.
- Falsifier: no such factor, or a factor of order three not equivalent to it.

**CF7 (nothing else).** Every other composition factor has order one and an algebraic solution. Their
genuine singular points in t > 0 lie in {2, 4}, with half-integer exponents, and at least one is at
t = 4 = 2 gamma.
- Falsifier: a factor of order two or more besides CF5's and CF6's; an order-one factor with a
  transcendental solution; or an order-one factor genuinely singular at a point of t > 0 outside
  {2, 4}, or with an integer exponent at t = 4.
