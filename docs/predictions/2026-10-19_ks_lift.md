# Pre-registered prediction: the Kustaanheimo-Stiefel lift (hydrogen as a constrained four-dimensional oscillator)

**Registered:** 2026-10-19, before any computation in this file (PI-directed: "Both please", after the
PI asked whether a symmetry between the nuclear skeleton and the electron skeleton could be exploited).
**Extends:** Paper 24 `sec:asymmetry` (Table `tab:asymmetry`, `thm:asymmetry`); atlas doors 1 and 3;
Papers 8-9 (Coulomb Sturmians at a shared p0); Paper 39's remark that the Kustaanheimo-Stiefel map is no
bridge at the spectral-triple level.
**Commit status:** committed on its own, before any computation.

## The door

Lift R^3 to R^4 = C^2 by the Hopf map: with z = (z1, z2), x1 + i x2 = 2 z1 conj(z2),
x3 = |z1|^2 - |z2|^2, r = |z|^2. The door releases one dimension: the U(1) fibre z -> e^(i phi) z, kept
as a constraint. Under the lift the Coulomb problem at fixed energy becomes an isotropic oscillator in
four dimensions, and the roles of energy and charge swap: the energy is consumed as the oscillator
frequency, and the charge comes out as the eigenvalue. The nuclear shell model runs on the
three-dimensional oscillator (Paper 24), so the question is whether the electron's skeleton is an
oscillator skeleton seen through one constraint.

## Model

Oscillator H = p_u^2/8 + 2 omega^2 |u|^2 (mass 4). Bargmann monomials in the circular variables
(A+, A-, B+, B-), A for the plane (u1, u2) and B for (u3, u4); level N = total degree; fibre charge
Q = (a+ - a-) + (b+ - b-). The hydrogen sector is Q = 0. Every generator used is a sum of terms
(variable) x (derivative in a variable), so its matrix on monomials has integer entries (halves for the
z-components). SU(2)+ acts on (A+, B+), SU(2)- on (A-, B-) as the conjugate doublet; the diagonal is
the three-dimensional rotation group.

## Predictions

- **KS1 (the label set):** the Q = 0 sector is empty at odd N; at N = 2(n - 1) it has dimension n^2, and
  under the diagonal SU(2) it holds l = 0, 1, ..., n - 1 once each, each with m = -l..l. Checked exactly
  for n <= 15 by counting, and by the minimal polynomial of L^2 for n <= 8.
- **KS2 (the Casimir):** on each Q = 0 level, J+^2 = J-^2, and the SO(4) Casimir 2(J+^2 + J-^2) equals
  (n^2 - 1) times the identity, as exact integer arithmetic, n <= 10. This is the corpus's diagonal
  Casimir of door 1, obtained here with no Fock projection.
- **KS3 (the projection is linear in this frame):** each Q = 0 oscillator eigenfunction at frequency omega,
  pushed down to R^3, satisfies the Coulomb Sturmian equation (-1/2 Laplacian - Z/r) f = -(p0^2/2) f with
  p0 = 2 omega and Z = omega (N + 2) = n p0. Checked symbolically for every Q = 0 state with n <= 3
  (14 states). So in this frame the eigenvalue is linear in the label with one universal constant p0:
  Paper 24's rows "Projection E(lambda)" and "Projection constant" take the oscillator column's values.
  The nonlinearity E = -Z^2/(2 n^2) of the hydrogen frame is a property of which number is consumed,
  not of the geometry.
- **KS4 (the price):** for any two Q = 0 states, the R^4 inner product equals c times the integral of
  f f' / r over R^3, with one constant c = pi/4 for every pair and every omega: the fibre volume.
  The lift introduces exactly one pi, an M1 volume-slot reading, which cancels in every normalized
  quantity; nothing else transcendental enters. Checked by exact integrals on all pairs with n <= 2 and
  three pairs at n = 3.
- **KS5 (pi in the spectral zeta does not separate the columns):** pi^2 enters at even integer argument
  for all four ladders: the S^3 Casimir ladder, sum over n >= 2 of n^2 (n^2 - 1)^(-s), equals
  pi^2/12 + 1/16 at s = 2; the Kustaanheimo-Stiefel ladder, sum of n^2 n^(-s), is zeta(s - 2); the
  oscillator Euler ladder, sum over N >= 1 of ((N+1)(N+2)/2) N^(-s), is
  (1/2)[zeta(s-2) + 3 zeta(s-1) + 2 zeta(s)]; the oscillator energy ladder with (N + 3/2)^(-s) is a
  combination of Hurwitz zetas at 3/2 that contains pi^2 and pi^4 at s = 4. Checked to 50 digits by
  direct summation against the closed forms at s = 4 and s = 6, and at s = 2 for the Casimir ladder.

## The price, predicted

FORCED: the label set, the degeneracy, the Casimir n^2 - 1 and a linear projection, all on an integer
lattice with no pi. The price is one fibre volume, pi/4, cancelling in every ratio. And the consumed
number changes role: energy in, charge out.

## Bearing on Paper 24 (to be raised with the PI, not acted on here)

If KS3 holds, rows 6 and 7 of `tab:asymmetry` are frame-dependent. If KS5 holds, step 5 of the causal
chain behind `thm:asymmetry` (a nonlinear projection needs spectral-zeta regularization that brings pi; a
linear-affine one does not) is not supported as stated, since the oscillator ladders' spectral zetas carry
pi too. Whether layer (ii), "calibration pi", needs a sharper statement or a new tier is a PI call, and
possibly a /qa question. Layers (iii) to (vii) are not addressed by this record.

## Falsifiers

1. KS1: a wrong count or wrong l content at any n in range.
2. KS2: a Q = 0 level where the Casimir is not (n^2 - 1) times the identity.
3. KS3: a pushed-down state that fails the Sturmian equation, or a charge other than n p0.
4. KS4: a pair with a different constant, or any other transcendental.
5. KS5: a ladder whose spectral zeta at s = 4 or 6 is free of pi.
