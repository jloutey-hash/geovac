## What changes, and why

AX0 (ii) compared the engine's G_L, K_L with a direct-projection quadrature at 22 digits, bar relative 1e-18. It
missed at L = 12 in three of fourteen values (2.0e-18 to 3.9e-18). After the miss, the same quadratures at 32
digits agreed with the engine to 9.1e-35, 6.3e-34 and 3.8e-33: the 22-digit quadrature cannot resolve the m = 1, 2
multipoles at L = 12, where they are small against their integrand.

**AX2-0 (ii), replacing AX0 (ii).** The same comparison at 32 digits, at the same L (DX: 1, 3, 7, 12; DD: 2, 5,
12) with bar relative 1e-25, **and at L = 20 for both** (engine values not computed before this record) with bar
1e-23. AX0 (i) and (iii) are re-run by the same driver as a consistency check and also gate. Driver:
`python debug/aadx_ap0.py ax2` (log `debug/data/aadx2_ap0.log`).

**Bar control (measured before registering; quadrature alone, no engine values;
`debug/aadx2_qctl.py`).** Relative self-difference |q32/q44 - 1| of the
direct-projection quadrature: L = 12: A1 DX 9.1e-35, DD 6.3e-34; A2 DX 3.2e-33, DD 3.8e-33. L = 20: A1 DX
9.1e-25, DD 6.1e-25; A2 DX 5.5e-25, DD 4.2e-25. So 32 digits resolves L = 12 with a margin of 1e7+ below 1e-25,
and L = 20 only to about 1e-24: the L = 20 bar is 1e-23, at least ten times the measured self-difference (a
cold reviewer caught that the first draft's 1e-25 at L = 20 would have voided on the quadrature again).

**Disclosed:** the 32-digit agreement at L = 12 was seen after the miss, so at those three values AX2-0 (ii) is
not a fresh test. Only L = 20 is new. AX2-0 checks the T recurrence and the raising-identity steps against a
direct projection that uses neither; the normalization, the addition-theorem factor and V_A's multipole are
shared with the engine and are tested only by P1 (eight values against an independent referee). No prediction
(P1, P2a, P3) has been run. P3's beta(220) uses F_221, one past AX0 (iii)'s L <= 220; the coefficients are exact
rationals and the engine's tables run to L_max + 3, so this is not a gap.
