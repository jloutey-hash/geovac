## What I know before computing (disclosed)

- Y_exp(t) = <rho_i rho_j rho_k f_ik f_jk / r_ij> with f = e^{-gamma r}, scales (1, 1, t), t on the apex,
  at the fingerprint fibre. At gamma = 3/2 and 2 its minimal annihilator has order nine with pieces of
  orders 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 3: four algebraic pieces, the Legendre piece with z = (t^2 + 1 - gamma^2)/(2t)
  (twisted by a power of t), and F_3's K3 operator twisted by t. Genuine singular points
  t = |1 - gamma|, 2, 1 + gamma, 2 gamma.
- Record QG's route: e^{-gamma r} = -d/dgamma [e^{-gamma r}/r], so Y_exp is the mixed derivative
  d/dgamma_1 d/dgamma_2 of the Yukawa-Yukawa-Coulomb triangle T(gamma_1, gamma_2) at gamma_1 = gamma_2.
  The loop with masses (gamma_1, gamma_2, 0) is elementary (a solid angle in the Gram data;
  `debug/ring_ext_QG_1_loop.py`).
- **Derived by hand while drafting, nothing computed:**
  - (i) r e^{-gamma r} = d^2/dgamma^2 [e^{-gamma r}/r]. So Y_lin, with f = r e^{-gamma r} on both
    edges, is d^2/dgamma_1^2 d^2/dgamma_2^2 T at gamma_1 = gamma_2 = gamma. Y_exp and Y_lin lie in one
    D-module over (t, gamma_1, gamma_2), generated by T.
  - (ii) If L(t; gamma) annihilates F(t; gamma), then L (dF/dgamma) = -(dL/dgamma) F. So dF/dgamma is
    annihilated by an operator whose composition factors are among those of L, each at most twice.
    By induction, the factors of any gamma-derivative of T are among the factors of T's family. The
    factors seen so far in that family are the three types above.
  - (iii) The Gaussian shape. For f = e^{-gamma r^2} the loop is not the massive triangle, so the
    QG route does not apply. At the level of the one function the avatar is
    H_G(t) = int_0^oo K0(tx) K0(x) e^{-x^2} dx. With g = K0(x) e^{-x^2}:
    theta^2 g + x^2 (4 theta + 3) g + 4 x^4 g = 0, so its Mellin transform obeys
    s^2 G(s) - (4s + 5) G(s + 2) + 4 G(s + 4) = 0. The degrees 2, 1, 0 are unbalanced, so the operator
    in t for H_G, sum_k t^{2k} p_k(theta), has deg p_k rising with k: an irregular singular point at
    t = infinity. By contrast cosh(gamma x) K0(x) obeys an order-four equation with balanced degrees,
    and the CF record's L5 is Fuchsian.
  - (iv) The one function in dispersion roots. By Parseval on the line, with K0(a|x|) the transform of
    pi/sqrt(k^2 + a^2): L(a, b; c) = (pi/4) int_{-oo}^{oo} dk / (Delta_a(k) Delta_b(k - c)),
    Delta_a(k) = sqrt(k^2 + a^2). Its curve is the double cover of the k-line branched at the four zeros
    +-ia and c +- ib. At c = i gamma all four lie on one line, and the branch point i(gamma - b) sits on
    the contour exactly when gamma = b. Not a prediction here; to be checked numerically.
  - (v) Convergence. K0(x) cosh(gamma x) decays like e^{(gamma - 1) x} / sqrt x, so C(t) converges for
    t > gamma - 1, the pseudo-threshold, and is a continuation below it. Paper 59 should say so.

## Predictions

**GA1 (main: the cusp-correct geminal brings no new type).** Y_lin's minimal annihilator at gamma = 3/2
has composition factors each equivalent over Q(t) to one of three operators: an order-one operator with
an algebraic solution; the Legendre operator with z = (t^2 + 1 - gamma^2)/(2t), up to a twist by a power
of t; F_3's operator twisted by t. Multiplicities may differ from Y_exp's. Its genuine singular points in
t > 0 are among {1/2, 2, 5/2, 3}.
- Falsifier: a factor of order two or more not equivalent to those two, for example a Legendre operator
  with another z, a second K3, or anything above K3; or a genuine singular point outside that set.
- Confidence: high for the factor types, by (ii); moderate for the singular set.

**GA2 (the Gaussian avatar leaves the Fuchsian class).** The minimal annihilator of H_G in t has an
irregular singular point at t = infinity. So on the Paper 18 and 34 bookkeeping the Gaussian avatar sits
with exponential periods, outside the ladder of Picard-Fuchs operators, while the Slater avatars (exp,
and by (ii) linexp) stay on it.
- Falsifier: a Fuchsian minimal annihilator for H_G.
- Cap: the avatar, not Y with a Gaussian geminal, which is not computed.
