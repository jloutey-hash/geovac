## Object

A chain of three densities on the slice (t; 1, 1), at fingerprint weights:
- **Root:** s-type, scale t.
- **Leaves:** two p_z (L = 1) densities, each the centre-derivative of the s leaf: rho_p = d/dz [K0(r)/(2 pi^2)] =
  f_p cos(theta), with f_p = -K1/(2 pi^2) and dipole moment -1. Both are joined to the root by Coulomb kernels.

**Reduction.** Laplace's multipole expansion replaces the shell theorem.
- The leaf potential is g(r) cos(theta), with g = (2/pi)[K1 - chi/r^2] = -1/r^2 + E, where E = (2/pi)[K1 + R/r^2] and
  R = pi/2 - chi.
- This g equals d/dz of the s-leaf potential (2/pi) chi/r. That was proved by differentiation and checked against
  Laplace's L = 1 formula.
- At the root, <cos^2> = 1/3, so the fibre is t^3 ∫ K0(t r) H(r) dr with H = (2/(3 pi)) r^2 g^2.

The t = -2 branch is measured as record SA did. At t = -(2 - eps) + i0 the branch is pi (2 - eps)^3 S(eps), where
S(eps) = ∫_1^∞ I0((2 - eps) x) H_sing(x) dx and H_sing = (2/(3 pi)) x^2 E^2 (the e^{-2x} piece of H). The pieces of H
with one or no exponential are holomorphic at -2:
- the integral over [0, 1] is singular only at t = 0;
- on [1, ∞) the pure-power piece branches at t = 0, and the e^{-r} cross pieces at t = +-1;
- so only E^2 reaches t = -2.

The I0 integral against the lower pieces diverges literally, so "holomorphic" means the continued value, as in SA.
Expansion fitted: eps^{3/2} S = A + A kappa eps + c eps^{3/2} + O(eps^2). The eps^{3/2} term comes from the x = 1 lower
limit, and there are no log terms because the Gamma arguments are half-integers. The driver fits {1, u^2, u^3, u^4}
in u = sqrt(eps) by least squares (called "Richardson" in its docstring).

## The risk (why this is a test)

For the L = 1 leaf, the leading tail terms of the leaf potential, of order r^{1/2} e^{-r}, cancel exactly. That was
found by the design pass and confirmed by control C3. If the next order had cancelled too, the chain would not reach
the n = 3 branch at -2. C3 derived the term that survives, exactly:

E(r) ~ sqrt(2/pi) r^{-1/2} e^{-r} [1 + 11/(8r) + 97/(128 r^2) - 463/(1024 r^3) + ...]

From this tail alone, before any evaluation near -2, it follows that:

- eps^{3/2} S(eps) -> **A = 1/(3 pi^2) = 0.0337737279**;
- eps^{3/2} S = A (1 + kappa eps) + O(eps^{3/2}), with **kappa = 47/8**.

The exponent is SA's n = 3 exponent, -3/2. The amplitude is exactly 1/3 of the s-chain's (1/pi^2, kappa = 31/8):
cos^2 averages to 1/3, and the tail is the s tail differentiated.

**Honest weight.** Once E is right (C2, C3), the leading amplitude follows from the tail asymptotics, so FB2 is
largely a consistency test of the derivation against a full-integrand evaluation from x = 1. The new content is that
the surviving term is nonzero, which C3 already established. That is the attainment statement. kappa is the less
certain clause. The l > 0 rung *bound* (`prop:tree_rung` for harmonic densities) is not tested here; this record
needs only that the lower pieces of H are holomorphic at -2.

## Decision gate

- **VOID** (overrides everything else): FB0 fails, or a step has not finished 48 h after launch.
- **GO:** FB1 and FB2 hold. FB3 does not gate.
- **STOP:** the last-5 fitted slope is above -1 or below -2. This covers S bounded (slope near 0): the chain does not
  reach the n = 3 branch at -2, or reaches another one.
- **BORDERLINE:** otherwise. This includes a slope in [-2, -1] but outside FB1's band, and FB1 holding with FB2
  missing.

## Caps

- One tree shape (chain of three), L = 1 leaves built as centre-derivatives, one slice.
- The branch at -2 read through S(eps), as in SA.
- The l > 0 rung bound and an operator for the fibre are not addressed. The paper-side extension of
  `prop:tree_reduction` to harmonic densities would be a separate step.
- **Scope of "l > 0" here.** The root is s-type, so only the L = 0 channel of Y10 x Y10 survives at it. The L = 2
  Gaunt piece is never exercised; there is no l > 0 density at an internal node, and no p root. The fibre is also
  d^2/dA1z dA2z of the displaced-centre s chain, so its membership in the class is inherited from the dictionary
  (Paper 63 `sec:dictionary`). The new content is non-vanishing at the n = 3 branch. A GO is attainment for this
  shape, not for l > 0 trees generally.
