## The question

Is the profile-independent constant c of the hyperfine x^3 ln x term, defined as in X3-1, equal to -64/63?

## What I know before computing (disclosed)

- X3-1's data: c = -1.01486 with 8 columns, -1.0129 to -1.0130 with 7, the same across point, ball and
  shell magnetization and n = 1, 2 to 2e-5 within one column set. The move from 7 to 8 columns went toward
  -64/63 = -1.015873.
- The EFT reading behind -64/63 (from `debug/eft_multifocal_check_memo.md`): the log comes from iterating
  lower-order contacts, the charge contact times the Zemach contact plus the charge's Friar contact, giving
  -2 delta_3 = -64/63. That algebra is the memo author's and was not re-derived by the PM.
- The fit window of X3-1 was x = 1e-5 .. 2e-3 at dps 60. At smaller x the x^3 ln x term is a smaller
  fraction of H, so precision has to rise with the window.
- Nothing in this file has been computed.

## Model

Hydrogen-like, Z = 1, atomic units, uniformly charged sphere of radius R = x, as in X3-1: bound states
and hyperfine factors from `debug/zemach_door.py` (interior power series, exterior Whittaker function,
secant on nu), raised to mp.dps = 120 with tolerance 10^-125. Cases: point, ball and shell magnetization,
n = 1 and 2. Window: x = 1e-6 .. 1e-3, 24 log-spaced points. Three column sets for H(x):
- A: x, x^2 ln x, x^2, x^3 ln x, x^3, x^4 ln x, x^4, x^5 ln x, x^5, x^6 ln x;
- B: A without its last column;
- C: A plus x^4 ln^2 x and x^5 ln^2 x.
c = (x^3 ln x coefficient) - (4/5) x (x coefficient), the X3-1 definition.

## Predictions

**X3-1b.** In all six cases and all three column sets, c = -64/63.
- Spread: the largest difference of c among A, B and C, over the six cases, relative to 64/63.
- Pass: spread below 1e-7 and |c + 64/63| < 1e-6 x 64/63 in every case and set.
- Falsifier: spread below 1e-7 and |c + 64/63| > 1e-5 x 64/63 in any case.
- Anything else (spread at or above 1e-7, or a gap between 1e-6 and 1e-5): UNCLEAR, reported as such.
- If the falsifier fires, the identified value is reported, and any integer-relation search on it is
  exploratory and unscored.

## Novelty status

- X3-1b: NOT FOUND, as for X3-1. The x^3 ln x term is not in Karshenboim 1997, Karshenboim 2001,
  Karshenboim-Ivanov 2002, Eides-Grotch-Shelyuto 2001 Sec. XXXIV, Friar-Payne 2005 or Volotka 2006 App. A
  (the X3-1 memo's search; the PM re-checks at scoring). A hit is a **FINDING** of low weight: it follows
  from the EFT rule, which is known.

## Controls

- Positive: the x coefficient reproduces the Zemach coefficients of `2026-10-19_zemach_door.md` to 1e-10.
- Positive: the fitting code, run on synthetic data built from the fitted series of case (ball, n = 1)
  with c shifted by 1e-4 and noise at 1e-100 added, recovers the shifted c to 1e-8.
- Consistency: the six cases agree with each other to 1e-8.

## Caps stated in advance

A fit, not a derivation. Two values of n, three profiles, one charge model (uniform sphere).
