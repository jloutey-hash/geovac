## The question

The true D = 0 fibre of the three-electron chain is
G(a;b,c) = int_0^inf x K1(ax) J(bx) J(cx) dx, with J = 3L - 3XK0 - X^2 K1 and L(X) = int_0^X K0.
Here a is the central density and b, c are the leaves. At equal scales G collapses onto two K3
periods and pi^3. At (1;2,4) it showed no relation with K3 periods, elliptic periods and simple
Tate numbers. Does it stay outside a principled widening of that basis, at more than one generic
triple? Does it collapse where the problem has thresholds? And, as a function on a one-parameter
slice, is it exactly "a K3 period integrated once", the class a null PSLQ is expected to reflect?

  transfers to them is not shown.
- **Derived by hand while drafting, nothing computed:**

  - (ii) The Bailey moduli at (1;2,4) are k+- = (sqrt21 +- sqrt5)/8, conjugate under

- **Literature read today** (memo Sec. 2):
  - Bonisch-Fischbach-Klemm-Nega-Safari, arXiv:2008.10574, full text. The full banana integral is

## Model

- **Fibre.** G as above, and the control Gctrl = int x K1(ax) B(bx) B(cx) dx with
  B(X) = 3XK0 + X^2 K1 (the L-free part).
- **Evaluation.** A parametrised copy of the shared double-exponential grid of
  `debug/three_electron_p3e_fast.py`: x = exp(t - e^{-t}), with step h and h/2 from one pass.
  - Accept a triple only if |h - h/2| < 10^-(D+20) for every integrand.
  - The tail cut is set by the smallest mass: x_max = ((D+10) ln 10 + 50)/min(a,b,c).
  - Precisions: D = 340, 400 and 460.
- **Triples.** Notation (a;b,c), a central.

| ID | Triple | Role |
|:--|:--|:--|
| T1 | (1;2,4) | re-test (geometric progression; noted) |
| T2 | (1;3,7) | generic; triangle inequality fails; residue moduli 1/2, 3/4 |
| T3 | (3;1,7) | generic; same mass set as T2, so the same holomorphic K3 V3, with the central density swapped |
| T4 | (3;4,6) | generic; triangle inequality holds, so the Bailey moduli are complex conjugates; residue moduli 1/7, 1/3 |
| S1 | (2;1,1) | threshold a = b + c, leaves equal; Bailey modulus 1/sqrt2 (CM) |
| S3 | (3;1,2) | threshold a = b + c, distinct masses; Bailey modulus 1/sqrt3 (not CM) |
| C0 | (1;1,1) | positive control, exact relation known |

  Generic means: no mass equals the sum of the other two, no two masses are equal, and the masses
  form no arithmetic progression. T2 to T4 are also not geometric progressions.
- **Basis blocks** (memo Sec. 3, fixed here):
  - K3: V, D1, D2, S12, after removing the internal relations found in the pre-step.
  - B: Galois-adapted Bailey products S_XY and A_XY, for X, Y in {K, E}.
  - R: pi P_ab, pi Q_ab, pi P_ac, pi Q_ac.
  - RR: P_ab P_ac/pi, P_ab Q_ac/pi, Q_ab P_ac/pi, Q_ab Q_ac/pi.
  - T3: pi^3, zeta(3), pi^2 log p for p in Pr.
  - Li: pi Li2(l) and Li3(l) for l in Lambda.
  - O: U, U_a, U_b, U_c (the vacuum sunrise and its mass derivatives).
  - LR: log p x {P_ab, Q_ab, P_ac, Q_ac}.

  Pr and Lambda are as defined in the memo. At T1, Pr = {2,3,5,7} and Lambda = {1/3, 3/5, 5/7, 3/7, -1/7}.
- **Spans:**
  - SA = K3 + B + R + RR + pi^3
  - SB = K3 + T3
  - SC = K3 + T3 + Li
  - SD = K3 + O + T3 + R
  - SE = K3 + R + LR + pi^3
- **Threshold spans:** the same, with K3 and B replaced by the degenerate Bailey block
  {kappa K(k)K(k'), kappa K E', kappa E K', kappa E E'}, after internal reduction.
- **PSLQ.** mpmath.pslq, cap 10^(floor(D/n) - 3), maxsteps 1e6.
  - A relation counts only if it is identical at D = 340 and 400, and reappears at 460.
  - Its residual must be confirmed at 200 digits by the adaptive mp.quad route of
    `debug/three_electron_p3e_check.py`.
- **Pre-step, not scored and run without G.** Basis-internal PSLQ in each span at each triple, to
  fix ranks. It may reduce a span by removing dependent elements. It may not add elements.
- **P3e-3.** The `density_loop_D*` pipeline (Mellin-route operator, exact factorization, no-split
  search with planted splits) applied to G(t;1,1) with the Slater weights, t on the central
  density.

## Predictions

**P3e-2W (generic null, widened).**
- (a) At T1, T2, T3 and T4, G has no relation that meets the counting rule in any of SA to SE.
- (b) Swap: at mass set {1,3,7}, no relation that meets the counting rule among
  {G(1;3,7), G(3;1,7)}, the shared K3 block (V and its first and second derivatives in all three
  masses, after reduction) and T3.
- *Falsifier:* one relation that meets the counting rule, at any generic triple, in a span whose
  controls pass.
- *Novelty status:* PROBABLY KNOWN, by analogy: the BFKNS special relative period, Paper 59's
  slice no-split result and Paper 61's T2 null all point to a null. Searched: the corpus (Paper 59,
  Paper 61, the prediction records, the banana dictionary memo) and the four literature items above;
  nothing addresses this object's value at a generic point.
  - A held null scores as **CHECK**. It remains evidence, not proof.
  - A stable relation falsifies the prediction and scores as **MISS**, but the relation itself
    would be a new result, recorded as such in the disposition.

**P3e-3 (the slice is K3 integrated once).**
- G(t;1,1) is annihilated by an operator whose only non-elementary composition factor is
  equivalent to F_3's operator 4 theta^3 - t^2 (theta+1)^3.
- No rational map splits that piece off. Exact search within the exponent bound, with a planted
  split found by the same code.
- At t = 1 the operator is singular, consistent with the known collapse at C0.
- *Falsifier:* a second non-elementary factor (elliptic F_2 or anything else), or a split found.
- *Novelty status:* PROBABLY KNOWN. Paper 59 `sec:density_ring` proves it for the Delta^{-3}
  weights. For the Slater weights it is near-corollary but not shown. A hit is a **CHECK**; a
  failure is a **MISS** that would re-open the slice result's scope.

**P3e-4 (thresholds collapse).**
- At S1 and S3, G has a relation that meets the counting rule in the threshold spans (degenerate
  Bailey block + R + RR + T3 + Li).
- Mechanism, by analogy: values drop at singular points of the fibre's operator. Examples are
  Paper 59's even Ising moments c_{4,0} and c_{6,0}, the BFKNS Sec. 3.7 values, and C0.
- Confidence: low to moderate. Paper 59's parity reading failed at n = 8, and a threshold collapse
  may need L-values outside the basis.
- *Falsifier:* no relation that meets the counting rule at S1 or S3 with controls passing; the two
  triples are scored separately. A null at S3 does not exclude a collapse into L-values of non-CM
  forms.
- *Novelty status:* NOT FOUND. Same search as P3e-2W; BFKNS Sec. 3.7 lists closed values only at
  equal-mass on-shell points of bananas, not at thresholds of this object. A hit is a **FINDING**.

**P3e-5 (degree homogeneity).**
- At every triple, Gctrl's reduction, and any relation found for G, uses only degree-3 elements.
- *Falsifier:* a control or relation that needs an element of another degree. The degree heuristic
  is then dropped, and SA to SE are rerun with {1, pi, pi^2, log p, pi log p} appended. That rerun
  is pre-registered here as the fallback.
- *Novelty status:* NOT FOUND; it is a bookkeeping hypothesis of this file. A hit is a **CHECK**.

## Controls (a span or triple is void if its control fails)

- **Positive, exact:** at C0, recover 216 G = 80 c30 - 6600 c32 + 81 pi^3 in the new pipeline at
  100 and 150 digits.
- **Positive, structural:** at every triple and in every span, Gctrl must reduce onto the K3 block
  (onto the degenerate block at S1 and S3). This is expected from the parity rule. At T1 it must
  reproduce the denominator 363825.
- **Positive, planted:** in every span, X = sum k_i B_i over 8 randomly chosen elements, with
  integer k_i of height 1e9 from a fixed seed (20261019). It must be recovered exactly at both
  precisions.
- **Negative:** G(1;2,4) searched in T2's SA and SB, the wrong masses. It must return no relation
  that meets the counting rule. Any returned dense vector at the cap must differ between 340 and 400
  digits, which calibrates the stability rule.
- **Grid:** for every integrand, |h - h/2| < 10^-(D+20). At 150 digits, agreement with
  `debug/data/three_electron_p3e_values_320.json` at T1.

## Tests and decision rules

1. Grids and caches at all seven triples. Pre-step internal reductions. Spans frozen.
2. Controls first. Any failure voids that span or triple; it is reported, not repaired after seeing
   G.
3. G PSLQs, then the counting rule, then the adaptive-route residual check on any survivor.
4. P3e-3 is independent of steps 1-3 and is run first if compute is limited.

## Honest caps

- A null PSLQ is not a proof, and the bases are guessed. The most plausible class has no finite
  closed basis (memo Sec. 5).
- These are fibre statements. The integrated chain value may sit lower (Paper 59's caps).
- Four generic triples and two threshold triples are a sample, not a family statement.
- The Galois-adapted products cover only the quadratic twist of the Bailey moduli. Other algebraic
  coefficient fields are not covered.

## Disposition (scored 2026-10-20)

Run by a computation agent against the frozen file; scripts `debug/p3e2w_*.py`, data
`debug/data/p3e2w_*.json` and logs, full record `debug/p3e2w_results_memo.md`. Reviewed by the PM.

**Controls.** C0 recovered (216, -80, 6600, -81) at 100 and 150 digits. Every grid |h - h/2| <= 6.9e-519.
T1 agrees with the old 320-digit cache; T1 reproduces the denominator 363825. Planted relations recovered in
every valid span. The wrong-mass negative control returned no stable relation. Voids, each fixed by the
registered cap rule 10^(floor(D/n) - 3) before any G value was searched: SE at T2, T3, T4 (30 entries give
cap 1e8 at 340 digits, below the 1e9 planted height); SB at S1 (its reduced span has 7 elements, too few
for the 8-element planted relation).

**P3e-3: HELD.** The Mellin operator factors exactly as N2 R3 R1 (theta + 2). R3 is equivalent to F_3's K3
operator 4 theta^3 - t^2 (theta+1)^3 by an explicit rational map, checked exactly modulo L3. R1 (solution
t/(1 - t^2)^4) and theta + 2 (solution t^-2) are elementary. G itself does not need N2:
R3 R1 (theta + 2) G <= 1.6e-31 at 32 digits at five t, against 1e3 to 4e4 for a perturbed operator. No split
was found in a box larger than the exponent bound, and a planted split was found by the same code. The
operator is singular at t = 1. So the slice is a K3 function integrated once for the Slater weights too,
extending Paper 59 `sec:density_ring` from the Delta^-3 weights. [Clarified 2026-10-20 by the PM: the one
integration has an elementary kernel that carries artanh t, from the second solution of R1 (theta + 2); whether that
term drops, as it does for the Delta^-3 chain, was not tested.] Class: **CHECK**.

**P3e-2W (a): HELD where testable (17 of 20 cells; 3 void).** [Count corrected 2026-10-20 by the PM: four generic
triples times five spans is 20 cells, and SE is void at T2, T3, T4; the earlier '19 of 22' was a miscount.] No relation for G met the counting rule at
T1-T4 in any span whose controls passed. Class: **CHECK** (a null is evidence, not proof).

**P3e-2W (b): HELD.** At {1,3,7}, no relation among G(1;3,7), G(3;1,7), the reduced K3 block and T3.
Class: **CHECK**.

**P3e-4: FAILED at S1 and at S3**, scored separately. No relation met the counting rule in any valid
threshold span. As registered, the S3 null does not exclude a collapse into L-values of non-CM forms.
Class: **MISS**, **MISS**. This was the one prediction that could have been a FINDING.

**P3e-5: HELD.** Every structural-control reduction at all seven triples uses degree-3 elements only; no G
relation exists to test; the fallback rerun was not triggered. Class: **CHECK**.

**Deviations** (how, not what; none changes a grade): a hand-written Bessel evaluator, checked against
mpmath, replaced mpmath's besselk (about 25 h per triple to 8-22 s); one 520-digit grid per triple truncated
to 340/400/460, with the h-criterion checked at 460; grids and PSLQ ran alongside P3e-3, with no G value
read before its controls except the registered T1 comparison; four implementation choices (Lambda from the
two residue moduli; the Bailey-moduli formula, reconstructed and checked numerically; a fresh seeded
generator per span; removing basis-internal relations only under the full counting rule) were fixed before
any G search.

**Consequence for the wall.** P3e-2 (generic chain fibre, OPEN-LEANING) gains a widened null at four generic
triples and a swap test, and the slice result now covers the physical Slater weights. The fibre still shows
no relation in any principled basis tried; the expected class is a K3 function integrated once, which no
finite basis of periods is expected to capture.
