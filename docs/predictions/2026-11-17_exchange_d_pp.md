## Object

f(R) = (ab|cd), with a and c on A, b and d on B. The orbitals are nodeless hydrogenic (n = l + 1, rate Z/n, the
`geovac.qfd_core` convention). f is computed as a Neumann sum in prolate coordinates.

**Scored cases.** These are (a, b) pairs, where a is the A orbital's rate. There are seven cases: all four
densities at (1, 1), plus 3ddel, ppsig and pppi0 at (1, 2). 3dsig at (1, 2) is dropped on cost: about 10–11 h,
against 3–4.5 h for the others.

**Not scored.**
- ppdel = (2p+1_A 2p-1_B | 2p-1_A 2p+1_B) equals 3(b/a)² × 3ddel identically: it has the same density polynomial, σ,
  H and exponential. Control C1i confirms this. So 3ddel's operator is also ppdel's, which is the σ = 2 part of real
  p_x p_x exchange.
- 3dpi (σ = 1) is built and validated but not scored.

Reference: `exchange_hp(*eq_gen.ref_orbitals(case, a, b), R)`.

## Instrument

`debug/eq_gen.py` imports the frozen `ep_gen` and `exch_gen` pieces unchanged. It derives the density polynomials and
prefactors from Cartesian solid harmonics, which is a different route from the reference's builder. C0 reproduces
EP's hand-typed fields exactly. eq_gen also adds two things:
- general σ, through the derivatives of Q0, which are cleared because H ≥ σ;
- η moments for any (σ, H), computed by integration by parts.

Truncation uses the proved per-τ valuation bound, 2τ − 4 for all four densities, checked on every term.

`debug/eq_run.py` is EP's `ep_run.py` with five changes:
- the generator is eq_gen;
- output names start with eq_;
- the reference orbitals come from `eq_gen.ref_orbitals`;
- the degree cap DMAX is set explicitly. It stays at EP's 60, inside the unchanged box
  d ≤ d_box(r) = min(DMAX, (E(N−HOLD−r)−MARGIN)//(r+1) − 1);
- the gate checks three frozen operators.

The ladder, the k0 rule (k0 and k0 + 4 reconstruct the same operator; cap 96), heldout (exact Q residual, third prime
16777213) and numeric (Frobenius vs `exchange_hp`) are all EP's.

edge leaves the third-prime fit underdetermined, which would void a valid operator. So the record keeps 60.

## Derivation (before computing)

1. **Constant class.** Unchanged from row 3c and EP. σ = 2 adds Q0′ and Q0″ terms, whose poles are cleared by
   (y² − 1)^H. Prior art; not scored.
2. **Equal exponents: order ≤ 4 over numeric constants, ≤ 5 for the instrument.**
   - At q = 0 the τ-sum stops at τ ≤ k_max + 2H − σ = 4, by parts. The generator truncates there by this bound, so
     C3's τ counts (5, 3, 5, 5) only echo it and cannot fail. The test of the bound is C1: there the reference sums
     τ to 8 and agrees to ≤ 1.7e-62.
   - So f lies in EP's rank-4 C(R)-module, spanned by e^{−2p}, e^{−2p}(γ + ln p), E1(2p) and e^{2p}E1(4p), which
     is closed under d/dR. With ln 2 a number, the order is ≤ 4.
   - The instrument is different. It finds one Q(R)-operator that annihilates every formal component, and ln 2 is
     a separate component. Lifting ln 2 to an indeterminate gives a fifth coordinate, ln 2 × e^{−2p}, which evolves
     on its own. So the instrument's order is ≤ 5. It is 4 when that coordinate is tied to the (γ + ln p)
     coordinate or absent, as in EX, EP and C6 (three of three).
   - Every solution lies in the extension generated by e^{±2p}, ln p, E1(2p) and E1(4p). These are singular only at
     0 and ∞. So at (1, 1) every finite nonzero singular point must be apparent, and the rates lie in {0, ±2a}.
3. **ppdel = 3(b/a)² 3ddel.** Proved from the densities.
4. **The PP family is symmetric in A and B.** Under z → R − z, f_PP(a, b) = f_PP(b, a). C7 checks this, with 3dsig as
   a negative control. The PP rates are therefore symmetric in a and b.
5. **Unequal exponents: no proof.** The heuristic is EP's, which held exactly there: order 9, the 1s rates, and a
   higher degree.
6. **R = 0 (heuristic).** The EX4b onset rule gives Σ(l_i + 1) − 1 + 6 = R^13 ln R for all four densities. Scaling
   fixes the coefficient's total degree at 14. The EP split gives a^9 b^5 for the d cases, a ratio (1,2)/(1,1) of 32,
   and a^7 b^7 for the PP cases, a ratio of 128. These are weaker than in EP, because ppsig's (ξ²η² − 1) factor or
   pppi0's H = 1 could cancel the leading term.
7. **Degree.** No bound is known. Each new apparent point adds roughly the order to the degree.

## Decision gate

- **VOID** (overrides everything else). Any one of:
  - EQ0 fails;
  - the scored primes disagree in shape;
  - an EQ1 instrument part (reconstruction stability, heldout residual, third prime when determined, numeric) fails
    after an operator is accepted on both scored primes;
  - reconstruction does not stabilise within cap 96;
  - a scored-prime run JSON carries an `error`;
  - the order at (1, 1) is 6 or more, or any finite nonzero point at (1, 1) is `genuine` (both contradict
    Derivation 2);
  - a step is unfinished 48 h after launch.
- **STOP.** Either of:
  - for some case, both scored-prime run JSONs exist with `first_hit: null` (the box r ≤ 14, d ≤ d_box(r),
    DMAX 60 exhausted; read as "not in the box", not as a statement about the class);
  - at (1, 2), a finite nonzero point is `genuine` on both scored primes (a climb).
- **GO:** EQ1–EQ5 hold in all seven cases. The reported clauses may miss.
- **BORDERLINE:** otherwise. This includes:
  - order 5 at (1, 1): a finding, meaning the ln 2 coordinate decouples;
  - an order below 4 at (1, 1);
  - an underdetermined third-prime check.

**Scoring order.** The gate first, then pppi0, 3ddel, ppsig and 3dsig at (1, 1), then pppi0, 3ddel and ppsig at
(1, 2). Each case runs `ladder`, `heldout` and `numeric` with suffix EQS. Steps are launched as tracked background
tasks, not `nohup`.

## Caps

- Nodeless orbitals only. l ≤ 2 on A with 1s on B, or l = 1 on both centres; σ ≤ 2.
- Two pairs, both with a = 1, so the power of a in the onset coefficient is not tested.
- PP at (2, 1) equals (1, 2) by symmetry. ppdel and 3dpi are not scored; 3dsig is scored only at equal exponents.
- Singular points are read mod p. The gcrd is not certified minimal, and the constants are formal.
