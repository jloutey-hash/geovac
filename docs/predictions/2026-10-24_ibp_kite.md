## The object

The kite phi(z) of the correlated triangle, z = w^2, as a two-loop momentum-space integral in D = 3 with
external momentum w entering at the apex k and leaving at the centre O. Routing: k1 on O-i, w - k1 on O-j,
q on the rung i-j. Five lines:

| line | momentum | factor | mass | index |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| density i | k1 | (k1^2 + a^2)^(-3/2) | a | 3/2 |
| density j | w - k1 | ((w-k1)^2 + b^2)^(-3/2) | b | 3/2 |
| geminal i-k | k1 - q | ((k1-q)^2 + g1^2)^(-2) | g1 | 2 |
| geminal j-k | w - k1 + q | ((w-k1+q)^2 + g2^2)^(-2) | g2 | 2 |
| Coulomb i-j | q | q^(-2) | 0 | 1 |

On the symmetric slice a = b = 1 and g1 = g2 = gamma. The densities are the fingerprint-fibre densities,
transform (p^2/a^2 + 1)^(-3/2); the geminal e^(-gamma r) has transform proportional to (q^2 + gamma^2)^(-2);
the Coulomb line 1/r has transform proportional to q^(-2). The five quadratic forms span the five scalar
products of (k1, q, w), so every numerator reduces to index shifts. The density indices stay in 3/2 + Z
and never reach zero, so the sectors are the subsets of the three integer lines; the sector with all three
removed is scaleless.

Y(t) attaches the apex density: Y is proportional to t^3 int z^(1/2) (z + t^2)^(-3/2) phi(z) dz, a
three-loop vacuum integral on the tetrahedron O, i, j, k with the sixth line (apex density, mass t,
index 3/2) on O-k.

## Model

Laporta reduction of the kite family in D = 3: IBP identities from d/dk1 and d/dq contracted with k1, q
and w, seeded on index shifts around the base point, solved by exact Gaussian elimination. Arithmetic over
Q(z) with the masses fixed rational, or by evaluation at sample points modulo primes with rational
reconstruction; every reconstructed object is checked at sample points not used to build it. The
differential equation in z from w . d/dw acting on the integrand, reduced to the masters. The operator for
phi from its cyclic vector in that system. Comparison with a stored operator by exact right division over
Q(z), and on the stored Taylor series by the coefficient recurrence.

## Predictions

**IB1 (main: the route sees the kite).** At gamma = 3/2 the reduction closes on a finite set of master
integrals, and the operator it gives for phi has order six and equals the stored order-six operator up to a
left factor in Q(z). It annihilates the stored Taylor series to the series' precision.
- Falsifier: a different operator, or one that fails on the stored series while the controls pass.
- A reduction that does not close within the caps is a STOP for feasibility, reported and not scored.

**IB2 (one run for every gamma).** With gamma kept as a symbol, the operator for phi has order six for
generic gamma and specializes to the stored operators at gamma = 1/2, 2 and 3 up to left factors. At
gamma = 1 the stored order-two operator right-divides its specialization.
- Falsifier: a generic order other than six, or a failed specialization at any stored value.
- If a symbolic gamma is out of reach, IB2 is scored value by value at the stored gammas and this is said.

**IB3 (Y on the tetrahedron).** The three-loop vacuum family with the apex density of mass t reduces, and
the operator it gives for Y at gamma = 3/2 is right-divisible by the stored L9; it annihilates the stored
Y_log series.
- Falsifier: L9 does not right-divide it, or it fails on the series while IB1 passed.
- A STOP for feasibility is allowed here and does not affect IB1, IB2 or IB4.

**IB4 (the route reaches where ours stopped).** The same pipeline at (a, b, g1, g2) = (1, 2, 3/2, 3) gives
an operator for the PF kite, and it annihilates the stored 300-coefficient series to below 1e-200 of the
largest term in every coefficient recurrence.
- Falsifier: no operator within the caps while IB1 passed, or an operator that fails on the series.
- A pass makes PF1-PF4 scorable. They are scored under their own record, not here.

## Novelty and prior art

- The method is known in this community. Momentum-space IBP identities for Hylleraas-type Slater integrals
  were used for Li and Be+ (arXiv:0909.5542, "Ground state of Li and Be+ using explicitly correlated
  functions"); Pachucki, Puchalski and Remiddi gave recursion relations for the Hylleraas three-electron
  integral (Phys. Rev. A 70, 032502, 2004); Lesiuk and Moszynski derived a fourth-order differential
  equation for two-centre integrals over Slater geminals (arXiv:1209.0839).
- The massive kite is studied in D = 4 - 2 epsilon (Remiddi and Tancredi; Adams, Bogner, Schweitzer and
  Weinzierl 2016) and with D = 2 - 2 epsilon sunset subgraphs (Bezuglov, Onishchenko and Veretin 2021,
  arXiv:2011.13337). One search found no D = 3 kite with half-integer density lines.
- So a pass is a CHECK on the method and the first derivation, not a guess, of these operators. IB4 is
  the item with practical weight. The searches are repeated at scoring.

## Controls

- Positive: the D = 3 one-loop bubble with masses (1, gamma) through the same reducer; its differential
  equation is solved by the arctan closed form above, checked at three p to 1e-30.
- Positive: the sector with the Coulomb line removed factorizes into two one-loop bubbles, indices
  (3/2, 3/2) and (2, 2); the reducer's value for it matches the product of independent evaluations at
  three z.
- Identification: the derived operator annihilates the stored phi series. If it does not, the integral
  family is misidentified, and nothing is scored until that is resolved.
- Negative: the Yukawa geminal, index 1 in place of 2, gives an operator that does not annihilate the
  exponential series; gamma changed by 1/1000 breaks the annihilation.
- Every reconstructed rational function is checked at sample points not used to build it.

## Caps stated in advance

One topology, D fixed at 3, the masses listed. The reducer is new code, written for this test, so the
stored operators and series are the independent check on it; minimality is inherited from the stored
bounded searches, not re-proved. No single computation runs longer than two hours, and no long background
job is started without a hand-back first. If the reduction does not close within those limits, the result
is a STOP for feasibility, and the recommendation is an installed reducer (Kira or FIRE under a Linux
layer, a PI decision).

## Disposition (appended 2026-10-24; predictions above unchanged)

**IB1 HELD (CHECK on the method). IB2 HELD, value by value. IB3 NOT RUN. IB4 HELD.**

- **IB1.** At gamma = 3/2 the reduction closes on nine master integrals, all in sub-sectors (none with all
  three integer lines present). The operator for phi from the cyclic vector has order six, d/dz-form
  degrees 21 to 27, and equals the stored order-six operator exactly after making both monic, modulo
  P = 2^61 - 1; the stored operator right-divides it with zero remainder. Every rebuilt rational function of
  z passed 10 held-out sample points. Since the stored operator annihilates the stored series (2.6e-345),
  this also settles the identification of the integral family.
- **IB2, scored value by value** (a symbolic gamma was not attempted, as the record allows). gamma = 2, 3
  and 1/2: nine masters, order six, equal to the stored operators exactly after making monic. gamma = 1:
  the reduction needs only three masters, and gives an order-two operator equal to the stored Legendre
  operator exactly. So the equal-mass degeneration of record MG shows up in the reduction itself, as the
  master count dropping from nine to three. Five values of five.
- **IB3.** The three-loop tetrahedron was not attempted in this round. It stays open; no STOP is claimed.
- **IB4.** At (a, b, g1, g2) = (1, 2, 3/2, 3) the reduction needs 18 master integrals, one of them in the
  top sector, and gives an operator of order 11 with polynomial coefficients of z-degree 102 to 113: about
  1300 unknowns, against the 260 the series route reached. Lifted to Q through the differential system
  rather than the operator: the system's entries have height at most 37 bits and were rebuilt from two
  primes, checked on a third; the operator was then built exactly over Q(z). Its integer coefficients reach
  565 bits; it agrees with the operator computed independently at 13 of 13 primes. On the stored
  300-coefficient series it leaves 1.3e-280 of the largest term (threshold 1e-200); with one coefficient
  changed by 1/200 it leaves 4.2e-41. PF1-PF4 are now scorable, under their own record.
- **Controls.** The D = 3 bubble: the reducer's equation is solved by the arctan closed form to 4e-41 at
  three z. The sector with the Coulomb line removed: the reducer gives three masters and an order-two
  operator that annihilates the exact rational Taylor series of the product of the two bubbles in all 117
  recurrence checks modulo P; the operator built at gamma + 1/1000 fails all 117 on the gamma series, and
  the gamma operator fails all 117 on the gamma + 1/1000 series. The series check was self-tested on the
  stored gamma = 3/2 operator (6.6e-202) against a 1/200 change (1.0e-3).
- **Deviations.** (1) The first version of the seed box counted an absent integer line as a numerator, so
  whole sub-sectors were never seeded; the master set then grew without bound under d/dz and the run was
  stopped for memory. Found by a trace and fixed before any operator was produced; no scored number comes
  from the faulty version. (2) The Yukawa negative control was not run; the gamma + 1/1000 and 1/200
  controls above stand in for it. (3) IB4 was lifted through the system, not the operator. (4) The kite-level
  singular points of the PF operator were looked at modulo one prime before IB4 passed (all eight crossed
  points genuine, -25/4 and -25 ordinary points); this is reported, and PF is scored under its own record
  with the exact operator.
- **Novelty.** Repeated at scoring: one further search found no D = 3 kite with half-integer density lines
  and no IBP treatment of this correlated-triangle family. The method is known (records cited above), so
  IB1 and IB2 are CHECKs; IB4 is the item with practical weight: the route reached an operator that the
  series route could not.
- **Cost.** Each slice operator took 9 to 13 minutes on six cores. The PF operator took about 11 minutes per
  prime and three primes, plus an exact cyclic-vector step of a few minutes.
