# Pre-registered: the two-centre exchange integral with a 2p orbital obeys an operator in R of the 1s shape (record EP)

**Registered:** 2026-11-17, before any scored computation. Owed item: "exchange beyond 1s (l > 0 orbitals)",
inventory rows 3c and 8.
**Owner:** Paper 58 (the exchange paragraph and caps; row 3c of `docs/chemistry_period_inventory.md`). Records EX and
EXb (`docs/predictions/2026-11-16_exchange_unequal_zeta.md`, `..._b.md`) found, for 1s, an order-9, degree-16
operator at unequal exponents (rates -(a+b), 2a, 2b, 0, -2a, -2b) and order 4 at equal exponents.
Cold review before commit: 4 MUST-FIX (the ladder stopped before the second scored prime, so STOP could not be read;
an order above the proved bound 4 mapped to BORDERLINE instead of VOID; frozen table missing `intclass_lib` and

4 NIT; all applied, driver re-frozen. The reviewer re-checked the order <= 4 proof (sound; "exactly 4" can fail low).

## Object

The exchange integral (ab|cd), a and c on A, b and d on B, hydrogenic orbitals, as a function of R, in two families:

- **S** = (2p-sigma_A 1s_B | 2p-sigma_A 1s_B): sigma = 0, density polynomial (xi^2 - eta^2)(1 + xi eta), prefactor
  a^5 b^3 R^7 / 32;
- **P** = (2p_{+1,A} 1s_B | 2p_{-1,A} 1s_B): sigma = 1, H = 1, prefactor a^5 b^3 R^7 / 64. This is the radial
  function of real 2p_x exchange.

a is the exponent of the A orbital, b of the B orbital. Scored pairs (a, b) = (1, 1) and (1, 2).

**Instrument.** `debug/ep_gen.py` is the frozen EX generator (`exch_gen`) with one function, `g_pieces`, copied and
generalised from P_tau(x) Q_tau(y) to general polynomials (the frozen one asserts top <= 2 tau + 6). Everything else is
imported from `exch_gen` unchanged. Truncation uses a proved valuation bound v(tau) = 2 tau - 2, checked on every term.
The guesser, CRT, exact residual and Frobenius are the frozen `exch_run.py` functions, used by import
(`debug/ep_run.py` rebinds only the generator, output names and reference orbitals).

## Derived before computing

- **Constant class:** the row-3c class by construction ({1, gamma, ln primes} x (ln R)^{<=1}, weight one). Prior art,
  not a claim.
- **Equal exponents, order <= 4 for any l (proved in `debug/ep_design_memo.md`):** the tau sum terminates at
  tau <= 3, and f lies in the rank-4 C(R)-module spanned by e^{-2p}, e^{-2p}(gamma + ln p), E1(2p), e^{2p} E1(4p),
  which is closed under d/dR. Prediction: exactly 4.
- **Unequal exponents:** no proof; EX's order 9 is empirical. The exponential skeleton is that of 1s, so the
  expectation is the 1s order and rate multiset with a higher degree.
- **Log onset at R = 0 (heuristic):** EX4b's coalescence rule gives R^11 ln R, with a coefficient of total degree 12,
  suggested a^7 b^5.

## Predictions

The scored run, in this order, suffix EPS: for case in (2psig 1 1), (2ppi 1 1), (2psig 1 2), (2ppi 1 2):

    python debug/ep_run.py ladder <case> <a> <b> --suffix EPS
    python debug/ep_run.py heldout <case> <a> <b> --suffix EPS
    python debug/ep_run.py numeric <case> <a> <b> --suffix EPS

preceded by the gate `python debug/ep_run.py gate 1s --suffix EPS` (1s at (1, 2), both scored primes).

- **EP0 (instrument gate).** On both scored primes the 1s (1,2) run's order, degree, coefficient degrees and
  coefficients mod p equal those in `debug/data/exch_run_1_2_<p>_EXS.json` (`ep_gate_EPS.json`); the frozen files
  hash-match; the valuation check passes in every output. Failure VOID. This is a determinism and environment gate:
  it runs the new `g_pieces_gen` and `generate`, but not the sigma = 1 branches or the extra density terms. A broken
  2p path is caught by control C1 and by EP1's numeric bar (VOID on failure).
- **EP1 (existence, all four cases).** The ladder accepts an operator on both scored primes with equal shape;
  k0 is the smallest accepted k at which reconstruction gives the same operator at k and k + 4, with k0 <= 92; the
  exact residual over Q is zero (this checks the lift to Q; independence comes from the third prime), the third-prime
  fit has nullity 1 and matches; `numeric_pass`: |Frobenius - exchange_hp(dps 80)| < 1e-30 at R = 1.4 and 3.015,
  dps 50 vs 80 < 1e-35, the reference's last tau terms < 1e-40 with T < 400, `root_equations_consistent` and
  `frobenius_equals_generator_to_N` true. Probability 0.8.
- **EP2a (equal exponents).** Order exactly 4 for S and P at (1, 1). Probability 0.8.
- **EP2b (unequal exponents).** Order in [9, 13] for S and P at (1, 2) (14 is the box cap) (probability 0.85);
  exactly 9 is reported (0.55).
- **EP3 (no climb at finite R).** Every finite nonzero singular point's verdict is `apparent` (degree-1 factors, log
  test run) or `exponents_only: apparent-type` (degree > 1, log test not run, distinct nonnegative integer exponents),
  on both primes, in all four cases. Probability 0.75.
- **EP4 (R = 0).** `fuchsian` true, `log_free_local_solutions < order`, and `f_has_lnR` true, in all four cases. If
  an R = 0 exponent is not an integer the log count is undefined and EP4 is read as missed. Probability 0.8.
- **EP5 (rates).** Rank <= 1 at infinity; every rate, rationally reconstructed and equal on both primes, lies in
  {+-(a + b), +-2a, +-2b, 0, +-(a - b)}; at equal exponents the rate set is {0, 2, -2}. Probability 0.6.

**Reported, not gating (probabilities stated in advance):** degree above the 1s degree at the same pair (> 8, > 16;
0.75); exactly 9 at (1,2) (0.55); the 1s rate multiset {-3, 4, 2, 0^2, -2^2, -4^2} at (1,2) (0.5); no a - b rate
(+-1 at (1,2); 0.6); onset R^11 ln R (0.45); coefficient ratio (1,2)/(1,1) = 32 in each family (0.4). Treated as
independent, the gating clauses give a joint GO probability of roughly 0.2-0.3.

## Decision gate

- **VOID** (overrides everything): EP0 fails; the scored primes disagree in shape; an EP1 instrument part
  (reconstruction stability, heldout residual, third prime, numeric) fails after an operator is accepted;
  reconstruction never stabilises within the cap of 96 candidates; order above 4 at (1, 1) in either family
  (it contradicts the proved bound, so the instrument failed); a step unfinished 48 h after its launch.
- **STOP:** for some case no operator is accepted in the box (r <= 14, d <= 60) on both scored primes (the reading
  is "not in the box", not "not holonomic"), or some finite nonzero point has a verdict starting `genuine` on both
  scored primes (a climb).
- **GO:** EP1, EP2a, EP2b (order in [9, 14]), EP3, EP4 and EP5 hold in both families.
- **BORDERLINE:** otherwise (including order below 4 at (1, 1), a real outcome).

## Caps

- l = 1 on A only, 1s on B; sigma <= 1 (d orbitals need D^sigma at sigma = 2, a small extension of the generator).
- Two exponent pairs; mod-p singular readings; the gcrd is not certified minimal; the constants are treated
  formally (as EX).
- The equal-exponent order bound is proved; the unequal-exponent order is measured only.
