# Pre-registration: a frozen core as an effective theory with a counting rule

**Registered:** 2026-10-20, before any computation that scores it.
**Prompted by:** the PI's question whether molecules admit an effective-theory reading, and the scan
`debug/molecular_eft_lit_scan_memo.md` (part C: no chemistry work found that writes a core as a
contact series with a counting rule, matches it on the atom alone, and tests the predicted fall of the
molecular error as terms are added).
**Extends:** the Phillips-Kleinman (PK) barrier of Paper 17 `sec:pk`, the documented bottleneck of the
composed builder; the multi-focal composition wall (Paper 57: the label skeleton has no native
matching step).
**Commit status:** committed on its own, before computing.

## The question

A frozen 1s^2 core acts on a valence electron through a short-range, non-local operator. Lepage's
effective-theory program (nucl-th/9706029) replaces such short-range structure by local terms
organized by a counting rule, with coefficients fixed at low energy. Does a Li+ core, written that way
and matched on the Li atom alone, describe a valence electron in a molecule with errors that fall as
the counting rule says, and where does it break down?

## Relation to the recorded dead ends (CLAUDE.md S3, six PK modifications)

Those attempts reshaped the PK barrier inside the composed LiH builder to fix R_eq. This record is a
diagnostic, not a seventh reshape: one valence electron, an exact frozen-core reference, and a
registered convergence law. The composed builder is not touched. Its PK form enters only as a
comparison (CE4).

## What I know before computing (disclosed)

- Paper 17 `sec:pk`: V_PK = A e^(-B r^2)/r^2, channel-blind, A = 6.93 Ha bohr^2, B = 7.00 bohr^-2 for Li;
  an l-dependent mode applies it to l = 0 only.
- Li+ Hartree-Fock total energy at the basis-set limit: -7.2364152 Ha (standard value; used as a
  solver control).
- Threshold law (textbook): a short-range potential shifts partial wave l as (k r_c)^(2l+1), so its
  effect on channel l falls steeply with l; an energy expansion of the short-range part (effective
  range) is the counting rule in energy.
- Hand expectation, not computed: the exchange and orthogonality effects of a 1s^2 core are largest in
  s, smaller in p, small in d.

## Model

- Atomic units. Li+ core: restricted HF 1s^2 orbital phi_c, solved numerically on a radial grid.
- Reference valence operator (exact frozen core): F = T - 3/r + 2J - K, with J, K built from phi_c,
  restricted to the space orthogonal to phi_c (Q = 1 - |phi_c><phi_c|). Atom: the levels ns, np, nd.
  Molecule LiH+: F + (-1/|r - R z|), lowest two Sigma states (X, A), at R = 2, 3, 4, 6, 10 bohr.
- Effective operator at order (K, L), with fixed range a = 0.6 bohr:
  V_eff = -1/r + sum_{l < L} P_l sum_{k=0}^{K} c_{lk} (r/a)^(2k) e^(-(r/a)^2),
  P_l the projector on angular momentum l about the Li nucleus; channels l >= L see -1/r only. For each
  l < L the K+1 coefficients are fixed so that the K+1 lowest levels of V_eff in channel l equal the
  K+1 lowest reference levels of that l (2s..; 2p..; 3d..). No molecular data enters.
- Error: Delta(K, L, R) = E_eff - E_ref for the X state (and A), same numerics on both sides.
- Comparison CE4: the PK form A e^(-7.00 r^2)/r^2 on l = 0 only, added to -1/r plus the exact local
  screening 2J - 2/r, with A refit so the 2s reference level is exact.
- Numerics: single-centre expansion about the Li nucleus, channels l = 0..l_max coupled by the
  multipoles of the proton (Gaunt coefficients), logarithmic radial grid, extrapolation in l_max and
  grid. Same code as `docs/predictions/2026-10-20_molecular_fns.md`.

## Controls (scored first; a failed control voids the predictions)

- **C1 (KNOWN).** Li+ HF total energy -7.2364152 to 1e-6.
- **C2 (KNOWN).** The single-centre H2+ energy at R = 2 matches -1.1026342144949 to 1e-7 (shared).
- **C3 (construction).** V_eff reproduces the matched atomic levels to 1e-10.
- **C4 (negative).** With all c = 0 (pure -1/r) the 2s level is off by more than 0.05 Ha (hydrogenic
  n = 2 is -0.125 against about -0.196).

## Predictions

- **CE1 (order by order).** At L = 3 and R = 4, 6, 10, |Delta(K)| for the X state falls at every step
  K = 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3, by a factor of at least 3 per step. *Falsifier:* any step with a ratio below 3.
- **CE2 (channel by channel).** At K = 2 and R = 6, |Delta(L)| falls by a factor of at least 10 for each
  added channel, L = 1 -> 2 -> 3. *Falsifier:* a ratio below 10.
- **CE3 (breakdown).** At K = 2, L = 3, |Delta| at R = 2 is at least 10 times |Delta| at R = 6.
  *Falsifier:* a ratio below 10.
- **CE4 (the PK form does not improve with order).** At R = 6 the refit PK form of Paper 17 has an X-state
  error at least 10 times larger than the effective operator at K = 1, L = 2. *Falsifier:* a ratio
  below 10.

## Novelty status

- C1, C2, C4: **KNOWN**. C3: construction.
- CE1-CE3: **NOT FOUND.** Searched: Lepage's lectures (the template, atoms only); core potentials fitted
  to all-electron data (Mitas group, arXiv:1805.00607; no counting rule); Cai, Wang, Chen,
  arXiv:2604.25199 (2026; an EFT of the frozen core for solids, band narrowing, no molecular
  convergence test); quantum-defect model potentials (fitted to Rydberg series, no convergence law
  in a molecule); the scan memo of 2026-10-20. Repeated at scoring time.
- CE4: **PROBABLY KNOWN** in substance: semilocal (l-dependent) pseudopotentials are standard because
  local ones transfer poorly. The comparison with the corpus's own PK form is the internal content.

## Caps, stated in advance

- One valence electron, frozen core, no core polarization, no correlation; LiH+, not LiH.
- One regulator shape (Gaussian) and one range a; a scan in a is not registered.
- The EFT reading is tested only through the registered convergence law; a held CE1-CE3 says the
  counting works here, not that it fixes the composed builder.

## Disposition (appended 2026-10-20, after computing; the predictions above are unchanged)

Drivers: `debug/molfns_solver.py` (shared single-centre solver), `debug/core_eft_A.py` (core, atomic
levels, fits), `debug/core_eft_B.py` (LiH+). Data: `debug/data/core_eft_A.json`,
`debug/data/core_eft_B_l24.json`, `debug/data/core_eft_B_l36.json`.

**Controls.**
- C1 PASSED on grid refinement: Li+ HF -7.2364147 at h = 0.0025 (5.3e-7 off), Richardson -7.23641518
  (2e-8). The error falls as h^2 (the Hartree quadrature). Production used h = 0.01 (8e-6 off); the
  same core enters the reference and every effective operator, so it cancels in the errors scored.
- **C2 FAILED** (the shared H2+ solver control, scored in `2026-10-20_molecular_fns.md`: about 7e-7
  against 1e-7). **By the rule above, CE1-CE4 are VOID.** The verdicts below are recorded as what
  the registered tests would have given, not as scores. The quantities do not depend on the H2+
  energy: they are differences between two operators on the same grid and channel set, and move by
  at most 8% between l_max = 24 and 36 (R = 4, 6) without changing any verdict. Strict reading of
  the control rule, chosen by the PM; the PI may overrule it.
- C3 PASSED where constructible: matched levels reproduced to 1e-13. K = 3 is not constructible for
  s and d: with four Rydberg levels the Jacobian is singular to double precision, since the third
  term moves bound levels by less than working precision. So CE1's step 2 -> 3 is untestable.
- C4 PASSED: hydrogenic n = 2 minus the reference 2s = 0.0713 Ha.

**Method choices made while computing, disclosed.** (i) The fits have several roots at K >= 1; the
branch rule is continuation from the K - 1 solution with the new coefficient starting at 0, targets
moved linearly in 40 steps. (ii) No fitted operator has a spurious deep state (checked from -200 Ha).
(iii) Unmatched atomic levels improve order by order: s next-level error 2.9e-4, 5.5e-6, 7.7e-9 for
K = 0, 1, 2; p 3.1e-5, 1.9e-7, < 1e-8.

- **CE1: would have FAILED.** At R = 4, 6 and 10 the first K-step improves by 1.75, 2.18 and 2.63, below 3.
  Class: would have been **MISS**.
- **CE2: would have been PARTLY.** At R = 6 adding the p channel improves by 15.9 (holds); adding the d channel makes
  the error worse (0.64), at every R. Class: would have been **MISS** for the failed clause.
- **CE3: would have HELD.** |Delta| at R = 2 over R = 6 at K = 2, L = 3: 5.56e-3 / 2.12e-6 = 2600. Novelty re-checked
  at scoring: that core potentials matched on atoms err more at short bond lengths is reported for
  correlation-consistent ECPs (the scan's reading of arXiv:1805.00607, not re-fetched here), so the
  qualitative statement is known. Class: would have been **CHECK**.
- **CE4: would have FAILED, reversed.** The refit PK form errs less than the effective operator at R >= 3 (0.86,
  0.55, 0.16, 0.12 of it). Class: would have been **MISS**.

**Unregistered observations (not scored).** The improvement per K-step grows with R, and so does the
p-channel step: the K-series has an R-dependent floor. Reading, not tested: the proton's field reaches
inside the core, where a smooth contact series has the wrong interior wave function; matching on
bound atomic levels fixes only exterior phases, so the molecule needs operators that couple to the
external field (the role norm conservation plays for pseudopotentials, Hamann, Schlueter and Chiang
1979). The PK model keeps the true interior screening and only replaces orthogonality, which is
consistent with its better transfer. Its refit height is A = 3760 Ha bohr^2 against Paper 17's 6.93;
Paper 17's model screens through Z_eff, not the exact J used here, so this is not a statement about
Paper 17's parameters. The d-channel contacts fit a defect of about 4e-8 with coefficients of order
10 to 40 and worsen every molecular error: overfitting at this scale.

**PI decision (2026-10-21): the void stands.** The PI took the PM's recommendation: a failed control
voids the predictions as registered, and the rule is not relaxed after the outcome is seen, in either
direction. CE1-CE4 stay VOID; the would-have verdicts above are the record of the negative, which is
also filed in `docs/failed_approaches_ledger.md`.
