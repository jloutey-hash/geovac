Gauge Structure, QED, and Gravity from the Spectral Geometry of S^3: A Synthesis of the GeoVac QED/Gauge Arc

Josh Loutey · GeoVac synthesis document · Synthesis / Reading Guides · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286867 · PDF

Abstract

The Geometric Vacuum (GeoVac) framework labels the hydrogen bound states by the harmonics of the unit three-sphere $S^3$ via Fock's conformal projection. This synthesis assembles the five Group 5 papers—gauge structure, the vector-photon vertex and the periods of Dirac spectral sums on $S^3$, the fine-structure-constant observation, and a spectral-action gravity sketch—into one narrative around a single organizing distinction: \emph{$U(1)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson structures can be built on the graph, the choice of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ being natural because $S^3 = \mathrm{SU}(2)$, while the couplings are calibration/observation, not derived}. On the scalar label graph the plaquettes (the 4-cycles) form a cycle basis, so $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson theory and its maximal-torus restriction, compact $U(1)$, are the exactly solvable two-dimensional theories, whose area law holds by factorization and carries no confinement information; on the cross-$\ell$ Dirac Rule-B graph compact $U(1)$ does not factorize and is compatible with compact electrodynamics under seven standard diagnostics, none of which discriminates a dimension [ref]; the natural gauge reach is $U(1)×\mathrm{SU}(2)×\mathrm{SU}(3)$ (an organizing reading, not a derivation), with $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ entering only as a pure-gauge theory whose natural matter coupling is obstructed. Of eight QED selection rules the scalar graph keeps one (triangle sparsity); the physical vector-photon vertex, computed exactly from $\mathrm{Spin}(4)$ representation theory, keeps triangle sparsity and $\Delta m$, satisfies the Ward–Takahashi identity, couples the ground state, and does not preserve the $1{+}6{+}1$ partition of the vertex model [ref]; the continuum $S^3$ Dirac spectral sums carry odd zeta values ($ζ(3)$, $ζ(5)$), the level-4 values Catalan $G$ and $\beta(4)$ when the spectrum is restricted to one parity class, and, in the vertex model's three-loop chain sum, the depth-2 period $ζ(5,3)$, through a depth-filtered period tower [ref]. \gvc{k-combination-rule}{The fine-structure constant approximately satisfies (to $8.8×10^{-8}$ relative; an Observation) the cubic $α^3-Kα+1=0$ with $K=π(B+F-\Delta)$ assembled from three spectral data of the unit $S^3$, each with an independently established spectral home; this is recorded strictly as an Observation (agreement $8.8×10^{-8}$, twelve single-mechanism derivations eliminated), never as a derivation [ref]}; in a look-elsewhere search of the same kind the expected chance count at this precision is about ten. The gravity paper gives a convergence theorem for a discrete replica derivative on the spinor cone (one standard ingredient argued rather than proved), and records the two-term spectral action of the round sphere (Chamseddine–Connes; an alternative proof there) and a Newton constant obtained by two routes, whose agreement (at leading order in $\Lambda$ on the black-hole cigar) is the induced-gravity statement of Susskind–Uglum and Frolov–Fursaev [ref]. The discipline throughout matches the rest of the corpus: every load-bearing claim wears its tier, the structural results are foregrounded, and the couplings are positioned as the free, calibration side of the construction.
