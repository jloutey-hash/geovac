Periods of Dirac Spectral Sums on S^3

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 28 · QED and Gauge Theory · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286799 · PDF

Abstract

We determine the periods of spectral sums built from the Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac spectrum on the unit three-sphere, $|\lambda_n| = n + 3/2$ with degeneracy $2(n+1)(n+2)$. (1) T9: the spectral zeta function of $D^2$ is a two-term polynomial in $π^2$ with rational coefficients at every integer $s \ge 1$; at half-integer $s \ge 5/2$ the same closed form carries odd zeta values (Theorem (ref)). (2) Parity: the Dirac Dirichlet series $D(s)$ lies in $\mathbb{Q}[π^2]$ at even $s$ and is a rational combination of $ζ(s{-}2)$ and $ζ(s)$ at odd $s \ge 5$ (Theorem (ref)). (3) \textbf{The $\chi_{-4}$ split:} the sub-sums over even and odd $n$ satisfy $D_{\mathrm{even}}(s) - D_{\mathrm{odd}}(s) = 2^{s-1}(\beta(s) - \beta(s{-}2))$ at every integer $s \ge 2$, so Catalan's $G$ and $\beta(4)$ appear in each half at $s = 4$ and cancel in the sum (Theorem (ref)). (4) Multi-loop chain sums: the two-loop sum is $2S_{\min}$, with $S_{\min}$ in closed form in $\mathbb{Q}[π^2, \ln 2, ζ(3), ζ(5)]$ (Hoffman double $t$-values, each reducing to depth $\le 1$); the three-loop sum $S^{(3)} = 40.6554\ldots$ (rigorous bracket $[40.65339, 40.65578]$) has an explicit closed form in $\mathbb{Q}[π^2, \ln 2, ζ(3), ζ(5), ζ(5,3)]$, in which $ζ(5,3)$, at coefficient $6π^2$, is the only constant beyond single zeta values and every level-2 depth-2 generator cancels; the four-loop sum has realized depth $\le 3$. Realized depth is $\le k-1$ at loop order $k = 2, 3, 4$ (Proposition (ref)). (5) Sommerfeld sums: the degeneracy-weighted coefficient sums $D_p$ of the exact Dirac hydrogen spectrum are weight-$(2p{-}1)$ zeta polynomials in which $ζ(2)ζ(2p{-}3)$ cancels and $\max(0, \lfloor(2p{-}5)/2\rfloor)$ products survive, observed through $p = 6$ (Observations (ref), (ref)); a shell-sum identity removes $ζ(s{-}1)$ from a fine-structure Dirichlet series (Theorem (ref)). The multi-loop sums are defined sums: their vertex weights come from a vertex model, the scalar-harmonic triangle with a parity clause (Eq. (ref)). The physical vector-photon vertex on $S^3$ obeys a different rule (Paper 33), so the period statements are statements about the sums as defined. Two results whose reading depends on the vertex rule are stated for the model: the ground-state zero of the model's one-loop self-energy (Theorem (ref)), which fails for the physical vertex, and an unnormalized one-loop magnetic-moment ratio (Sec. (ref)), whose normalization is owed.
