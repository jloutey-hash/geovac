Selection Rules of the Vector-Photon Vertex on S^3: the Physical Vertex, the Vertex Model and the Graph

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 33 · QED and Gauge Theory · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286817 · PDF

Abstract

We compute the electron–photon vertex $V = \int_{S^3} \langle \psi_1, c(A) \psi_2 \rangle$ on the round unit three-sphere, with $\psi_{1,2}$ Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac eigenspinors, $A$ a coclosed 1-form eigenform and $c$ Clifford multiplication, and use it to score eight selection rules. Representation theory of $\mathrm{Spin}(4) = \mathrm{SU}(2)_L × \mathrm{SU}(2)_R$ gives the rule, and two independent exact computations confirm it with no extra channel and no accidental zero: a group-theoretic route through Clebsch–Gordan coefficients (896 cells: sign pair, photon component and levels $n_1, n_2 \le 3$, $q \le 7$) and a coordinate route by exact monomial integration in $ℝ^4$ (360 cells), which agree wherever both run. For spinors of the same sign of $D$ the vertex keeps the window $|n_1 - n_2| < q < n_1 + n_2$, $n_1 + n_2 + q$ odd, of the vertex model used for spectral sums in Paper 28, and adds the channel $q = n_1 + n_2 + 1$. For opposite signs it opens channels with $n_1 + n_2 + q$ even, which the model has none of. The ground state couples, also to itself: the diagonal vertex $V(a, a)$ does not vanish. The Ward–Takahashi identity $\langle u, c(df) v \rangle = (\lambda_u - \lambda_v)\langle u, f v\rangle$ holds exactly. Each of the eight rules is scored three ways: on the GeoVac graph (scalar Fock graph 1/8, native Dirac graph 4/8, the latter by the definition of its edges), in the vertex model, and with the physical vertex. With the physical vertex, rules 1 (triangle sparsity) and 4 ($\Delta m$) hold, rule 6 holds in Ward–Takahashi form, and rules 2 (ground-state zero), 5 (E1 parity) and 8 ($q \le n_1 + n_2$) fail; rule 3 holds as a statement of representation theory but not as the model's channel-count table; for rule 7 the diagonal-vertex zero fails and Furry's theorem itself is not decided without charge conjugation and three-vertex traces. The $1{+}6{+}1$ partition that the vertex model gives does not survive the physical vertex. Kept from the graph and model analyses: the pendant-edge value $\Sigma_{\mathrm{graph}}(\mathrm{GS}) = 2(n_{\max}-1)/n_{\max}$, the fact that the edge Laplacian does not block-diagonalize by photon labels $(q, m_q)$, the diagonal $α_z$ identity of the model, and the normalization identity $\Sigma_{l=1} = 1/(4π)$. Coupling normalization, regularization and the gauge sector are owed.
