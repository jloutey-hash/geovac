State-space Gromov–Hausdorff convergence of spectral truncations of compact semisimple Lie groups with bi-invariant metric

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 40 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286668 · PDF

Abstract

Let $G$ be a compact connected semisimple Lie group of rank $r \ge 1$ equipped with a bi-invariant Riemannian metric, and let $\Tcal_G = (C^\infty(G), L^2(G, \Sigma), \Dop_G)$ denote its canonical metric spectral triple, with $\Dop_G$ the Kostant cubic Dirac operator. For each cutoff $\Lammax > 0$, let $\Tcal_\Lammax = (\Op_\Lammax, \Hilb_\Lammax, P_\Lammax \Dop_G P_\Lammax)$ denote the Connes–van Suijlekom spectral truncation obtained by projection onto irreducibles with Casimir $\le \Lammax^2$. We prove that (for $SU(2)$; for general $G$ conditionally on the per-group spin-window decomposition, see below) $\Tcal_\Lammax$ converges to $\Tcal_G$ in van Suijlekom's state-space Gromov–Hausdorff distance, with the state spaces metrized by the left-translation Lipschitz seminorm as in Paper 38 (convergence in the Dirac-commutator seminorm is open), with $$ d_{\mathrm{GH}}(\Tcal_\Lammax, \Tcal_G) \;\le\; \gamma_\Lammax(G) \;\to\; 0, \qquad \gamma_\Lammax(SU(2)) = \frac{2}{π} \frac{\log \Lammax}{\Lammax} + O(1/\Lammax), $$ with constant $1$ from the lifted-state route of Paper 38 (the Lipschitz comparison constant $C_3(G) = 1$ of Lemma (ref) enters only the withdrawn approximation-pair route). The $SU(2)$ moment constant $2/π$ [INTERNAL THEOREM] is Paper 38's $(4/π)\log n/n$ read in the cutoff variable used here: $\mathrm{Cas}(\mathrm{ad}) = h^\vee = 2$ on $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ fixes $\langle X, Y\rangle = -2 \mathrm{tr}(XY)$, whose geodesic distance from the identity is the rotation angle — exactly the distance Paper 38 integrates against — and Paper 38's shell index is $n_{\max} = 2j_{\max} + 1$, so $n \simeq 2\Lammax$. It is a kernel constant, not the convergence rate: a tapered amplitude gives $O(1/\Lammax)$ on $SU(2)$ [MEASURED], Paper 38; for general $G$ this is open. Because $\gamma_\Lammax \Lammax$ is invariant under rescaling the bi-invariant metric, the constant in $\Lammax$ does not depend on the normalisation; Paper 38's values $4/π$ (dual-Coxeter) and $2/π$ (unit $S^3$) are per its shell index $n$. Whether the leading constant $c(G)$ of $\gamma_\Lammax(G)$ is the same for every compact connected simple $G$ is [OPEN]. Paper 38 [ref] is recovered as the rank-$1$ case $G = SU(2)$. The proof is the lifted-state route (UCP compression plus the conjugation average against the L2 central spectral Fejér kernel with closed-form quantitative rate); the lemma chain (L1$'$ operator system, L3 Lipschitz comparison with constant $1$ at every rank, proved by compression, L4 Berezin reconstruction via Peter–Weyl) supplies the substrate and supporting estimates, L3 entering only the withdrawn approximation-pair route. The approximation pair of \S(ref) is recorded but does not supply an independent proof: its dual reach is a named gap and both height constituents are refuted (Lemma (ref), corrected 2026-09-04). [OBSERVATION] Paper 38 reads its Fejér-moment constant (a reading, not a derivation) as the $k=0$ volume-slot content of a master Mellin engine $\mathcal M[\Tr(D^k e^{-tD^2})]$ on the spectral triple; whether the value so read extends from $SU(2)$ to the other simple groups is open with $c(G)$: a Plancherel $×$ Vandermonde cancellation is proposed as the mechanism (a proof sketch), and the rank-$2$ and rank-$3$ extractions reported here compare mismatched conventions and do not test it (status note in \S(ref)). For $SU(2)$ the result supplies an explicit rate (translation-seminorm metrization) for the state-space Gromov–Hausdorff convergence proved by Gaudillot-Estrada and van Suijlekom [ref]; for general $G$ it gives the bound $d_{\mathrm{GH}} \le \gamma_\Lammax(G) \to 0$, conditionally, with the rate form open. The strictly stronger Latrémolière propinquity is not claimed: its dual-reach step is a named gap (Remark (ref)), exactly as for the rank-1 case (Paper 38). Moreover the general-$G$ lifted-state construction underlying even the state-space GH statement is verified only for $SU(2)$, so for general $G$ the convergence is conditional on that per-group spin-window decomposition; the closed-form moment $\gamma_\Lammax(G)$ (the genuine new content) is unaffected. For semisimple $G$ with more than one simple factor the leading constant is open (Remark (ref)).
