Counting Angular Momentum Labels by Isotropic Packing

Josh Loutey · GeoVac synthesis document · Mathematical Foundations · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286740 · PDF

Abstract

[SYMBOLIC PROOF] We show that the angular momentum label set $(\ell, m)$ is counted exactly by a geometric construction: uniform-density packing of distinguishable states on concentric isotropic shells in two dimensions. Starting from two axioms—discrete binary distinguishability and geometric indifference (maximum entropy)—and the construction's structural choices (motivated, not forced), the construction yields per-shell angular capacities $2k-1$ for shell $k$, equal in count to the spherical harmonic basis $Y_{\ell m}$ with $\ell = k - 1$ and $m = -\ell, \ldots, +\ell$ (assigning the $m$ values to positions is a labelling choice). [OBSERVATION] The principal quantum number $n$ corresponds to a cumulative grouping of the first $n$ shells, producing the $n^2$ orbital count, but this grouping is not forced by the axioms alone. Similarly, the per-shell count carries a factor of 2 from the binary initialization ($N_{\mathrm{init}} = 2$), which we read as corresponding to spin multiplicity (a counting correspondence, not a derivation of spin). On the label set taken as a graph, the Laplacian splits into $\ell$-blocks (the edges change $n$ or $m$, never $\ell$) and so does not converge to the $S^3$ Laplacian—the spinless node set is the exact $S^3$ label set, on which $-\Delta_{S^3} = L^2 + A^2$ is the diagonal $SO(4)$ Casimir $n^2-1$—and [INTERNAL THEOREM] its top eigenvalue saturates the bipartite bound $\lambda_{\max} \to 2 d_{\max} = 8$ (attained in a mid-$\ell$ block, not in the $s$-wave block, whose own bound is 4), so that under a single matched scale $K_{\mathrm{vac}} = -1/16$ the extremal eigenvalue reaches the hydrogen ground-state energy—matched, not derived: the content that could have failed is the spectral bound, not the target the scale is matched to. \gvc{graph-s3-wall}{The per-shell spectrum $E_n = -Z^2/(2n^2)$ is not a function of the graph Laplacian}. On the labels it is the function $E_n = -Z^2/(2(C+1))$ of the diagonal Casimir $C = n^2-1$, i.e. the bound-state Hamiltonian is $H = -\tfrac{Z^2}{2}(1-\Delta_{S^3})^{-1}$, and that function is fixed by Fock's continuum projection: the eigenvalue $2π^2/n$ of the $S^3$ Coulomb kernel forces the focal length $p_0 = Z/n$, and the energy shell $p_0^2 = -2E$ then gives $E_n$ (Paper 7). [OBSERVATION] This angular cross-section recurs across the natural geometries of the Geometric Vacuum series as a basis label: the full $(\ell, m)$ labeling, with $2\ell+1$ states per $\ell$, labels the basis on $S^3$ for hydrogen, the per-electron partial waves of the hyperspherical treatment of helium, the angular channels of the molecule-frame hyperspherical treatment, and each fiber of the composed bundles ($\ell$ is a conserved quantum number only for the one-electron atom; elsewhere it is a channel label coupled by Gaunt coefficients); in the two-centre prolate-spheroidal geometry only $m$ survives as a label, because the $\eta$-separation replaces $\ell$ by a separation index that reduces to it only in the united-atom limit. This is Paper 0 in the series.
