Boundary CFT_3-on-S³ observables and the GeoVac spectral triple: partition function and wedge KMS entropy

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 50 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286705 · PDF

Abstract

We exhibit three complementary boundary-side quantities, framed by standard CFT$_{3}$-on-$S³$ calculations, around the GeoVac spectral triple, each obtained by a structurally distinct operation — (A) from the full, untruncated continuum spectra of the conformally coupled scalar and Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac operators on the round $S³$, (B) from the wedge KMS state $\rhoW = e^{-\Kalpha}/Z$ built on the truncated triple from a posited wedge generator, (C) by comparing that generator with a continuum one: (A) the universal F-theorem coefficient $F_{S³}$, the standard zeta-regularised determinant $-\tfrac{1}{2}ζ'_{\Delta}(0)$ of the continuum spectrum, is re-derived in closed Hurwitz form and agrees with the Klebanov–Pufu–Safdi (2011) values (symbolically for the Dirac, to 61 digits with PSLQ identification for the scalar) for both the conformally coupled scalar ($F_{s} = \tfrac{\log 2}{8} - \tfrac{3ζ(3)}{16π^{2}}$) and the free Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac ($F_{D} = \tfrac{\log 2}{4} + \tfrac{3ζ(3)}{8π^{2}}$) — an independent re-derivation of known continuum values, in which the finite cutoff $n_max$ never enters; (B) the wedge KMS entropy on the truncated triple has the exact asymptotic $S(\rhoW) = 2\logn_max + (\coth 1 - \log(2\sinh 1)) + O(1/n_max)$, whose slope $2$ is the exponent of the $\simn_max^{2}$ degeneracy of the lowest shell of the posited generator $\Kalpha$ (the slope is independent of the inverse temperature; the constant is the unit-$\beta$ value, and $\rhoW$ is not the Bisognano–Wichmann-normalised state, which would be $e^{-π\Kalpha}/Z$ since $\Kalpha$ carries the values of $2J_{z}$; the $2$ is not the dimension of the entangling surface, which for a CFT$_{3}$ is a circle); (C) the framework's wedge generator $\Kalpha$ (a posited relabelling built from the polar rotation generator $J_{\mathrm{polar}} = 2J_z$, not derived from a state) shares a discrete integer spectrum and closure at rotation angle $2π$ (its adjoint flow closes at $t = π$) with the Euclidean rotation generator whose Wick rotation is the continuum Casini–Huerta–Myers modular Hamiltonian of a hemispherical cap of the spatial $S^{2}$ (the latter itself generates a boost-like flow with continuous spectrum); this is a structural analogy with the Euclidean generator only, and the identification of $\Kalpha$ as the modular Hamiltonian of a wedge-restricted state is [OPEN] (2026-09-28; see [ref]). The scalar/Dirac free energies decompose orthogonally into their two transcendental sectors: $F_{D} + 2 F_{s} = \log(2)/2$ isolates the $\log 2$ sector (a level-2 cyclotomic period, in the ring of the M3 sub-mechanism of the master Mellin engine of [ref]), and $F_{D} - 2 F_{s} = 3ζ(3)/(4π^{2})$ isolates the $ζ(3)/π^{2}$ sector (an M1$×$M3 cross-product), following the engine's period-ring stratification [ref]; no Seeley–DeWitt (M2) content appears in these values. This is a decomposition of the continuum KPS values, and the first application of the master Mellin engine's tagging to independently published (non-GeoVac-internal) values. The F-theorem values themselves are due to [ref] and are not new; the present paper's contributions are (i) their independent re-derivation from the continuum spectra via closed Hurwitz forms (with PSLQ confirmation), (ii) the orthogonal $\log 2$ / $ζ(3)/π^{2}$ dual-basis decomposition of those continuum values, classified as M3-ring and M1$×$M3 content (Theorem (ref)), and (iii) the exact large-$n_max$ asymptotic of the wedge-KMS entropy on the truncated triple, a lowest-shell degeneracy count. The framework has no bulk, so none of this is holographic. The CHM comparison (C) is recorded as an analogy only: $\Kalpha$ is posited, and a GH distance between spectral triples does not bound the convergence of one operator to another. The bulk-side identifications (Ryu–Takayanagi minimum surfaces, JLMS bulk-modular reconstruction, AdS$_{4}$ / H$^{4}$ dual geometry) are blocked at the framework's existing infrastructure: zero AdS / H$^{4}$ machinery, and the Wick-rotation $S³ \to H^{3}$ supplies no natural discrete subgroup [ref].
