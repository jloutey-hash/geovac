Wilson Lattice Gauge Theory on the GeoVac Label Graphs: Exact Factorization on the Scalar Graph and Compact U(1) on the Dirac Rule-B Graph

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 30 · QED and Gauge Theory · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286806 · PDF

Abstract

The GeoVac label graphs carry Wilson lattice gauge theories, and the two graphs studied here sit at opposite ends of solvability. On the scalar label graph, whose vertices are the labels $(n,l,m)$ of the $S^3$ harmonics, no edge changes $l$, and each $l$-block is a planar $(n_{\max}-l)×(2l+1)$ grid. Taking the plaquettes to be the graph's 4-cycles (the unit squares of these grids), the plaquettes form a basis of the cycle space at every $n_{\max}$, so after gauge fixing on a spanning tree the plaquette holonomies are independent Haar variables. $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson theory on this graph is therefore the exactly solvable two-dimensional lattice gauge theory: $Z(\beta) = [2e^{-\beta} I_1(\beta)/\beta]^{\beta_1}$, and every closed loop enclosing $A$ plaquettes has $\langle W \rangle = (I_2(\beta)/I_1(\beta))^{A}$, an area law by factorization that carries no information about confinement. The weak-coupling expansion gives the curl form $(\beta/8)\sum_a \langle A^a, B_2 B_2^{\top} A^a\rangle$, which is supported on the cycle space and completes the edge Laplacian $B^{\top}B$ to the full Hodge-1 Laplacian of the plaquette-filled complex; the cubic term is nonzero, with a vertex proportional to $\epsilon^{abc}$. Restricting the links to the maximal torus gives compact $U(1)$ Wilson theory, with $\langle W\rangle = (I_1/I_0)^A$, and we record the abelian dictionary between Wilson and graph-Hodge data together with the exact $n_{\max}=3$ edge-Laplacian spectrum. On the Dirac Rule-B graph, whose edges are E1 dipole transitions and all change $l$, the graph is connected, its 4-cycles outnumber the independent cycles ($44$ against $11$ at $n_{\max}=2$) and close into 2-cycles of three plaquettes, so compact $U(1)$ does not factorize. Seven standard diagnostics are compatible with compact $U(1)$ and discriminate no dimension: the monopole density falls roughly exponentially in $\beta$ on a range below the four-dimensional transition, full Monte Carlo Wilson loops resolve no area law (the area coefficient of a joint area-plus-perimeter fit is consistent with zero at $n_{\max}=2$), and the Polyakov loop on the product graph with a time circle is well defined through one extra cohomology class, while its expectation vanishes by centre symmetry. Each Rule-B link lies in many plaquettes ($8.8$ and $37.5$ on average at $n_{\max}=2,3$), so the comparison class is mean-field or large-dimension lattice gauge theory, not a three-dimensional lattice. The label graphs have no continuum limit, and nothing here bears on one.
