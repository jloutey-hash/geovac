Observables as Projections of a π-free Graph: A Synthesis of the GeoVac Precision-Observation Arc

Josh Loutey · GeoVac synthesis document · Synthesis / Reading Guides · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286869 · PDF

Abstract

The Geometric Vacuum (GeoVac) framework separates into two structurally distinct layers, and that separation is the organizing principle of its precision-observation arc. Layer 1 is a bare combinatorial graph: quantum-number labels $(n,\ell,m,\ldots)$, rational matrix entries, algebraic eigenvalues, no physical units, and—verified symbolically—no $π$. Layer 2 is a finite family of named projections from that graph onto continuous physical settings; every physical variable, every dimension, and every transcendental constant that appears in any GeoVac calculation enters through exactly one projection and is traceable to it, apart from external inputs (literature values and applied field strengths) and textbook-assembly rows that enter through no GeoVac projection. This synthesis assembles the five Group 6 papers into a single narrative built on that thesis. The spine is: the projection taxonomy and its three-axis tagging (Paper 34 [ref]); time as the prototype free-side projection—$π$ enters the Klein–Gordon spectrum on $S^3×ℝ$ at exactly one step, temporal compactification, while rest mass preserves the algebraic ring (Paper 35 [ref]); entropy as an embedding-class projection artifact, identically zero on the one-body graph and finite only after the metric embedding of the two-body repulsion (Paper 27 [ref]); and the entanglement structure that isolates that repulsion in the angular-momentum eigenbasis (Paper 26 [ref]). Roothaan autopsies close the arc:\ they decompose precision atomic observables into projection-chain-tagged components and locate framework kernel gaps; the hydrogen Lamb shift, whose leading one-loop assembly is textbook QED with tabulated Bethe logarithms (Paper 36 [ref]), is the limiting case. The literature itemization differences the autopsies meet are stated in their sources. Two disciplines run throughout. First, every transcendental named here is tagged to its projection—$π$ via the M1 volume slot (unit-sphere and Fock volumes), $π^{2k}$ via the spectral action, $ζ(2k)$ via even-zeta Dirichlet series, odd $ζ$ via the half-integer-Hurwitz spinor lift, Catalan $G$ via quarter-integer vertex parity, $2π$ via temporal compactification. Second, the precision comparisons set framework-native values against measurement, some with residuals the autopsies leave unexplained; they are not first-principles predictions of the constants, and the multi-focal-composition wall—the framework has no native theorem for composing multiple Fock-style projections—is named as an active boundary. The unifying reading is that compactness forces discreteness (the converse is false: discrete spectrum is the compact-resolvent property), made operational: the discrete skeleton is the compact/closed regime, and a transcendental is precisely the price of releasing compactness by integrating over a promoted continuous parameter.
