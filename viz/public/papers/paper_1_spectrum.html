The Geometric Atom: Atomic Structure as a Packing Problem

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 1 · Mathematical Foundations · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286750 · PDF

Abstract

We present a discrete geometric framework for the hydrogen atom based on finite information capacity. The hydrogen state space is modeled as a paraboloid lattice dual to the SO(4,2) conformal algebra. This single-particle model exhibits a fundamental algebra-geometry duality: (i) Algebraic exactness: [PANEL-VERIFIED] Transition operators $T_\pm, L_\pm$ reproduce the exact Rydberg quantum-number structure ($n$ and $\ell(\ell+1)$) from operator eigenvalues; the energies $E_n = -1/(2n^2)$ follow once the matched kinetic scale is applied (an Observation, Paper 7). (ii) Geometric artifacts: [MEASURED] A node-amplitude proxy for the $s/p$ gap is nonzero at finite lattice cutoff ($37%$ at $n_{\max}=5$, oscillating through $15.6%$ at $8$ and $1.7%$ at $10$ to $0.39%$ at $30$ on the binary production lattice; see the \S III QA note). [INTERNAL THEOREM] Its size tracks the residue of $n_{\max}$ modulo $3$ — exactly closed form on $n_{\max} \equiv 0$, and generically larger off it (the earlier $n_{\max}/(\sqrt{3} π)$ asymptotic is [retracted 2026-09-04: sp-ratio-false-precision], read off three sampled cutoffs) — so the quoted endpoint sits on a favourable branch and the sequence is not evidence of convergence (2026-09-03; the earlier wording, ``the graph Laplacian lifts $s/p$ degeneracy \ldots through differential weighted connectivity'', both contradicted the clause below and named the weighted construction's mechanism for binary-lattice numbers, where every edge weight is $1$). The cutoff sweep from $n_{\max}=5$ to 30 shows this is a discretization artifact—a residue-controlled truncation effect, not spectral aliasing [retracted 2026-09-04: sp-splitting-aliasing-mechanism] (that reading was withdrawn 2026-09-03: the $l$-blocks are disconnected and carry no degeneracy to alias)—that oscillates and reads $0.39%$ at $n_{\max}=30$ on the $n_{\max}\equiv 0 \pmod 3$ branch ($1.65%$ and $2.58%$ at $28$ and $29$): a proxy value, not evidence of recovering the exact Coulomb degeneracy [retracted 2026-09-04: sp-splitting-aliasing-mechanism], which belongs to the continuum operator (the graph's $l$-blocks are disconnected, so the graph has no $s/p$ degeneracy of its own). (iii) Geometric phase structure: [SYMBOLIC PROOF] The Berry phase vanishes identically for the real-valued transition operators; the edge-weight log-holonomy $\Theta(n) = -2\ln((n{+}1)/n) \sim n^{-1}$ measures the curvature of the weight function across the lattice. Crucially, this model describes only the electron manifold—electromagnetic interactions and the fine structure constant $α$ cannot emerge from a single-particle lattice and require coupling to photon degrees of freedom, which we address in a companion paper.
