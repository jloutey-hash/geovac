Spectral truncation of the flat plane: state-space convergence with a squared window, and the disk-with-cone left open

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 53 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286712 · PDF

Abstract

The spectral-truncation convergence results of the companion Papers [ref] treat compact groups, where a Fejér-type kernel is available and the state spaces are compact. We treat the flat plane $ℝ^{2}$ — non-compact, but with a transitive translation group. Truncate $L^{2}(ℝ^{2})$ to the band-limited subspace $\Hilb_{\Lambda}$ (Fourier support $|\xi|\le\sqrt\Lambda$), compress bounded uniformly continuous multipliers into it, and metrize the truncated state space by the translation Lipschitz seminorm. With a lift built from the translates of one window vector $\psi_{\Lambda}\in\Hilb_{\Lambda}$, the compression/lifted-state argument of Paper 38 (whose scalar compact-group form is due to Gaudillot-Estrada and van Suijlekom [ref]) goes through with the positive kernel $|\psi_{\Lambda}|^{2}$. Four estimates hold with the single constant $\gamma_{\Lambda}=\int|x| |\psi_{\Lambda}(x)|^{2}dx= \gamma_{1}\Lambda^{-1/2}$ [INTERNAL THEOREM], and they give a Gromov–Hausdorff bound $d_{\mathrm{GH}}\le\gamma_{\Lambda}$ between the Monge–Kantorovich state spaces, taken over the states of finite first moment [INTERNAL THEOREM] for those state classes; whether they are the canonical ones is open. The window must have a finite first moment. The sharp ball window does not: its first moment diverges logarithmically. The smooth-edged windows $(1-|\xi|^{2})_{+}^{m}$ with $m\ge1$ do; for $m=1$, $\gamma_{1}=32/(5π)$. The two-dimensional Bochner–Riesz map is not a usable lift: its kernel is negative somewhere at every order, and its Lebesgue constant exceeds $1$ [PRIOR ART] (both classical facts [ref], with a one-line argument recorded here). On the finite Dirichlet disk-with-cone the paper reports measurements. The Markov–Ces\`aro reconstruction map is not positivity-preserving (a ring counterexample), and its reconstruction error on the open disk decays on every tested observable. Convergence on the disk, and on the cone with apex angle $α\ne1$ (which has no translation group), is left open.
