Tomita–Takesaki modular structure on truncated SU(2) spectral triples: the four-witness 2π period at finite cutoff

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 42 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286670 · PDF

Abstract

The four-witness Wick-rotation theorem (Hartle–Hawking [ref], Sewell [ref], Bisognano–Wichmann [ref], Unruh [ref]) asserts that a state of a quantum field restricted to a region bounded by a bifurcate Killing horizon with surface gravity $\kappa_g$ is KMS at inverse temperature $\beta = 2π/\kappa_g$ with respect to Killing-time evolution. The shared $2π$ across all four faces is $\Vol(S^1)$, the regularity period of the Euclidean rotation of the observer's causal-domain coordinates. That continuum statement is prior art and is not re-proved here. We study its finite-cutoff period on the Connes–van Suijlekom [ref] truncated Camporesi–Higuchi [ref] spectral triple $\Tcal_{n_max}$, with a hemispheric wedge and a posited wedge generator $K_α^W = \mathrm{diag}(2m_j)$ on the $m_j > 0$ wedge states, a relabelling built from the doubled polar rotation generator $2J_z$ (whose own compression to the wedge is zero). Because $K_α^W$ has integer spectrum, $e^{2π i K_α^W} = I$, and the conjugation flow closes, $\sigma_{2π}(O) = O$, for every operator $O$ — an elementary fact [SYMBOLIC] that holds for any integer-spectrum generator whatsoever and therefore does not single out a Bisognano–Wichmann modular flow. The Tomita–Takesaki reading is not an independent check: the wedge state is defined as $\rho_W = e^{-K_α^W}/Z$, so Tomita's theorem for a Gibbs state returns the same generator, $\sigma_t^{\TT} = \sigma_{-t}^{α}$. Nor is the agreement of the four witnesses (Bisognano–Wichmann, Hartle–Hawking, Sewell, Unruh) a finite-cutoff result: the six witness instantiations use the same $K_α^W$ with the surface gravity $\kappa_g$ relabelled, and $\beta H_{\mathrm{local}} = K_α^W$ is $\kappa_g$-independent by construction, so their bit-identical residuals hold by construction. We record these closures (bit-exact in floating point at $n_max \in \{2, 3, 4, 5\}$, residual $O(\sqrt{\dim \Hilb_{n_max}} \cdot \varepsilon_{\mathrm{machine}})$) as what they are. We also record the operator-level distinction between $2J_z$ ($e^{2π i K} = +I$) and the wedge-restricted Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac (half-integer spectrum, $e^{2π i D_W} = -I$, the spinor double-cover sign; the conjugation flow closes for both). The genuine finite-cutoff question — derive $K$ as the modular Hamiltonian of a wedge-restricted Camporesi–Higuchi state, rather than posit it — is [OPEN]. The Lorentzian extension to signature $(3, 1)$ via Bizi–Brouder–Besnard 2018 [ref] Krein-space spectral triples is carried out at finite cutoff in the present paper (\S(ref), which absorbs the former Paper 43): a Krein carrier and Krein-self-adjoint Lorentzian Dirac, an axiom audit at Bizi–Brouder–Besnard signature $(4, 6)$ in which the universal chirality anticommutation fails on the truthful Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac, the same automatic (and signature-blind) period closure, and a Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonality between the posited local Hamiltonian and the wedge Dirac that is a chirality-parity identity [SYMBOLIC], the only transcendental of its residual, $1/π^2$, being the posited $\beta = 2π/\kappa_g$ squared. Its continuum (Lorentzian-propinquity-rate) closure was described here as the named open frontier when this paper was drafted; that is superseded (2026-06, Paper 45 [ref]): the natural device fails structurally, and the chase is closed on the convention branch (an [ARGUED] reading: its Euclidean-only leg is generator-independent, its Wick-rotation/KMS-circle leg is conditional on O0 for the Bisognano–Wichmann generator; Paper 45 Q1). The mechanism is visible in this paper's own result (i): the posited wedge generator has integer spectrum, so the finite-cutoff flow is a compact $\beta = 2π$ circle rather than a non-compact boost [SYMBOLIC], and on the argued reading [ARGUED] every Lipschitz/boost seminorm built on it is signature-blind. The finite-cutoff closures reported below stand as computed; what does not follow from them is a metric statement about signature, or an identification of the posited generator with a Bisognano–Wichmann boost. The construction is a finite-dimensional illustration of the Connes–Rovelli thermal-time setup [ref] on a hemispheric wedge of the round $S³$, not a derivation of it.
