The GeoVac Spectral Triple: A Synthesis Paper Locking the Framework into the Marcolli–van Suijlekom Gauge-Network Lineage

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 32 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286660 · PDF

Abstract

The GeoVac framework has, over Papers 0–31, accumulated a body of structural results—the $S^3$ Fock-projection equivalence (Paper 7 [ref]), the angular sparsity theorem (Paper 22 [ref]), the periods of Dirac spectral sums on $S^3$ (Paper 28 [ref]), the Bargmann–Segal $S^5$ HO lattice (Paper 24 [ref]), Wilson lattice gauge theory on the label graphs (Paper 30 [ref]), and the universal/Coulomb partition (Paper 31 [ref])—each self-contained but together suggesting one parent structure. [INTERNAL THEOREM] This paper names and constructs that structure: a finite spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{GV}}, \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{GV}}, D_{\mathrm{GV}})$ on the Fock-projected $S^3$ graph, with $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{GV}}$ the algebra of complex functions on graph nodes, $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{GV}}$ the Camporesi–Higuchi spinor space restricted to the truncation, and $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$ the diagonal Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac operator [ref] on the truncated spinor space of unit $S^3$ (no graph form is attached to $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$; Remark (ref)). This is not a new derivation; it is a structural map. Three results follow. [OBSERVATION] (1) Lineage. The construction sits inside the published Marcolli–van Suijlekom gauge-network framework [ref] (with the Perez-Sanchez correction [ref] that the continuum limit is Yang–Mills without Higgs), and inside the Connes–van Suijlekom spectral-truncation program [ref]. Every structural ingredient of the GeoVac construction has published precedent; what is not duplicated is \gvc{k-combination-rule}{the $α$ observation ($K = π(B + F - \Delta)$ matches $α^{-1}$ to $4.8 × 10^{-7}$;\ the root of the cubic $1/α = K - α^2$ matches to $8.8 × 10^{-8}$; Paper 2 [ref])} from a discrete spectral construction. [OBSERVATION] (2) Sub-sector identification. Paper 28's Dirac spectral sums on $S^3$ are sums over the spectrum of $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$, and the spectral action on that spectrum is Paper 51's [ref]; Paper 31's universal/Coulomb partition is the $\mathcal{A}/D$ split of the present triple's spectral data. Paper 30's compact $U(1)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson actions are read as the Cartan-torus and full non-abelian slices of an almost-commutative extension by $M_2(\mathbb{C})$, but through the gauge-network (edge-hopping) Dirac of those papers, not through $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$: the diagonal $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$ commutes with every $π(f)$ and so admits no inner fluctuations. These are read as four projections of one structure, at the level of spectral and algebraic data. [INTERNAL THEOREM] (3) Axiom audit. $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{GV}}$ is finite, involutive, faithfully represented on $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{GV}}$; $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$ is self-adjoint, and since $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$ and every $π(a)$ are diagonal in the same basis, $[D_{\mathrm{GV}}, π(a)] = 0$ identically: the diagonal triple is a spectral, not a metric, object (the metric role belongs to the non-diagonal multipliers of the truncated operator system on the true Camporesi–Higuchi window — the span of complete Dirac eigenspaces, Paper 38 [ref] — whose state spaces are proved to converge under the left-translation Lipschitz seminorm; convergence in the Dirac-commutator seminorm is [OPEN], that seminorm being non-degenerate on the window only as measured at $n_{\max} \le 3$). A real structure $J_{\mathrm{GV}}$ exists on the Dirac sector with $J_{\mathrm{GV}}^2 = -\mathbb{1}$ (the quaternionic/pseudoreal sign for the 2-dimensional Dirac spinor representation of $\mathrm{Spin}(3) = \mathrm{SU}(2)$) and $J_{\mathrm{GV}} D_{\mathrm{GV}} = +D_{\mathrm{GV}} J_{\mathrm{GV}}$, the signs $(\epsilon, \epsilon') = (-1, +1)$ of the continuum $S^3$ factor ($\mathrm{KO}$-dimension $3$ mod $8$ [ref]). The continuum factor is odd, and so is the true Camporesi–Higuchi window (measured, $n_{\max} \le 3$: the commutant of its operator system is the scalars), the truncation that converges; only the chirality-doubled Fock/Weyl model truncation is even, via the chirality-doubling grading $\gamma_{\mathrm{GV}} = \sigma_x ⊗ \mathbb{1}$, with sign triple $(-,+,+)$ and no $\mathrm{KO}$ label attached at finite cutoff. [MEASURED] On the Fock/Weyl model of the operator system (the computational object of this paper, in its Weyl and chirality-doubled forms) the order-zero and order-one conditions fail at finite $n_{\max}$, with residuals that grow over the measured cutoffs (order zero at $n_{\max} = 2, 3, 4$, order one at $n_{\max} = 2, 3$; mechanism open). [MEASURED] On the true Camporesi–Higuchi window that converges, the charge conjugation $J\psi = (i\sigma_y)\overline{\psi}$ satisfies $J^2 = -\mathbb{1}$, $JD = +DJ$ and $J\mathcal{O}J^{-1} = \mathcal{O}$ exactly, while order zero and order one fail from $n_{\max} = 1$ with residuals that grow toward the sup-norm scale of the generators; there every residual is a Toeplitz boundary term of the compression, confined to the top Dirac levels of the window. [OBSERVATION] The four-way $S^3$ coincidence—Fock projection image, Hopf bundle total space $S³$ (base $S^2$), Camporesi–Higuchi spin carrier, $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ gauge manifold—is the statement that the manifold reached by the operator-system truncations in the continuum limit, the spinor bundle of the Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac, and the gauge group of the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ almost-commutative extension (Paper 30) all coincide on the unique rank-1 non-abelian compact simply-connected Lie group. \noindentScope. This paper does not derive new physics. It synthesizes existing GeoVac results into the axiomatic spectral-triple language. The synthesis lifts each result from a free-standing observation to a sub-sector of a single named structure, and makes explicit which axioms hold rigorously, which hold up to the standard finite-truncation caveats, and which remain open. [PANEL-VERIFIED] The real-structure question—whether the GeoVac Dirac operator $D_{\mathrm{GV}}$ (the diagonal spectral form) carries a real structure matching the signs of $S^3$ exactly at finite $n_{\max}$ or only in the continuum limit—is resolved in favour of finite-$n_{\max}$ exactness for the three $J$-relations $J^2 = -\mathbb{1}$, $JD = +DJ$, $J\mathcal{O}J^{-1} = \mathcal{O}$ only, measured on the Fock/Weyl model objects and, independently, on the true Camporesi–Higuchi window. [MEASURED] The order-zero and order-one conditions fail at finite $n_{\max}$ with residuals that grow with the cutoff, on the Fock/Weyl model of the operator system and on the true window alike (on the true window as a boundary term of the compression). The Fock/Weyl model truncation is even (chirality doubling; sign triple $(-,+,+)$, no $\mathrm{KO}$ label); the true window is odd (measured, $n_{\max} \le 3$), like the continuum (its operator system generates all of $B(\mathcal{H})$, so it admits no grading), and there the pair $(\varepsilon, \varepsilon') = (-,+)$ is forced by equivariance; the residual open items are catalogued in Sec. (ref).
