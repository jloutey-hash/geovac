State-space Gromov–Hausdorff convergence of truncated tensor-product Camporesi–Higuchi spectral triples on S³ × S³

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 39 · Operator Algebras / Noncommutative Geometry · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286666 · PDF

Abstract

We prove that the Connes–van Suijlekom truncations of the tensor-product Camporesi–Higuchi spectral triple $\Tcal_{S³}^{\lambda_a} ⊗ \Tcal_{S³}^{\lambda_b}$ on $S³ × S³$ at two (possibly distinct) focal lengths $\lambda_a, \lambda_b > 0$ converge to the continuum tensor-product triple in van Suijlekom's state-space Gromov–Hausdorff distance, with explicit rate \( \Lambda(\Tcal_{\nmaxa} ⊗ \Tcal_{\nmaxb}, \Tcal_{S³}^{\lambda_a} ⊗ \Tcal_{S³}^{\lambda_b}) \le \gamma^{(ab)}_\lambda \le \lambda_a \gamma_{\nmaxa} + \lambda_b \gamma_{\nmaxb}, \) of leading constant $1$, where $\gamma^{(ab)}_\lambda$ is the joint first moment of the product Fejér measure and $\gamma_n = (4/π)\log n / n + O(1/n)$ inherits from the single-factor result of [ref]. The proof is the lifted-state construction (Lemma (ref)), which uses neither a Berezin map nor any partial inverse: the dual reach $\mathrm{reach}_P$ is unnecessary, both almost-inverse defects being the same Fejér smoothing at the same moment. The theorem is [INTERNAL THEOREM] in the translation-seminorm metrization — the footing of Paper 38's thm:main_unconditional, with which both state spaces are metrized; that seminorm agrees with the truncated-Dirac commutator seminorm on the continuum and, unlike it, is non-degenerate at finite cutoff. The limit is the graded tensor product of the two odd (KO-$3$) Camporesi–Higuchi real spectral triples — Hilbert space $\Hilb_a ⊗ \C^2 ⊗ \Hilb_b$, Dirac $\DCH^{(a)}⊗\sigma_1⊗ 1 + 1⊗\sigma_2⊗\DCH^{(b)}$, grading $1⊗\sigma_3⊗ 1$, real structure $J_{a,b} = J_a⊗\sigma_1 K⊗ J_b$ at KO-dimension $6$ (Lemma (ref)); the Clifford $\C^2$ is the doubling that carries the product grading and real structure, the naive $\DCH^{(a)}⊗ I + \gamma_a⊗\DCH^{(b)}$ being unrealisable ($\gamma_a = 0$ on $S³$). The strictly stronger Latrémolière propinquity is not claimed: it is not pursued here, and its dual-reach step would reintroduce the $\mathrm{reach}_P$ named gap of Papers 38/40 that the lifted-state route avoids; the object established is van Suijlekom state-space Gromov–Hausdorff convergence. \emph{An earlier $(B,P)$-pair assembly (the retained Lemma (ref)) gave a weaker conditional bound with constant $2$ via the joint Lipschitz comparison constant $C_3^{(2)} \le \sqrt2$; that route's height leg was refuted and its dual reach is a named gap, and it is superseded by the lifted-state route above.} The result extends [ref] to the simplest non-abelian tensor case, and closes a tensor-product state-space-GH case that the published literature on products of metric spectral triples [ref] does not cover; existing tensor-product propinquity results require the second factor to be finite-dimensional, AF, abelian, or to reduce to a single-character / Hopf-$S^1$ structure.
