A Convergent Discrete Replica Derivative on the Spinor Cone, with the Two-Term Camporesi–Higuchi Spectral Action on S^3 × S^1

Josh Loutey · GeoVac Paper 51 · QED and Gauge Theory · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21286826 · PDF

Abstract

We prove one new theorem and record the two continuum results it serves. The theorem concerns a finite-difference discretization of the two-dimensional cone of apex angle $2πα$ carrying an anti-periodic (spinor) field: its heat trace $K_n(α, t)$ converges to the continuum heat trace in $C^1$ in $α$ near $α = 1$, so the replica derivative $dK_n/dα|_{α=1}$, whose mode sum carries an extra weight $(j+\tfrac12)$, converges, and that weight costs nothing in the rate (Theorem (ref)). The proof dominates the mode series and its $α$-derivative by one summable Gaussian sequence. Numerically, the replica combination converges to the continuum value $1/6$, at order $O(a)$ in the radial spacing $a$ on the production grid. The continuum results are these. (i) The Chamseddine–Connes spectral action of the Camporesi–Higuchi Dirac operator on $S³_R$ and on $S³_R × S^1_\beta$ has exactly two power-law terms, up to exponentially small corrections. This is Chamseddine and Connes's result (by Poisson summation); we give an alternative proof through the Bernoulli identity $B_{2k+1}(3/2) = (2k{+}1)/4^k$, which makes the spectral zeta function vanish at every non-positive integer. The scalar and transverse-traceless tensor heat traces on $S³$ do not truncate. (ii) Traced over the rank-4 spinor bundle, the action's Einstein–Hilbert coefficient gives $G = 3π/(\phi(1)\Lambda^2)$ for a cutoff with Mellin moments $\phi(s)$. The replica derivative of the black-hole cigar's tip term, with the Dirac cone coefficient $-(1/12)(1/α - α)$ and the fermionic sign of the effective action, gives $S_{\BH} = A\Lambda^2\phi(1)/(12π) = A/(4G)$, positive and with the same $G$. That the two routes agree is the induced-gravity statement of Susskind–Uglum and Frolov–Fursaev, specialised to this spectrum. The absence of higher-curvature terms holds on the conformally flat $S³ × S^1$; the cigar is not conformally flat, and there the two routes agree at leading order in $\Lambda$. We also show that the second variation of the action on the $(1,1)$ sector of $SO(4)$ cannot distinguish the diagonal-$SU(2)$ spins $J = 0, 1, 2$, so it cannot by itself isolate a graviton. None of these results uses the GeoVac label lattice: they concern the continuum Camporesi–Higuchi spectrum and a finite-difference cone.
