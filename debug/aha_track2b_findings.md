# /aha Track 2b — base-rate census verdict (2026-08-21)

Full table: `debug/data/aha_period_census.md`. Diagnostic only; no paper edits, no production

## Verdict: the recurrence is AT base rate for blind search, ABOVE base rate conditional on
## mechanism — and the honest reading is "constrained landscape," not "surprising coincidence."

**Denominator.** Population A (blind numerical trials — PSLQ or exhaustive scans testing a given
constant against a period basis, no construction forcing the outcome): **N = 30** independent
objects/campaigns (9 Yukawa couplings, 11 W1e terms, the Δ 3,228-candidate scan, S_min, the S_min
depth-2 χ₋₄-targeted 35-attempt sweep, S^(3), the 480-cell Hain–Brown panel, 2 Bethe-log/A_60
probes, the Paper 59 integrated-T2 search, and the Paper 59 diagonal-cusp-coefficient search).
**Confirmed disc-4/ℚ(i) hits in this population: 0.** Two campaigns (W1e, and the S_min depth-2
χ₋₄ sweep) built and ran a disc-4 basis on purpose and both came back clean-negative. One object
(the integrated Paper 59 T2 observable itself) is still open, and it is trending negative at
reachable precision (~19 of the ~32–40 digits needed), with an independent structural argument
(Gevrey-1 resurgence, Borel radius 2) suggesting it may never land on a finite classical period at
all. **So: at the level of "does GeoVac's blind search machinery land on disc-4 more than chance
would predict," the answer is no — it essentially never does.** That is itself informative: it
rules out the reading that disc-4 is simply a common, easy-to-hit basin that PSLQ keeps stumbling
into.

**Where disc-4 actually comes from.** All three disc-4/8 hits in the corpus (M3's vertex-parity
Hurwitz identity, Paper 56's Kramers-doublet CM point, Paper 59's elliptic-family CM fibers) are
**theorems, not search results** — each is a specific, narrow construction (a chirality/vertex
projection literally applied to the Dirac Dirichlet series; a J²=−1 complex-structure forcing on
the fundamental spinor rep; a classical elliptic family's rational modulus map crossing a CM
point that happens to sit inside the physical parameter domain) that produces disc-4 content as a
*necessary consequence* of the construction, the same way computing ζ(2) on a circle necessarily
produces π². Counting these as 9 independent "forced mechanisms" (Population B: M1, M2, the
untwisted M3/F-theorem sub-sector, the vertex-parity M3 sub-sector, Paper 38's 4/π, B, F, and the
two independent CM constructions), 3/9 ≈ 33% land disc-4/8 — but the informative conditioning is
narrower: **among the mechanisms that reach beyond pure-Tate territory AT ALL** (i.e. exclude the
ones that are pure-Tate by construction — M1, M2, Paper 38, B, F, and the untwisted M3 sub-sector,
6 of the 9), **100% (3/3) land specifically at disc-4 or disc-8**. Paper 34's own 28-row projection
dictionary — an independent, pre-existing cross-check — gives the same shape: 1/28 rows (3.6%)
carries disc-4 content, and that row is the identical vertex-parity construction.

**Reading supported: constrained landscape.** The corpus does not have many roads out of
pure-Tate territory. It has essentially three: (1) half-integer spin / vertex-parity, which
forces quarter-integer Hurwitz shifts and hence χ₋₄; (2) a genuine complex (Hodge) structure,
which for a rational J with J²=−1 has no field to land in *except* ℚ(i) (the norm-1 torus of any
imaginary quadratic order with a rational-square-root discriminant of −1 specifically is ℚ(i) —
there is no other candidate at this level of algebraic simplicity); and (3) any elliptic curve
family with a rational 4-branch-point structure, whose universal cover is the Legendre/Γ(2)
family, whose CM points are forced by classical Chowla–Selberg theory and are dominated at the
simplest level by disc-4 and disc-8. None of these three routes was built *to* reach ℚ(i) — they
were each built for unrelated structural reasons (chirality grading in Paper 28, a real spectral
triple's KO-dimension in Paper 56, the genus of a 3-center ERI in Paper 59) and ℚ(i) fell out as
the first non-trivial CM field each one's own internal arithmetic could reach. That is precisely
what "few reachable classes" predicts, and it is exactly the language Paper 56 itself already
uses for the seam ("convergent, not common-cause," rem:paper59_cm) — this census supplies the
quantitative base-rate argument behind that qualitative call, rather than overturning it.

## The 3 most informative rows

1. **A2/A5 (W1e 11-term sweep; S_min depth-2 χ₋₄ sweep, 35 attempts).** The two campaigns in the
   whole corpus that explicitly built a disc-4 PSLQ basis on purpose and tested real numbers
   against it. Both clean negative. This is the strongest evidence that disc-4 is not a
   promiscuous basin — targeted search for it fails routinely.
2. **A7 (Hain–Brown 480-cell panel, Population A) vs B6/B7 (Kramers-CM, elliptic-family-CM,
   Population B).** The blind search against the *generic* modular ring failed; the two forced
   constructions independently found the *narrower* CM-ℚ(i) ring instead. Paper 56 already
   explains the A7 miss as a structural consequence of B6 — the corpus landing specifically on
   the CM-restricted sub-locus, not the generic modular one, is the single cleanest piece of
   "constrained landscape" evidence in the table.
3. **A10 (Paper 59 integrated T2, still open).** The one place where the corpus is actively
   testing whether disc-4 content survives from the *fiber* level (B7/B8, forced, always present)
   up to the *observable* level (genuinely uncertain). Current trend and independent resurgence
   argument both point toward "does not fully classicalize" — i.e. even where the family's raw
   material is disc-4-saturated, the physically integrated quantity may still escape into
   irregular/resurgent territory rather than landing as a finite disc-4 period. This is the
   sharpest test of whether the seam is deep (survives integration) or shallow (fiber-only), and
   it is not yet decided.
