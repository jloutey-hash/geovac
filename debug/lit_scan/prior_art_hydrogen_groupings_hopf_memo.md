## What we can build on

1. **The HG chiral grouping is the parabolic (uncoupled SU(2)×SU(2)) basis, regrouped.** At fixed n, the eigenspaces of (L_z − A_z)/2 are spanned by parabolic states |n1 n2 m⟩ with fixed m₋ = (m − (n1−n2))/2. The sign depends on convention. A_z here is the n-scaled Runge–Lenz component: Mardoyan's eigenvalue (n1−n2)/n multiplied by n.
   - The "exact Clebsch–Gordan occupations" in record HG are the squared Park/Tarter coefficients, Mardoyan eq. (2.2.25)/(2.2.29).
   - Any write-up should credit Park 1960 for the labeling and the coefficients, and present only the base-graph construction as new.
   - Eqs. (2.2.26)–(2.2.27) and the ₃F₂ form (2.2.29) can be reused directly as an independent second route for HG's coefficients. This is the independent-route cross-check memory rule.
2. **The half-Casimir identity is elementary.** With J± = (L ± A)/2 and J₊² = J₋² = (n²−1)/4, summing over the shell gives n²(n²−1)/4. The sum of L² over the shell is Σ_l (2l+1) l(l+1) = n²(n²−1)/2. The ratio of one half follows from tr(L²) = tr(A²) on the shell, equivalently tr(J₊·J₋) = 0. Present it as a check, not as a finding.
3. **Two-centre and spheroidal interbasis machinery.**
   - Mardoyan Ch. 2.3–2.7 gives the spheroidal hydrogen basis, which interpolates between spherical (R→0) and parabolic (R→∞) states, with three-term recurrences for the spheroidal↔spherical and spheroidal↔parabolic coefficients.
   - This is the natural object for door 11. The separation operator, a combination of l² and A_z, labels states once L_z is fixed.
4. **Manton 1987 is the precedent to cite** for "discrete Hopf bundle + base = fibre quotient + links inherited". His conclusions (no flat connection, a discrete Chern number) suggest a test for Paper 25's base graph: does a U(1) connection lifted from the label graph have quantized total flux? Morrison 1989 supplies the Yang–Mills action on the same lattice.

**Caveat for door 11 (needs a wording check):** "a second nucleus keeps only L_z" holds for the *Lie* commutant inside so(4). The two-centre problem nonetheless has a second conserved operator, the Erikson–Hill / Coulson–Joseph separation constant. That operator is quadratic in angular momentum and Runge–Lenz components, so it lies in the enveloping algebra and not in so(4). If the record or the atlas says "only L_z survives" without the word "Lie" (or "in so(4)"), the classical literature contradicts it. Door 24 has the same issue in a milder form: in the cavity analogue, accidental level crossings survive at special radii.

## Not found

- No prior construction of a *graph on hydrogenic labels (n,l,m)* quotiented by rotation multiplets or by chiral (J₋_z) eigenspaces. No prior spectrum of such a base graph was found: neither "one zero mode per l-block, integer at n_max=3" nor "connected, irrational" in the chiral grouping. These appear to be new (EXTENDS-OURS), subject to a deeper search of the spin-network and Gelfand–Tsetlin graph literature.
- No discrete Hopf map defined on the SO(4) harmonic-label lattice itself. The discrete Hopf constructions found are point lattices (Manton: 24-cell; Yershova: grids) or matrix truncations (the fuzzy-sphere line).
- I did not read the full texts of Park, Tarter, Englefield, Bander–Itzykson, Kibler, Biedenharn–Louck, Gilmore, Kalnins–Miller, Dowker, Madore or Jayewardena; none is cited above beyond what is stated.
- I found no source that states the finite-nucleus breaking in so(4)-subgroup language (J₊ + J₋ kept, both chiral factors broken). It is standard folklore (Fewster; Al-Hashimi–Wiese state it for L versus Runge–Lenz), but I have no explicit citation for the chiral phrasing.
