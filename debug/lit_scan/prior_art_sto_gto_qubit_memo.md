## What we can build on

1. **Lesiuk et al. 2015 is the credit Paper 62 is missing.** It states, for correlated work on a molecule (Be2), that STOs never did worse than equal-size GTOs. It also builds an STO family on the cc rule (even-tempered, CISD-optimized, layered core and diffuse functions) six years before Forster-Visscher. Paper 62's sentence "No matched-size correlated STO-against-GTO head-to-head on a molecule was found" should be softened: a *qualitative* claim exists; a quantitative, matched-size, matched-accuracy table on a polyatomic, with an SCF/correlation split, was not found. The Tucholska 2017 Mg table (equal size, correlated, STO much better) is a small-scale atomic precedent to cite next to it.
2. **Stark/Zimmerman 2025, Table 3, gives HBCI correlation energies for H2O and HF in even-tempered STO TZ/QZ/5Z sets.** That is an independent STO data point for both of Paper 62's molecules. Function counts are in their SI (not read). Caveats: their frozen-core convention, HBCI rather than CCSD(T), and their geometries would all have to be matched before any comparison.
3. **The qubit side is open.** No paper found compares Pauli counts or 1-norms between STO and GTO bases. Koridon 2021, Ollitrault 2025 and Deb-Prasannaa 2026 show that the field treats the 1-norm and accuracy-per-qubit as properties a basis can be designed for. Paper 62's matched-accuracy ratios (about 9x terms, about 2.7x 1-norm) answer a question they pose but do not ask about Slater functions. The shape-blind Pauli count should be stated with Koridon's caveat: it holds for dense canonical-orbital integrals, not after localization and thresholding, and not for diagonal bases (plane-wave dual, gausslets).

Also, Nemec et al. 2010 should be cited as the honest counterpoint: *stock* ADF STO sets need about 45% more functions than 6-311G at HF level. The STO advantage therefore depends on how the exponents are designed, not on the function type alone, which is the point of Paper 62's atom-optimized construction.

## Not found

- A quantitative matched-size or matched-accuracy correlated (MP2/CCSD(T)) table, STO against cc-pVXZ, for any polyatomic. Nothing found from ADF/SCM, SMILES, Hoggan, Bouferguene, Guseinov, Steinborn, Pinchon, Kurt or Weatherford. The searches were moderate and not exhaustive.
- Any split of the DZ-to-TZ (or STO-GTO) gap into SCF and correlated parts.
- Any qubit Pauli-count or 1-norm comparison between Slater (or Sturmian) bases and Gaussian bases, and any QC work in Sturmian or numerical-atomic-orbital bases with resource counts.
- An explicit published statement that the JW Pauli count is independent of orbital shape at fixed orbital number. Only the generic O(N^4) / binomial counting appears, and the plane-wave/gausslet results show the count *does* depend on structure.
- Full text of Persson-Taylor 1996.
