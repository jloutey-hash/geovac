# Sprint L4-reducibility — differential-Galois scan of the rank-4 Picard–Fuchs operator (2026-08-16)

**Frontier, thrust chosen by PI:** the *reducibility scan of `L₄`* — the decidable handle on
Paper 59's own stated-open closed-form problem for the one-mass slice `N(D)` (§obstruction /
§scope: "whether its fourth-order operator factors over the differential field generated by the
periods `K(ρ),K(1−ρ)` — a differential-Galois problem we leave open"). This is the `N(D)`-closed-form
leg of the frontier, distinct from (but "of a piece with", per §scope) the `T2` Γ(2)-MMV realization.

`L₄` is the Paper 59 eq:pf operator in the Laplace variable `D` (ρ = c₂/c₁ the scale ratio):

    D·ρ·L'''' + 2ρ·L''' + D(1−2ρ)·L'' + (1−2ρ)·L' − D(1−ρ)·L = 0 .

Symbol `(s²−1)(s²+ω²)`, `ω²=(1−ρ)/ρ`; irregular rank-1 singularity at D=∞ with exponentials
`e^{±D}, e^{±iωD}`; regular singular point at D=0.

## What was run, and the verdict

A complete **low-order factorization battery**, all NEGATIVE, plus two new structural facts
(self-adjointness; trivial eigenring). Every routine self-checked on a manufactured factorization
before use. Consolidated driver `debug/routeC_L4_reducibility.py` (runs the whole battery at
ρ=1/5 and ρ=1/3); permanent backing = `tests/test_routeC_momentum.py::test_L4_*` (6 tests).

1. **Local exponents at D=0 = {0, 1, 1, 2}** (indicial polynomial `ρ·s·(s−2)·(s−1)²`, symbolic).
   The DOUBLE exponent at s=1 is the local-monodromy *origin* of the `D ln D` nonanalyticity of
   `N(D)` that Paper 59 flags as a fingerprint — the paper MEASURED it; here it is DERIVED from the
   indicial equation (`N(0)` = the s=0 solution; the `D` and `D ln D` = the s=1 double root; `D²` = s=2).

2. **`L₄` is formally self-adjoint: `L₄* = L₄`** (exact, `L₄*−L₄ ≡ 0`). Consequences:
   - the differential Galois group `G ⊆ Sp₄(ℂ)` (preserves the Wronskian symplectic form) — the
     signature of a genuine *polarized* period, corroborating the "not elementary" reading;
   - order-1/2 **left** factors coincide with the right factors (so the order-3-right route is
     closed by the same searches; confirmed independently by running the searches on the adjoint).

3. **Symbol factors over ℚ(ρ) ONLY as `(s²−1)(s²+(1−ρ)/ρ)`** (the two Bessel sectors). No mixed
   quadratic split exists (it would need `β²=−(1−ρ)/ρ<0`). So the only candidate order-2
   factorizations are the K₀ sector and the J₀ sector.

4. **No order-1 right factor** — i.e. no hyperexponential solution `y=e^{λD}Q(D)` (`Q` polynomial)
   for `λ∈{±1,±iω,0}`, at ρ=1/5 (ω=2) and ρ=1/2 (ω=1). Because the D=0 exponents are INTEGERS,
   this is the **complete** order-1-factor test for polar locus {0,∞}. Also negative on the adjoint.

5. **No order-2 right factor over ℚ(ρ)(D)** with polar locus {0,∞} (general ρ, symbolic). Ansatz
   `a=am1/D+a0+a1·D`, `b=bm2/D²+bm1/D+b0+b1·D` (simple pole in a / double pole in b at 0, bounded
   at ∞ to match the symbol) → 0 solutions. **Positive control passed**: the identical pipeline
   recovers a *planted* factor `∂²+(1/D)∂−1` exactly, so "0 solutions" is a genuine negative, not a
   solver artifact. Also negative on the adjoint (= order-2 left factor of `L₄`).

6. **Not the Bessel-sector symmetric product** (products `K₀(D)J₀(ωD)` etc. as solutions): its
   local exponents are {0,0,0,0} ≠ {0,1,1,2}. **Not a Sym³** of a rank-2 operator (the pattern
   `{3a,2a+b,a+2b,3b}` is an arithmetic progression; {0,1,1,2} is not). The two natural
   elementary closed forms are excluded by monodromy — cleaner than the prior sprint's numerical
   "single Bessels aren't solutions" (which only ruled out single Bessels, not products).

## Verdict: `L₄` is IRREDUCIBLE over ℂ(D) (computed) — base-field reducibility CLOSED

The decidable battery below is the evidence trail; the **monodromy computation** (last section)
*closes* it: `L₄` is irreducible over ℂ(D), hence over ℚ(ρ)(D), apparent singularities included.

The low-order factorization battery over the natural polar locus {0,∞} is exhausted (no factor of
order 1, 2, or — via self-adjointness — 3, left or right), the two candidate symbol factorizations
are both dead, the two natural symmetric/tensor structures are dead, the operator is self-adjoint
(`G⊆Sp₄`), and the eigenring is trivial — then the monodromy in the exponential-line basis seals it.

**This is exactly the reducibility check Paper 59 asserted-without-checking.** The paper calls
`N(D)` "a period of the rank-four irregular connection" and "one transcendence level above the
sunrise" as an OBSERVATION — but a *reducible* rank-4 operator would secretly carry lower-rank
content, making "rank-four" vacuous. The scan supplies the missing evidence that the connection
does not reduce: the "rank-four" and "above the sunrise" claims are now backed, not bare.

## The eigenring collapses the residual to a single target

7. **`E(L₄) = ℂ` (scalars only): `L₄` is indecomposable.** The eigenring (endomorphisms of the
   solution space, `{R : ord R<4, LR≡0 mod L}`) is trivial — computed by rational-solution search,
   **robust across ansatz windows `[-2,2],[-3,3],[-4,4]` and both ρ=1/5, 1/3** (only the scalar
   `R=c·1` survives). So no direct-sum splitting.

Now combine self-adjointness + `E(L₄)=ℂ` + no-hyperexponential. The symplectic form pairs the
exponential solution `y_λ` (at D=∞) with `y_{−λ}` (the concomitant is D-constant only when
`λ+μ=0`). Then:

- A **non-degenerate** invariant subspace `W` would give `V=W⊕W^⊥` (both invariant, self-adjoint)
  → an idempotent in the eigenring → `dim E≥2`. Excluded. **Every invariant subspace is isotropic.**
- The exponential torus `T=(ℂ*)²` (exponents `±1,±iω` span a rank-2 lattice for generic ρ) forces
  invariant subspaces to be spans of the four exponential lines `{y_{±1},y_{±iω}}`. Their two
  **symplectic** pairs `{y_{+1},y_{−1}}` (K₀ sector) and `{y_{+iω},y_{−iω}}` (J₀ sector) are
  non-degenerate → not isotropic. The isotropic 2-planes are exactly the **four "mixed" planes**
  `span{y_{±1}, y_{±iω}}` (one real + one imaginary exponential).
- 1-dim isotropic = a hyperexponential solution — **already ruled out** (item 4).

**⇒ `L₄` is reducible over ℂ(D) if and only if one of the four mixed Lagrangian planes is
invariant — equivalently, iff the Stokes matrices at `D=∞` fail to couple the real exponential
pair `{±1}` to the imaginary pair `{±iω}`.** A single, razor-sharp target, replacing the vague
"differential-Galois question." (The two symplectic-pair planes — the two Bessel sectors — are the
`E≥2` decomposable case, already dead; the order-2 search dispatched them at polar locus {0,∞}.)

## The Stokes step closes it: `L₄` is irreducible (computed)

The single target — do the Stokes matrices couple the real pair `{±1}` to the imaginary pair
`{±iω}`? — is decidable, and we decided it by computing the **monodromy of `L₄` around `D=0`
directly in the exponential-line basis**. The reduction that makes it tractable: `ℙ¹` has only two
singular points (`0`, `∞`), so `M₀ = M_∞^{-1}`, and by Ramis `M_∞ = (formal monodromy)·(Stokes
product)`; both the exponential torus and the formal monodromy are diagonal in the exponential-line
basis, so **the Galois group's invariant coordinate subspaces are exactly the `M₀`-invariant ones**.

8. The four exponential lines are the four **Lefschetz-thimble** solutions
   `y_{x_k}(D)=∫ e^{-Dx}dx/√Q` from the four branch points `x_k∈{±1,±iω}`. The connection matrix
   `C` = their jets at a generic `D₀` (thimble integrator validated: the `x=1` thimble reproduces
   `N(D)` on the real axis, ratio 1.0). `M₀` = monodromy by RK4 of the companion system around
   `|D|=|D₀|`. Then `M₀` in the exponential-line basis is the **exact integer matrix**

   ```
   [-1  2  2  2]
   [-2  3  2  2]
   [-2  2  3  2]
   [ 2 -2 -2 -1]
   ```

   (clean to ~1e-10 — a numerical artifact can't produce integers; **identical for ρ=1/5 and
   ρ=1/3**, a topological invariant of the 4-branch-point configuration; robust across step counts
   and `D₀` sectors). It is **unipotent with a single 2×2 Jordan block** (`(M−I)²=0`, rank 1, char
   poly `(λ−1)⁴`) — exactly the one `D ln D` log — and has **NO invariant coordinate subspace of any
   dimension** (min off-block norm over all 14 proper coordinate subspaces = 2.0).

**⇒ `L₄` is IRREDUCIBLE over ℂ(D), hence over ℚ(ρ)(D).** The Stokes matrices *do* couple the real
and imaginary exponential pairs; no mixed Lagrangian plane survives; combined with the eigenring and
no-hyperexponential, `L₄` has no invariant subspace at all. **Monodromy is blind to apparent
singularities, so the apparent-singularity gap is closed too** — there is no factor of any kind.

**Consequence for the closed form.** `N(D)` is a period of an *irreducible* rank-4 connection: it has
**no closed form through any factorization of `L₄`**. The differential-Galois *reducibility* question
(Paper 59 §obstruction/§scope) is therefore **settled — negatively (irreducible)**, not merely
sharpened. The sole surviving open problem is a *transcendental* closed form at the irreducible
level — the elliptic-polylog / Γ(2) multiple-modular-value structure of §modular — which is the same
object as the `T2` MMV frontier (specialist/collaboration; Avery track HELD).

## Rigor: the irreducibility is theorem-backed (not just measured)

Upgraded during the "complete artifact" review. The irreducibility now has TWO backings, and the
paper (§obstruction) presents both:

- **Structural (rigorous, cited):** `N(D)` is a period of the **Fourier–Laplace transform** of the
  rank-1 connection `dx/√Q` (4 branch points, local monodromy −1 at each ⇒ FL generic rank = total
  drop = 4 = order of `L₄`). That connection is irreducible (rank 1), its minimal extension is a
  **simple** holonomic D-module, and **FL is an exact auto-equivalence of holonomic D-modules on 𝔸¹,
  so it preserves simplicity** (Katz, *Exponential Sums and Differential Equations*, Ann. Math.
  Studies 124, 1990 — verified). ⇒ `L₄` is irreducible over ℂ(D), apparent singularities included.
  This is a **proof**, not a measurement.
- **Explicit (concrete confirmation):** the exact-integer monodromy above — the mechanism made
  visible (the Stokes coupling of the real/imaginary exponential pairs), and an independent check of
  the theorem. Tagged `[MEASURED, exact-integer certificate]`.

So the paper's irreducibility claim is rigorous (theorem) + illustrated (computation). (The earlier
sympy apparent-singularity probe is moot — the D-module argument excludes apparent-singularity
factors structurally, and monodromy is blind to them anyway.)

## Field distinction (do not overclaim)

Base field ℚ(ρ)(D) [rational-in-D factors] ⊊ period field ℚ(ρ,K,K')(D). The scan is over the base
field. "No rational factor" ⇒ no *elementary/Bessel-composite* closed form with rational
coefficients; it does NOT by itself forbid a genuinely elliptic closed form built from the periods
(which is what an elliptic-dilogarithm-type answer would be — and that route was already obstructed
in §obstruction by the in-module argument, a DIFFERENT mechanism). The two negatives are
complementary: neither the rational route (this scan) nor the period-level ABW route (§obstruction)
closes `N(D)`.
