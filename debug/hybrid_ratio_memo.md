Date 2026-11-19. Derivation memo, one author, one pass, unreviewed. No paper, test, doc or git state touched.

`debug/hybrid_ratio_analyse.py` (order count), `debug/hybrid_ratio_check.py` (cross-check against the unpatched
evaluator), `debug/hybrid_ratio_summary.py` (label and root checks), `debug/hybrid_ratio_batch.sh`,

## 1. A correction to the earlier memo's function list

`debug/minorder_two_centre_memo.md` (class B) lists the E-atoms on the + side as e^{a_d R}E1((a_p + a_d)R) and
e^{a_d R}E1((a_p + a_c + a_d)R). The first is a typo: it is **e^{a_d R}E1((a_c + a_d)R)**. Fam1 has a_p = a_c so it
cannot tell them apart; fam2 at a_d = 1/2 (a_p = 2, a_c = 1) shows E1(3R/2) = E1((a_c + a_d)R) in the memo's own scan
data, and every parametric form here has it. The corrected list, at a generic ratio:

  exponentials  e^{-a_d R}, e^{-a_c R}, e^{-(a_p + a_c)R}
  E-atoms       e^{-a_d R}Ei((a_d - a_c)R), e^{-a_d R}Ei((a_d - a_p - a_c)R),
                e^{a_d R}E1((a_d + a_c)R),  e^{a_d R}E1((a_d + a_p + a_c)R)

Seven atoms, closed under d/dR (each E-atom's derivative lands on e^{-a_c R} or e^{-(a_p + a_c)R}). Rates of f:
-a_d, -a_c, -(a_p + a_c), +a_d (four). The constants are logs of rate ratios (e.g. ln u, ln(3u + 2), ln(4u + 3) on
fam1's middle region) and, at a coincidence, Euler gamma.

## 2. The count that gives both bounds at once

W = the free C(R)-module on the seven atoms (at a coincidence: on that point's atom list). By Lemma GI (earlier memo,
reviewed SOUND WITH CAVEATS) the atoms are independent over C(R), so the order of f equals dim_{C(R)} of the cyclic
d/dR-module generated by f inside W. W_exp, spanned by the exponential atoms, is a d/dR-submodule.

**Proposition (quotient count).** order(f) = d_E + k, where
* d_E = sum over E-shifts c (the exponential in front of an E-atom: here c = -a_d and c = +a_d) of
  dim_C span{q_j : q_j the coefficient of an E-atom with shift c};
* k = number of exponential rates with a nonzero coefficient in L f, where L is an operator of order d_E whose
  kernel is span{q_j e^{c_j R}}.

Proof. dim module(f) = dim pi(module(f)) + dim(module(f) cap W_exp), pi: W -> W/W_exp.
(i) W/W_exp is a direct sum of rank-one modules C(R)e^{cR}, one per E-atom, d/dR acting as on e^{cR}. Lines with
different c are non-isomorphic (e^{(c - c')R} is not rational), so the cyclic module of pi(f) splits by c, and in the
c-component it is the module generated by the vector (q_j) under d/dR, whose dimension is the rank of the Wronskian
matrix of the q_j, i.e. dim_C span{q_j}. Sum: d_E.
(ii) module(f) cap W_exp = {P f : P in Ann(pi f)}. C(R)[d/dR] is a left principal ideal domain, so Ann(pi f) = C(R)[d/dR] L
with L of order d_E; any order-d_E operator killing every q_j e^{c_j R} is that generator (it kills pi f, because
L(q e^{cR} y) = L(q e^{cR}) y + exponential terms). So the intersection is module(L f), and L f is an exponential
polynomial; its module dimension is its number of rates with nonzero coefficient (Theorem A). QED.

Here d_E = 2 with q_- := coefficient on the c = -a_d atoms and q_+ on c = +a_d; then L is the Wronskian operator
A2 y'' + A1 y' + A0 y of {q_- e^{-a_d R}, q_+ e^{a_d R}}; the exponentials cancel in the Wronskians, so A0, A1, A2 are
rational in R (computed exactly). Lower and upper bound come out of the same identity: no separate minor and generic
rank are needed.

## 3. Obtaining the closed forms with the exponent ratio as a symbol

`hybrid_ratio_engine.py` runs geovac's own `hybrid_closed_form_shell` algebra (`hybrid_terms`,
`shell_inner_double`, `_integrate_with_e1`, `e1_moment`, `e1_moment_shifted`, `finite_power_exp`, called in the
same order; `shell_parts` is a line-for-line copy of `hybrid_closed_form_shell` that returns the summands instead of
expanding their sum) with three substitutions:

1. `radial_poly`, `radial_norm`, `radial_product` accept sympy Z; the per-orbital normalisation is dropped (a
   nonzero constant factor, irrelevant to the order).
2. sympy's `expand` multiplies out denominators (c/(2u+1)^2 e^{-..} becomes c/(4u^2 e^{..} + ...)), which the
   evaluator's term separator rejects; a proxy re-factors any expanded denominator. Same function, other spelling.
3. Every exponent region is swept by one positive symbol u, chosen so that every rate combination the algebra meets is
   +-(positive combination of 1 and u). By the scaling f(R; sZ_A, sZ_B) = s f(sR; Z_A, Z_B) only a_d/a_c matters:

   | region | parametrisation | ratio a_d/a_c covered |
   |---|---|---|
   | lt | a_d = 1, a_c = 1 + u | (0, 1) |
   | mid | a_p = 1 + u, a_c = (a_c/a_p)(1 + u), a_d = a_c + u | (1, 1 + a_p/a_c) |
   | gt | a_c = 1, a_d = 1 + a_p/a_c + u | (1 + a_p/a_c, oo) |
   | eq1, eq2 | a_d = a_c; a_d = a_c + a_p | the two coincidences |

   Each map u -> ratio is a bijection onto the region, so u in (0, oo) is every exponent choice there.

Why the symbolic result is the true integral at every u > 0. The evaluator's branch decisions (`is_negative`,
`> 0`, `== 0`) were taken by sympy's assumption system on expressions in a positive symbol, so each holds for all
u > 0; specialising u commutes with the field operations wherever no denominator vanishes. The collected coefficients
have u-denominators only among u, u + 1, 2u + 1, 3u + 2, ... (all with no root u > 0; checked per run by
`hybrid_ratio_summary.py`), so the form is continuous on the region and equals the integral at generic u, hence
everywhere (the integral is continuous in the exponents). A hidden coincidence inside a region (an intermediate
1/(rate difference) the engine never branched on) would show up as such a root; none does.

**Independent check.** `hybrid_ratio_check.py` (fresh process, geovac UNPATCHED, normalised orbitals) compares
the parametric atom form at 27 (case, region, u) points against `hybrid_closed_form` at three R: the ratio is one
constant (the normalisation) with relative spread at most 4.5e-33 (40-digit arithmetic). Points include both
coincidences for all four cases.

The collector (`hybrid_ratio_rep.py`) walks the unexpanded expression into exact Q(u, R) coefficients per atom and
constant monomial (pi, gamma, logs split into logs of irreducible factors, prime surds), refusing products of two
E-atoms; sympy's expand + cancel route was abandoned after a 20-minute timeout on fam1.

## 4. Results

Every run (20 of 20 in the four cases) gives d_E = 2, and:

**E-part.** At each shift the two coefficients are equal and opposite, and the two shifts are mirror images:

  E-part = q(R) e^{-a_d R}[Ei((a_d - a_p - a_c)R) - Ei((a_d - a_c)R)]
           + eps q(-R) e^{a_d R}[E1((a_d + a_p + a_c)R) - E1((a_d + a_c)R)],   eps = +-1 per case

in the 12 open-region runs (`ratio_to_first` = -1 at each shift; q+(R)/q-(-R) = -1 for fam1 and H2, +1 for H4 and
H7). At the coincidences one atom of the -a_d pair is ln R, again with a coefficient proportional to q. q is a
polynomial in R divided by a power of R, coefficients in Q(u): fam1 middle region,

  q = -72 (24u^5 + 52u^4 + 42u^3 + 15u^2 + 2u) R^3 + ... + (21u^2 + 6u - 6)) / (R^3 (u+1)^6 (2u+1)^6).

The gcd of q's R-coefficients is a nonzero rational in every run (72 here): q vanishes identically in R for no u > 0.
q is free of gamma and of every log. Proportionality within each shift and q(R) vs q(-R) give d_E = 2 exactly; the
kernel of L is {q(R)e^{-a_d R}, q(-R)e^{a_d R}}, so L is even under R -> -R. (A reading, not used: the kernel
looks like the decaying/growing pair of a radial Helmholtz-type operator at the B-orbital's rate.)

**L f.** In all twelve open-region runs L f has exactly three rates, {-a_d, -a_c, -(a_p + a_c)}, i.e. every
exponential rate of f survives. For each, the gcd over Q[u] of the numerator's coefficients (with respect to R and to
the constants) is a nonzero rational (e.g. fam1 middle: 497664, 62208, 497664; H2 middle: 107495424, 53747712,
214990848), and no constant (gamma, ln) survives into L f. So k = 3 for every u > 0, with no assumption on the
arithmetic of the logs: the logs of rate ratios sit only on the exponentials that L annihilates. Order 2 + 3 = 5.

**Coincidences.** At a_d = a_c the shift -a_d carries Ei((a_d - a_p - a_c)R) and ln R (the E1 collapses), still
proportional, d_E = 2; the rate -a_d merges with -a_c, L f has two rates, order 4. At a_d = a_c + a_p the shift -a_d
carries ln R and Ei(a_p R); -a_d merges with -(a_p + a_c); order 4. Gamma is present in f at both points and absent
from q and L f. Confirmed for all four cases (eq1, eq2 rows of `hybrid_ratio_results.json`).

**Labels.** `hybrid_ratio_summary.py` checks for every open-region run that no two rates (of f, of the closure, of
L f) coincide, no two E-labels at one shift coincide, and no Ei argument vanishes, for any u > 0; and that every
u-factor of every denominator and zero-gcd has no positive root. Issues found: none, in 20 runs. So GI's
distinct-label hypothesis holds at every exponent choice, and the exceptional set inside each region is empty.

**Exceptional set, stated.** {a_d = a_c} and {a_d = a_c + a_p}; each lowers the order by exactly one; there is no
other. In particular the ratios a_d = a_p (inside fam2's and H4's middle region at u = 1) and every point the earlier
scan sampled are ordinary.

## 5. What this does and does not settle against the paper sentence

Paper 58 sec:census (as the task quotes it): "hybrids carry E1, with Ei when a_d > a_c, gamma + ln R when a_d = a_c,
and both at the second coincidence a_d = a_p + a_c; on the panel and scan points the order is the number of distinct
rates plus one; least order proved only at the exponents computed."

* For (2p0 2p0|1s 1s_B), (2p1 2p1|2p1 2p1_B), (2p0 2p0|2p0 3d0_B), (2p0 3d0|2s 1s_B): "order = rates + 1" is now a
  theorem at every exponent choice (both bounds), conditional on Lemma GI and on the evaluator's algebra (Sec. 6).
* The function-content clause is consistent with every run, with one precision: Ei is present for a_d > a_c, and
  for a_d < a_c the same atoms appear as E1 (Ei of a negative argument); nothing else changes across the regions.
* For H1, H3, H5, H6, H8, H10 the "proved only at the exponents computed" clause still applies.

## 6. Where this could be wrong

1. **The evaluator.** Everything is conditional on `hybrid_closed_form_shell`'s algebra being right for symbolic
   rates. I did not change it, but sympy's branch decisions on symbolic expressions are a new path. Mitigation: the
   27-point agreement with the unpatched evaluator to 1e-33, at points in all regions and at both coincidences, and
   the unpatched evaluator's own quadrature tests. A wrong branch would have to agree at every checked u, which a
   region-wide wrong sign would not.
2. **Continuity argument.** It needs every u-denominator of the final coefficients to have no root u > 0, which is
   checked. An intermediate denominator that cancels is harmless by continuity of the integral in the exponents.
3. **Lemma GI** (hand proof, reviewed once) and the Proposition (hand proof, unreviewed). The Proposition's step (i)
   uses that the cyclic submodule of a semisimple module with pairwise non-isomorphic isotypic parts splits along
   them; step (ii) uses that C(R)[d/dR] is a left PID. Both standard; a reviewer should check (i) for the c-components
   with two E-atoms each.
4. **Zero tests.** "Nonzero for all u > 0" rests on the gcd of the numerator's coefficients being a nonzero rational,
   computed in exact rational-function arithmetic (sympy sparse fields). A wrong cancellation in the collector would
   matter; the collector is new code (exact, but unreviewed). Its output is what the 27-point check evaluates, so a
   collector error in a coefficient would have shown as R-dependent ratio spread.
5. **Normalisation dropped.** A per-orbital constant, nonzero for Z > 0. It cannot change the order.
6. **Scaling to "every exponent choice".** Uses f(R; sZ) = s f(sR; Z) for hydrogenic orbitals with all charges
   scaled; the region maps are bijections of u > 0 onto ratio intervals; each case's Z_A and Z_B are free.
7. **Heavy cases not done.** The parametric build for d-pairs (L up to 4) exceeded the 20-minute cap (Sec. 7). The obstruction is cost,
   not mathematics: the same pipeline applies unchanged; Sec. 7.

## 8. What a guard should reject (for a later, separately reviewed guard)

* order 4 claimed at a generic ratio, or 5 at a coincidence (the Proposition's count must give 5 / 4);
* d_E computed as 4 (treating the two atoms at one shift as independent lines): would give order 7;
* a log constant treated as surviving into L f (it must not, at any region);
* the memo's E1((a_p + a_d)R) label: on fam2 it is not in the closure; a guard building the atom list from that
  formula must fail against the closed form.
