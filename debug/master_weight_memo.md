# Weight of Pachucki's two-centre master integral f0(R; a, b) at the exchange point

Date 2026-11-19. Derivation memo; no paper, test, record or git state touched.
Script: `debug/master_weight_closed_form.py`. Data: `debug/data/master_weight_closed_form.json` (dps 60, 218 s).

**Verdict: GO.** Explicit closed form, verified against the helper's quadrature to 47-60 digits at three R and
three (a, b). Weight statement: f0 is a two-fold iterated integral (upper bound, by construction) and is NOT of
weight one: (i) **elementary proof** that f0 is no C(R)-linear combination of e^{wR}, e^{wR} ln R,
e^{wR} Ei(nu R) for ANY finite set of complex rates w, nu (extends the exact9 lemma, which covered the nine
functions only); (ii) **proof sketch resting on standard differential algebra** (Kolchin-Ostrowski; Risch's
structure lemmas) that f0 is not even algebraic over the field generated by such functions together with logs
of exponential-rational functions, nor expressible with dilogarithms Li2(u) of exponential-rational arguments u.
So no dilogarithm form exists; f0 is a genuinely new weight-two iterated integral. Unreviewed, one author.

## 1. Setting (label 60, transcription checked)

u = a < w = b, c = 1/(2(a^2 - b^2)) < 0, LC = ln((a+b)/(b-a)). Label 60 (arXiv:0905.1635, source opened) is

  FM1(s) = c [ sum_i k_i e^{lam_i s} Ei(mu_i s) + LC (e^{-2bs} - e^{-2as}) ],

| i | k_i | lam_i | mu_i |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +1 | 2b | -2(a+b) |
| 2 | -1 | 2a | -2(a+b) |
| 3 | -1 | -2b | 2(b-a) |
| 4 | +2 | -2b | b-a |
| 5 | +1 | -2a | 2(a-b) |
| 6 | -2 | -2a | a-b |

and f0(R) = int_R^oo FM1(s) ds/s (label 59). This table equals `Calc.FM1` of `tests/_pachucki_extension.py`
exactly at (a, b) = (1, 2), (1, 3/2), (1, 3) (check 0). Every lam_i + mu_i < 0 (decay).

## 2. Closed form (Question 1)

Define the entire functions

  Ein(x) = sum_{k>=1} x^k/(k k!),  E2(x) = sum_{k>=1} x^k/(k^2 k!),
  Phi(x, y) = sum_{j>=0, k>=1} x^j y^k / (j! k k! (j+k)) = int_0^1 e^{xu} Ein(yu) du/u.

Ein(x) = Ei(x) - gamma - ln|x| is weight one. E2 is formally an iterated integral (E2(x)' = Ein(x)/x), but in
the assembled f0 it only appears together with the ln R Ein and Ein pieces of the same T. Phi is the new object:
d/dR Phi(lam R, mu R) = e^{lam R} Ein(mu R)/R, a two-fold iterated integral of the kernels e^{lam s} ds/s and
e^{mu u} du/u (a Kampe de Feriet-type double series on the ray (lam R, mu R)).

**Per-term formula.** For lam + mu < 0, g = gamma + ln|mu|,

  T(lam, mu; R) := int_R^oo e^{lam s} Ei(mu s) ds/s
     = K(lam, mu) - g ln R - (1/2) ln^2 R - g Ein(lam R) - ln R Ein(lam R) + E2(lam R) - Phi(lam R, mu R).

Proof: differentiate (each piece termwise; total = -e^{lam R}(g + ln R + Ein(mu R))/R = -e^{lam R}Ei(mu R)/R),
and the constant is fixed by R -> 0. K(lam, mu) is the finite part at eps = 0 of the Mellin transform
int_0^oo s^{eps-1} e^{lam s} Ei(mu s) ds (the ln R, ln^2 R subtractions match the eps^-1, eps^-2 poles exactly).
From int_0^oo s^{eps-1} e^{-ps} E1(qs) ds = Gamma(eps) int_1^oo (p + qt)^{-eps} dt/t
= 1/eps^2 - (gamma + ln q)/eps + [(gamma + ln q)^2/2 + pi^2/12 + Li2(-p/q)] + O(eps), (p + q > 0):

  mu < 0 (q = -mu, p = -lam):  K = -[(gamma + ln q)^2/2 + pi^2/12 + Li2(-p/q)],
  mu > 0 (p = -lam > mu):      K = -[(gamma + ln mu)^2/2 - 5 pi^2/12 + Re Li2(p/mu)]   (continuation q -> -mu).

Verified against direct quadrature for 8 (lam, mu) pairs (both signs of mu, including the b = 2a coincidence
lam = mu = -2) at R = 0.4, 1.7: |diff| <= 4e-57 (check 1).

**Assembled.** Summing with the k_i, the LC terms giving c LC [E1(2bR) - E1(2aR)]:

  f0(R) = -Lam(R) ln R + W1(R) + W2(R),

  Lam(R) = 2c [Cinh(2bR) - Cinh(2aR)],  Cinh(x) = sum x^{2k}/(2k (2k)!)          (exact9 memo's A_1 = -Lam),
  W1(R)  = c sum_i k_i [ E2(lam_i R) - (gamma + ln|mu_i|) Ein(lam_i R) ] + c LC [Ein(-2aR) - Ein(-2bR)],
  W2(R)  = -c sum_i k_i Phi(lam_i R, mu_i R).

(Naming caution: E2(x) = Phi(0, x) is itself an iterated integral, and the E2 sum is the partner of -Lam ln R:
d/dR[-Lam ln R + c sum k_i E2(lam_i R)] = -L ln R / R. The genuinely weight-one pieces are the Ein and LC terms;
-Lam ln R is a product of weight-one functions. Theorems A/B below do not use this split.)

The ln^2 R terms cancel (sum k_i = 0), the constant ln R terms cancel (sum k_i ln|mu_i| = 0), and **the
constant C(a, b) = c [sum_i k_i K(lam_i, mu_i) + LC ln(a/b)] vanishes**: numerically 5e-62 and 4e-61 at
(1, 2), (1, 3/2) (the residue of 60-digit arithmetic). This is forced: f(r) of label 58 carries the factor r, so
f0(0) = 0. It is a dilogarithm identity among the six Li2 constants (not derived by hand here; observed to 60
digits at two exponent pairs). Consequence: **Li2 does not appear in f0 at all**; the only constants left are
gamma and ln of exponent ratios inside W1.

Equivalently f0 is the explicit iterated integral
f0(R) = c sum_i k_i T(lam_i, mu_i; R) + c LC [E1(2bR) - E1(2aR)], with T as above.

**Numerical verification vs the helper (`f0_quadrature`, an independent mpmath quadrature of label 59), dps 60:**

| (a, b) | R = 0.3 | R = 1.4 | R = 3.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1, 2) | rel 3e-60 | rel 6e-57 | rel 4e-48 |

(The R = 3.1 loss is cancellation in the entire power series, size e^{4bR}; still >= 46 digits.) The split

(-0.128 and -21.7 there, against f0 ~ 0.030 and ~ 0.003, from the JSON parts): the Phi part
is a large component that cancels against the rest, not a correction.

The independent Neumann route (`neumann_integral('f0', ...)`) was not re-run here; the existing tests compare
f0 with it (exact9 memo, caveat 1).

## 3. Asymptotics (Question 3)

* **R -> 0:** f0 = -Lam ln R + W1 + W2 with Lam = -R^2 - (a^2+b^2) R^4/3 + ..., (Lam = sum l_n R^n/n,
  l_2 = 4c(b^2 - a^2)... = -2 gives -R^2), and W1, W2 = O(R). So f0(0) = 0, f0 = m R + R^2 ln R + O(R^2): the
  ln R coefficient is exactly -Lam (identity of the closed form; also matches the exact9 memo's contour check C2).
  Check: helper quadrature at R = 1e-12 gives 4.62e-13 at (1, 2) and 5.55e-13 at (1, 3/2), i.e. linear, f0(0) = 0.
* **R -> oo:** from Ei(x) ~ e^x/x, FM1(s) = -c e^{-2as}[LC + b/((b^2-a^2) s) + O(s^-2)] + O(e^{-(a+b)s}/s), so

    f0(R) ~ LC e^{-2aR} / (4a (b^2 - a^2) R) * (1 + O(1/R)).

  Check at (1, 2): (f0/lead - 1) R = 0.117, 0.112, 0.109, 0.108 at R = 10, 20, 40, 80 (converging to a constant:
  the O(1/R) correction, leading term confirmed). Decay rate 2a, as in the exact9 memo.

## 4. Weight (Question 2)

Upper bound: f0 is by construction a two-fold iterated integral (Section 2) of kernels in
{e^{lam s} ds/s} -- weight <= 2 in Paper 58's sense.

**Theorem A (elementary, full proof).** For every real 0 < a < b, f0 is not in the C(R)-linear span of
{e^{wR}, e^{wR} ln R, e^{wR} Ei(nu R) : w in S, nu in N} for any finite sets S, N of complex numbers.

Proof. Suppose f0 = sum r_w e^{wR} + sum s_w e^{wR} ln R + sum r_{w,nu} e^{wR} Ei(nu R), r, s in C(R). Near
R = 0, Ei(nu R) = ln R + (entire) (the constant ln nu is absorbed), so the right side is P ln R + Q with
P = sum_w rho_w e^{wR}, rho_w in C(R), and P, Q meromorphic on C. The left side is -Lam ln R + (entire)
(Section 2). By (F3) of the exact9 memo (ln R is not meromorphic on a slit-plane continuation), -Lam = P.
Differentiate: Lam' = L/R with L = c(e^{2bR} - e^{2aR} + e^{-2bR} - e^{-2aR}). Exponentials with distinct
complex rates are linearly independent over C(R) (standard; e.g. a minimal relation differentiated against
itself). Compare the coefficient of e^{2bR}: if 2b is not in S, c/R = 0, impossible; otherwise
rho' + 2b rho = -c/R with rho in C(R), which has no solution (a pole of order m >= 1 of rho gives order m + 1 on
the left; a polynomial rho gives a polynomial left side). QED. (The exact9 lemma is the case S, N = the nine's.)

**Theorem B (proof sketch; rests on standard differential algebra, not written to full rigor).** Let
F0 = C(R)(e^{w R} : w in S) (finite S containing 2a, 2b, a+b and every nu in N), and
F1 = F0(ln R, Ei(nu R) for nu in N, ln v_1, ..., ln v_m) with v_j in F0. Then f0 is not algebraic over F1, and
f0 is not in F1(Li2(u_1), ..., Li2(u_n)) for any u_k in F0 with ln(1 - u_k) adjoined to F1.

Sketch. (1) The primitives theta_nu = Ei(nu R), theta_0 = ln R are algebraically independent over F0 together
with the log-type ones after discarding redundancies: by Kolchin-Ostrowski a dependence forces a constant
combination into F0; its derivative sum c_nu e^{nu R}/R + (log derivative) = q' with q in F0, and Risch's
structure for hyperexponential monomials (q is a Laurent polynomial in the exponentials up to normal poles,
which q' cannot cancel at simple order) leaves q_nu' + nu q_nu = c_nu/R, insoluble in C(R) unless c_nu = 0;
log-type primitives reduce to ln R plus linear terms. (2) f0' = -FM1/R is linear in the theta_nu with F0
coefficients. If f0 is algebraic over F1 then (trace argument) f0 in F1, and by the polynomial lemma for
primitive extensions f0 = sum_{mu,nu} C_{mu nu} theta_mu theta_nu / 2 + sum p_nu theta_nu + q, C constant and
symmetric, p_nu, q in F0 (log-type theta included). Matching the coefficient of theta_nu in f0' gives
  sum_mu C_{mu nu} theta_mu' + p_nu' = [coefficient of e^{lam R} theta_nu / R in -FM1/R] summed over lam.
(3) Take nu = -2(a+b) (term 1, lam = 2b). The e^{2bR} component forces p = 0 and C_{2b, nu} = -c != 0 (so Ei(2bR)
must be present; that is the shuffle e^{2bR}Ei(nu R)/R = theta_{2b}' theta_nu). Now the theta_{2b} equation: its
right side is 0 because FM1 contains no e^{-2(a+b)R} Ei(2bR) term (lam = -2(a+b) is never among
{+-2a, +-2b}), while its e^{-2(a+b)R} component reads -c/R + p' - 2(a+b) p = 0, insoluble in C(R). Contradiction.
Log-type theta' contribute no e^{w R}/R components with w != 0 (step 1). (4) Dilogarithms: Li2(u)' =
-ln(1-u) u'/u contains no Ei-type primitive; with the Li2(u_k) independent over F1 (Kolchin-Ostrowski again)
and f0' in F1, f0 = sum c_k Li2(u_k) + h, h in F1, and the Ei-coefficient equations for h are those of step 3
unchanged. Contradiction.

The term-1 key and the absence of its swap hold for every 0 < a < b. Swapped (here: self-swapped) keys do occur
at special ratios -- term 5 at b = 2a (lam = mu = -2a) and term 6 at b = 3a (lam = mu = -2a); there
e^{-2aR}Ei(-2aR)/R = (Ei(-2aR)^2/2)' is a shuffle product and that term IS reducible (the script finds exactly
{(-2, -2)} at (1, 2) and (1, 3), none at (1, 3/2)). Term 1 is never affected, so the contradiction stands.

What is proved vs observed:
* PROVED (elementary): Theorem A, so "weight one in the linear sense" is excluded for every rate set.
* PROVED modulo cited standard theorems, unreviewed: Theorem B (no polynomial/algebraic expression in weight-one
  functions, no Li2-of-exponentials expression). References for the tools are from memory: Kolchin, Amer. J.
  Math. 90 (1968) 1151 (Ostrowski's theorem on algebraic dependence of primitives); Bronstein, Symbolic
  Integration I (Springer), chapters on primitive and hyperexponential monomials -- chapter/lemma numbers
  unverified. Rosenlicht-style trace argument for "algebraic => in the field".
* VERIFIED numerically: the closed form (>= 46 digits), C(a, b) = 0 (60 digits, two pairs), asymptotics.
* NOT shown: that Phi(lam R, mu R) cannot be rewritten through Li2 of arguments involving Ei, or through
  polylogarithm-type functions outside F1(Li2(F0)); "genuinely new" means relative to that class.

## 5. The exchange integral (Question 4)

* **a < b: the integral is weight two.** K = c_1 f0 + (combination of g_2..g_9, all in F1). Rank 9 at every
  real t = b/a > 1 (exact9 memo, Section 4) forces c_1 != 0 (the span of g_2..g_9 is closed under d/dR, so
  c_1 = 0 would cap the rank at 8). Since c_1 in Q(t)(R) is a nonzero rational function, K in the span (or the
  field) of Theorem A (B) would put f0 there. So the 1s exchange integral and the four sigma = 0 integrals with
  l > 0 are of weight exactly two at every 0 < a < b (Theorem A level fully proved; Theorem B level per its
  sketch).
* **a -> b: the weight-two part cancels.** Already established symbolically (`debug/pachucki_extension_memo.md`,
  `pachucki_ext_limit.py`): every eps-coefficient I_k of f0(R; 1, 1 + eps) reduces to the four-function class
  and contains none of the three weight-two integrals J1..J3; the eps^0 term of K equals Sugiura's formula
  exactly. The closed form shows why: c ~ -1/(4a eps) multiplies W2, but terms 1 and 2 have the same mu and
  lam_1 - lam_2 = 2(b-a), so k_1 Phi(lam_1 R, mu R) + k_2 Phi(lam_2 R, mu R) = 2(b-a) R d_x Phi + O(eps^2), and
  d_x Phi(x, y) = int_0^1 e^{xu} Ein(yu) du is weight one (one integration by parts); terms 3-4 and 5-6 have
  mu = O(eps), and Phi(x, 2y) - 2 Phi(x, y) = O(y^2) = O(eps^2). So c W2 tends to a weight-one function: the
  weight-two content is a collision effect of the six (lam, mu) keys at a = b, consistent with Sugiura. Not
  re-run numerically here (the existing symbolic result is stronger).

## 6. Literature

* **Pachucki, PRA 80, 032520 (2009), arXiv:0905.1635 -- opened (source).** Labels 58-60 give f at the exchange
  point only as the one-dimensional integral int_r^oo F^(-1)(r')/r' dr' with F^(-1) in Ei; no closed form for
  u != w is given. A dilogarithm appears in his paper only in the three-body constant G(0,1,1;1,1,1) (label 10),
  not in f. So the closed form above is not his; Li2 constants appear termwise and cancel (C = 0).
* **Lesiuk & Moszynski, arXiv:1410.6113 (Paper II, exchange) -- source opened, searched.** Per-Neumann-term
  closed forms in E1, gamma, logs; no dilogarithm found by search. Consistent with Paper 58's per-term weight
  one; they do not sum the Neumann series in closed form.
* **Ruedenberg, J. Chem. Phys. 19, 1459 (1951)** (citation as given in Lesiuk-Moszynski's bibliography); not
  opened. **Harris; Maslen & Trefry**: not opened. All UNVERIFIED for whether a dilogarithm or this iterated
  integral appears. Given the per-term structure, any summed closed form in that literature would have to carry
  the Phi-type function of Theorem B; I found none.

## 7. Where this could be wrong

1. Theorem B is a sketch. The weakest joints: the multi-primitive polynomial lemma (stated from Risch theory,
   applied with several primitives at once), and the handling of log-type primitives ln v with v in F0 having
   non-monomial factors. Theorem A does not depend on them.
2. "Weight" here means iterated-integral depth over the kernels e^{lam s} ds/s; Paper 58 uses "weight one" for
   its seed sets {exp, E1, ln, gamma}. Theorem A is exactly the negation of "a combination of these seeds
   (any rates) with rational coefficients", which is what the corrected Paper 58 sentence needs.
3. C(a, b) = 0 is observed (60 digits at two pairs), not derived as a dilogarithm identity; it is however
   implied by f(0) = 0 from label 58's factor r together with the verified closed form.
