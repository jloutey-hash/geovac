## 2. Leg (A): the CM/Hodge structure is ABSENT on the Hardy sector

The HO phase circle is `U(θ) = e^{iθ(N̂+3/2)}` — the U(1) generated by the Euler operator (P24
`eq:euler`), with the physical generator `N̂ + 3/2` (P24 `eq:H-bargmann`; graph diagonal
`Fraction(2N+3,2)`). It acts on the shell `H_N` (SU(3) irrep `(N,0)`, dim `(N+1)(N+2)/2`) by a **scalar**.

**Positives first (so the negative is not overstated).** The circle *is* compact and *does* carry a μ₄:

- `U(2π) = −1` on every shell, `U(4π) = +1`: the 3/2 zero-point makes the flow a genuine **double cover**
  (period 4π) — the metaplectic `Mp(6,ℝ) → Sp(6,ℝ)` lift, the HO's own double cover, *with no spin*;
- `U(π) = −i·(−1)^N` has **order 4**: the substrate's order-2 symmetry (parity = the bipartition of the
  Bargmann graph, verified below) acts through an **order-4 lift**. That is exactly the "order-2 physical
  symmetry acting through its order-4 lift" shape the seam scan named as its defended survivor — realised
  here without spin.

**Three independent obstructions to the Hodge/CM leg** (each exact, each sufficient on its own):

- **(iv) the traceless lemma.** If a 2-dim block of the flow is conjugate to a Hodge circle
  `cos(at)I + sin(at)J` with `J²=−I`, then comparing determinants — `det e^{itK} = e^{it(a₁+a₂)}` vs
  `det(cos·I + sin·J) = 1` — forces `a₁ + a₂ = 0`. The generator must be **traceless**, i.e. its spectrum
  must contain a ±pair. The Hardy generator is `N + 3/2 ∈ {3/2, 5/2, …}` — **strictly positive**; the bare
  Euler operator is non-negative. No 2-dim block is traceless, at any cutoff, either way.
- **(vii) ℚ-rationality on the substrate's OWN ℚ-form.** The Bargmann substrate's ℚ-form is the ℚ-span of
  the `(N,l,m)` nodes — every graph entry rational (P24 `thm:pi-free`, re-certified in the run:
  56 nodes / 165 edges at `N_max=5`). On that ℚ-form the flow's quarter point acts on shell `N` by `i^N`:
  ℚ-rational exactly on **even** shells, where it squares to `+1`; squares to `−1` exactly on **odd**
  shells, where it is **not** ℚ-rational. No ℚ-rational `J` with `J²=−1` arises as the quarter point.
  (S³ Dirac: `J` is an *integer matrix* **and** is the quarter point — both at once. That is the P56 content.)
- **(viii) the missing conjugate half.** A polarized weight-1 Hodge structure needs the conjugate pair
  `V^{1,0} ⊕ V^{0,1}`; each Hardy shell carries a single weight. Doubling by the antiholomorphic half
  `(N,0) ⊕ (0,N)` restores it — verified exactly: the doubled weight multiset *is* closed under negation
  (N = 1,2,3), and the flow weights become `±(N+3/2)`, lifting obstruction (iv). But `(0,N)` is precisely
  the antiholomorphic half the Hardy/Bargmann construction discards by definition ("the holomorphic sector
  of S⁵", P24 `sec:bs`). **The S³ Dirac's ±(n+3/2) spectrum is the already-doubled case: chirality supplies
  the conjugate half for free.**

Supporting (weaker, but the cleanest representation-theoretic statement):

- **Frobenius–Schur.** The angular Kramers-type pairing `(Jψ)_{j,m} = (−1)^{j−m}ψ_{j,−m}` satisfies
  `J² = (−1)^{2j}` (verified j = ½, 1, 3/2, 2). The Hardy tower carries only **integer** `l`
  (`l = N, N−2, …`), so every such `J` squares to `+I`: real/orthogonal type, no complex structure.
  At the SU(3) level, `(N,0)` for `N ≥ 1` is **complex** type (FS = 0) — verified exactly by the weight
  multiset failing closure under negation at N = 1…5 — so there is no SU(3)-invariant bilinear form and
  hence no polarization compatible with the substrate's own symmetry group. `j = ½` is **quaternionic**
  (FS = −1), which supplies *both* the symplectic `Q` and the `J` with `J² = −I`.

**Tautology fence** (seam-audit rule, `sprint_qi_seam_audit_memo.md` §6). One can always *impose* a
ℚ(i)-structure on a complex vector space by hand and call multiplication-by-`i` a CM structure; the audit
already rules that non-evidence ("any two ℚ(i)-structures on two ℚ²'s are abstractly isomorphic"). The
P56 claim is that the Dirac `J` is **forced**. Nothing on the Hardy side forces one: (iv), (vii), (viii)
each fail independently.

**q-series identification (the checklist's step 2(b)).** The Hardy partition function is
`Σ_N g_N q^N = 1/(1−q)³` — verified exactly through `q^40` — i.e. the **Hilbert series of ℂ[z₁,z₂,z₃]**,
a rational function of `q` with a triple pole at `q = 1`; motivically the class of `𝔸³`, **pure Tate**.
With the zero-point shift it becomes `q^{3/2}/(1−q)³`: a rational function of `q^{1/2}`, so the shift
contributes a **square root** (a μ₂ / double-cover datum), not a fourth root. Thermally,
`Z(β) = 1/(2 sinh(β/2))³` (checked at β = 1). Against θ₃² (the P59/P56 seam's route-(b) CM carrier),
three independent exact discriminators separate them:

| test | Hardy `g_N` | `θ₃² = Σ r₂(n)qⁿ` |
|:--|:--|:--|
| constant-coeff. linear recurrence over ℚ (⇔ rational gen. fn.) | **yes**, order 3 | **no** (order ≤ 10 excluded) |
| multiplicativity (Hecke-eigenform signature) | **no** (`g₆ = 28` vs `g₂g₃ = 60`) | **yes** |
| Dirichlet series / conductor | `½[ζ(s−2)+3ζ(s−1)+2ζ(s)]`, **conductor 1** | `4ζ(s)β(s)`, **conductor 4** |

(both closed forms verified numerically at s = 4: |diff| = 0.0 and 1.3e-16 with an explicit 2.2e-13 tail bound).
So the *ungraded* Hardy object is decisively pure-Tate; it is nothing like a CM theta series.

## 3. Leg (B): the χ₋₄ twist IS present — the unanticipated positive

### 3.1 The computation

The Bargmann graph's edges all have `|ΔN| = 1` (checked on the production graph, 165/165 edges), so the
graph is **bipartite** and `(−1)^N` is a natural ℤ₂ grading of the substrate — and it is exactly the phase
circle's half-period point `e^{iπN̂}`. Grading the spectral zeta by it:

> **`Z₋(s) = Σ_N (−1)^N g_N (N + 3/2)^{−s} = 2^{s−3}( β(s) − β(s−2) )`**

Derivation (exact, same two Hurwitz identities as P28): with `x = N + 3/2`, `g_N = (x² − 1/4)/2`, so
`Z₋(s) = ½η_H(s−2) − ⅛η_H(s)` with `η_H(s) = Σ(−1)^N x^{−s}`; the parity split gives
`η_H(s) = 2^{−s}[ζ(s,3/4) − ζ(s,5/4)] = 2^s(1 − β(s))`, and the two `1`'s cancel in the difference.

Verified at dps 50 by **three independent routes** — a hand-written Euler-transform direct sum (no library
accelerator, no logs in the summand, so the Levin/log memory-rule trap does not apply; stable to 0.0 across
(300,150) vs (600,300) terms/levels and to 6.7e-52 across dps 50 vs 80), the Hurwitz split, and the closed
form — agreeing to ~5e-51 at s = 4, 5, 6, 7. β(2) anchored against `mpmath.catalan` (|diff| = 0.0).

**Headline value:** `Z₋(4) = 2(β(4) − G) = 0.14595791512777264211…`, with `G` Catalan's constant.

### 3.2 It is exactly ¼ of the S³ Dirac object

P28 `thm:chi4`: `D_even(s) − D_odd(s) = 2^{s−1}(β(s) − β(s−2))`, and P28's own s = 4 statement is
`8(β(4) − G)`. The ratio to the Hardy object is **exactly 4**, verified numerically at s = 4 and s = 6
(ratio = 4.0 to printed precision). The reason is structural, not numerical:

- both towers carry the **same eigenvalues** `m = index + 3/2`, because both are "index + d/2" at d = 3 —
  the S³ Dirac's spinor conformal weight `d/2` on `S^d`, and the HO's zero-point `d/2` for `d` modes;
- their degeneracies differ by exactly 4 in P28's convention: `2(m²−¼)` vs `(m²−¼)/2`.

### 3.3 Ablation — what actually makes the 4 (computed, not guessed)

The task asked whether the 3/2 zero-point produces χ₋₄ or a different (χ₋₃ / trivial) twist. Computed:

| zero-point shift | Hurwitz arguments | graded ζ at s = 4 | class |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 (integer) | {1/2, 1} | 0.862004855396904 | `ζ(s,½)−ζ(s,1) = (2^s−2)ζ(s)` — **pure Tate** |
| 2 (integer) | {1, 3/2} | 0.039537821972791 | **pure Tate** |
| 1/2 | {1/4, 3/4} | 15.517167668876 | **χ₋₄**, conductor 4 |
| **3/2 (the HO)** | **{3/4, 5/4}** | **0.145957915127773** | **χ₋₄**, conductor 4 |
| 5/2 | {5/4, 7/4} | 0.014029328726115 | **χ₋₄**, conductor 4 |

(direct and Hurwitz routes agree in every row; the integer-shift rows were additionally re-expressed as
ζ-only combinations, |diff| = 0.0 at s = 4 and s = 6.) The **ungraded** Hardy zeta is pure Tate at every
shift, via `ζ(s,3/2) = (2^s−1)ζ(s) − 2^s`.

So the discriminant is **shift parity**, and the character in every half-integer case is **χ₋₄** — the same
one, not χ₋₃ or trivial. A counterfactual μ₃ grading was computed for completeness: a 3-fold split of
`N + 3/2` gives Hurwitz arguments {1/2, 5/6, 7/6} — **sixths**, i.e. characters of conductor dividing 12
(field ℚ(μ₁₂)), not a clean χ₋₃; and the substrate has no ℤ₃ grading anyway (it is bipartite).

### 3.4 Honest fences on leg (B) — read before quoting it

1. **Mechanism transfers; a measurement does not.** On the Dirac side, `D_even − D_odd` is attached to a
   computed observable (P28: the two-loop sunset's vertex-parity constraint) and the β-values are
   **MEASURED** there (80-digit PSLQ). On the HO side, `Z₋(s)` is a *natural* object (the substrate's own
   bipartition grading) but is **not currently attached to any HO/nuclear observable in the corpus**. The
   claim is "the arithmetic is available by the identical mechanism", not "χ₋₄ has been measured in an HO
   observable". Tier: **MEASURED (identity)** / **OBSERVATION (significance)**.
2. **No conflict with Layer 6, but a conflation risk.** Layer 6 says "no half-integer-**weight** chirality
   grading exists on the (N,0) tower" — that is the *angular* weight (`l` integer), and it is correct. The
   HO's 3/2 is a *different* half-integer (the zero-point / metaplectic index). Both statements stand; a
   reader could easily merge them and conclude the Hardy tower has no half-integer content at all, which
   §3 shows is false.
3. **The zero-point shift is present in the skeleton, not imposed.** P24's graph diagonal is
   `Fraction(2N+3, 2)` and `thm:pi-free` certifies it; the 3/2 is the metaplectic index of `Mp(6,ℝ)`, not a
   bookkeeping choice. (One *can* normal-order it away to the bare Euler operator, and then leg (B)
   disappears too — that is a real difference from the Dirac case, where the shift cannot be removed
   without breaking the ±-symmetry that chirality forces. Worth stating as the residual asymmetry inside
   leg (B), and it is the one place a reviewer could push back.)

## 4. PROPOSAL for the PI — a candidate **Layer 7** (leg A), plus an explicit NON-layer (leg B)

Not applied. Drafted in the register of `sec:asymmetry_layer4`'s existing six-item list.

> **Layer 7 (proposed): CM / Hodge-circle arithmetic of the compact flow.**
> *S³ (Coulomb-specific).* The compact Bisognano–Wichmann/KMS circle `K = diag(2m_j)` has ±-symmetric
> integer spectrum, so at its β/4 quarter-period it *is* a ℚ-rational complex structure `J`, `J² = −1`
> (P56 `rem:kms_hodge_circle`); with the ℚ-rational Riemann form `QJ = I` the fundamental doublet is a
> polarized weight-1 ℚ-Hodge structure whose Hodge group is the norm-1 torus of ℚ(i) — a CM point,
> conductor 4 (P56 `prop:hodge_cm_point`).
> *S⁵ Hardy.* The phase circle generated by the Euler operator is equally compact and even carries a
> genuine μ₄ (the metaplectic 3/2 lifts the graph's bipartition parity to order 4), but **no CM Hodge
> structure exists**, for three independent reasons: (i) the generator's spectrum is strictly positive,
> and `det(cos·I + sin·J) = 1` forces a traceless generator, i.e. a ±pair; (ii) on the substrate's own
> ℚ-form (the π-free `(N,l,m)` basis, `thm:pi-free`) the quarter point acts by `i^N`, which is ℚ-rational
> only where it squares to `+1`; (iii) a weight-1 Hodge structure needs the conjugate half `(0,N)` —
> exactly the antiholomorphic sector the Hardy construction discards.
> *Mechanism.* The Coulomb side's `J` is supplied by the **quaternionic** (FS = −1) half-integer spin rep
> together with the chirality-doubled ±spectrum; the Hardy side's `(N,0)` is **complex** type (FS = 0) and
> singly-graded. Equivalently: chirality already provides the conjugate half that a Hodge structure
> requires, and holomorphic-sector restriction is precisely the act of throwing it away.
> *Relation to the earlier layers.* The two obstructions overlap Layer 6's stated conditions (strictly
> positive Hardy spectrum; no half-integer angular weight), so Layer 7 is a **new consequence level**
> (arithmetic / Tannakian: the field of definition of the flow's Hodge group), not a new obstruction —
> the same relation Layers 5 and 6 bear to Layers 1–3.

> **Explicit NON-layer (companion; belongs in the *universal residue* column, with Wilson–Hodge
> combinatorial vocabulary).** Conductor-4 period content does **not** separate the two sides. The Hardy
> tower's bipartition-graded spectral zeta is `Z₋(s) = 2^{s−3}(β(s) − β(s−2))`, exactly ¼ of P28
> `thm:chi4`'s `2^{s−1}(β(s) − β(s−2))`, because both towers carry the same `index + d/2` eigenvalues at
> `d = 3`. χ₋₄ is produced by a **half-integer index shift × a ℤ₂ grading**; on S³ the shift comes from
> spin, on S⁵ from the metaplectic zero-point. Compactness alone produces neither leg (S³ scalar: compact
> and pure-Tate; S⁵ Hardy ungraded: compact and pure-Tate, `1/(1−q)³`).

## 5. Discriminator assessment

The background discriminator under test was: *"arithmetic/CM enters with the SPIN double cover (J² = −1,
μ₄ quarter-periods), not with compactness per se."* The computation splits it:

- **"not with compactness" — CONFIRMED and strengthened.** A *third* compact circle now sits on the
  pure-Tate side of the ungraded ledger (after the S³ scalar sector): the Hardy phase circle, with
  `1/(1−q)³` and a conductor-1 L-function. Compactness gives torsion; it does not give ℚ(i).
- **"with the spin double cover" — needs splitting.** Only leg (A) is spin-specific, and the sharp reason
  is not "spin" as such but **quaternionicity + the ±-paired (chirality-doubled) spectrum**: a ℚ-rational
  `J` must act as a *real rotation of a ℚ-rational 2-plane*, which needs a traceless generator. Leg (B)
  needs only **a half-integer index shift plus a ℤ₂ grading**, and the metaplectic double cover supplies
  it with no spin anywhere in sight.
- **Sharpened statement.** *ℚ(i) enters wherever an order-2 datum is lifted to order 4 — spin or
  metaplectic — and that is enough for conductor-4 **periods**; it takes the further, spin-specific
  ingredient of a ±-paired ℚ-rational carrier for those periods to sit on a **CM Hodge structure**.*
- **Consequence for P56 `rem:kms_hodge_circle`, qualification 2 (flagged, PI-gated, not applied).** That
  remark's discriminant — "whether the μ₄ point acts faithfully (spinor) or through μ₂ (scalar)" — is
  **necessary but not sufficient**: the HO's μ₄ point acts with order 4 (not through μ₂) and still yields
  no CM structure. The complete discriminant is faithful μ₄ **acting by a real rotation on a ℚ-rational
  2-plane**, i.e. a ±-symmetric generator spectrum.
- **Corroboration for the seam scan's "defended survivor".** The scan's step-C candidate — "ℚ(i) enters
  wherever an order-2 physical symmetry acts through its order-4 lift" — gains an independent, spin-free
  instance here (parity → `e^{iπ(N+3/2)} = −i(−1)^N`, order 4 → χ₋₄). It does **not** upgrade the P56↔P59
  seam (still CONVERGENT: no comparison map, and this is a third carrier, not a bridge).
