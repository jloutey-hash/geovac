## Why

Paper 25's S² base graph collapses each m_l multiplet at fixed (n, l); since 2026-10-31 the paper calls this a
stand-in (orbits of L). The Hopf fibre itself is generated by
L_z + A_z = 2 J⁺_z, where J^± = (L ± A)/2 are the two chiral SU(2) factors of SO(4); shell n carries spin
j = (n−1)/2 under each. The PI's principle, "use the geometry the quantum numbers suggest", asks which base-graph
quantities depend on the grouping. This record fixes a Hopf recipe before any Hopf base-graph quantity is
computed, and separates comparisons fixed by SO(4) (verified as controls) from open ones (predicted).

## Method (fixed before scoring)

- **Graph and generators.** 14 nodes (n ≤ 3), 0/1 adjacency of `GeometricLattice(max_n=3)` (13 edges); L, A
  imported from `tests/test_atlas_so4_commutant.py` (same node order, checked).
- **Hopf groups (primary).** In shell n, group (n, μ) is the eigenspace of J⁻_z = (L_z − A_z)/2 with eigenvalue
  μ ∈ {−j, …, j}. This keeps the J⁻ label and collapses the fibre label m⁺ (the J⁺_z eigenvalue), just as the
  rotation recipe keeps l and collapses m_l. Each group is an irreducible SU(2)⁺ multiplet. The subspace is
  canonical: it is an eigenspace, so no basis or phase choice enters. Group weight = dim = n (analogue of 2l+1).
  At n_max = 3: groups (1,0), (2,±½), (3,−1), (3,0), (3,1), with weights 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3.
- **Occupation.** p_{n,μ}(n,l,m) = ⟨n,l,m|P_{n,μ}|n,l,m⟩ = |⟨j, m−μ; j, μ | l, m⟩|², an exact rational number. M is
  the 14 × 6 matrix of occupations. Its rows sum to 1, and for the rotation grouping it is the 0/1 assignment matrix.
- **Edge rule (primary): occupation-weighted crossing count.** W_full = Mᵀ A M. The base adjacency is its
  off-diagonal part. The diagonal (intra-group, "fibre") part is dropped, as Paper 25 drops intra-sector edges.
  The base Laplacian is L_base = diag(W 1) − W. Why primary: (i) it reproduces Paper 25's rule exactly when M is
  0/1 (control C2–C3); (ii) it depends only on diag P_g, so it does not depend on the relative phase convention
  between the graph's 0/1 edges and the hydrogenic states; (iii) it is exact rational, so arithmetic questions
  about the spectrum can be decided exactly.
- **Edge rule (alternative, reported only): coherent Frobenius weight.** W[g,g′] = ‖P_g A P_g′‖²_F. It reproduces
  Paper 25 (C4) and sees only subspaces (C5), but is not phase-free: node phases fixing L and the graph leave one
  relative sign at n_max = 3 (s-bundle (2,0)→(3,0) vs p-bundle (2,1)→(3,1)), entering via the cross term
  Tr(P_g T_s P_g′ T_p†), nonzero for g′ = (3,±1) (derived from the rule's form; no Hopf weight evaluated). If run:
  the guard's `geovac.shibuya_wulfman` sign convention.
- **m⁺ vs m⁻ is not a choice:** diag((−1)^l) commutes with the l-preserving adjacency and swaps J⁺ ↔ J⁻, so both
  give identical W under both rules (F3).
- **B analogue.** B_H = Σ_g (weight) × (Casimir of the group whose irreps the groups are) = Tr J⁺² (primary).
  Reported alongside: Σ_g Tr(P_g L²).
- **Driver:** `debug/hopf_grouping_controls.py` (`forced` and `controls` run; `score` written but NOT run). The
  scored route is exact (sympy CG²). The numerical eigenprojectors of J⁻_z are the second route, and they agree
  with the exact occupations to 7.8e-16 (F2).

  Hopf-recipe Casimir sum is exactly half of B. Symbolic identity Σ_{l<n}(2l+1)l(l+1) = n²(n²−1)/2, residual 0;

- F4: L² is not diagonal on Hopf groups, but Tr(P_g L²) = n(n²−1)/2 on every group (3.6e-15): the Hopf grouping
  keeps B = 42 and erases its resolution into l(l+1).

- F9: Σ W_full = 1ᵀA1 = 26 under any grouping; only the inter/intra split can move.

## Predictions

**HG0 (controls; measured, PASS).** C1–C6 as above.

**HG1 (derived before scoring; a failure means a bug, which voids the run).**
(a) The Hopf base graph is connected: exactly one zero mode, against three for rotation. Derivation: every term of
Mᵀ A M is ≥ 0. The radial edges (1,0,0)–(2,0,0) and (2,0,0)–(3,0,0) join the s nodes, and by F7 those have occupation
1/n in every group of their shell. So (1,0) links to both shell-2 groups, and each shell-2 group links to all three
shell-3 groups.
(b) W_H is invariant under μ → −μ (F5). So the characteristic polynomial factors as x · p₃(x) · p₂(x): the
mirror-even sector has dimension 4 and holds the zero mode; the odd sector has dimension 2.
(c) Inter-group weight > 5 strictly. All 5 radial edges cross between shells, hence between groups. At least one
angular edge has positive cross weight: (2,1,−1) lies wholly in μ = −½ and (2,1,0) is split ½/½.
- Probability 0.99 each.

**HG2 (open: the fibre gets thinner).** Inter-group weight exceeds half the edge total, i.e. > 13/2 (rotation:
5/13). Reason: an angular edge moves m by one at fixed l. At fixed μ = m⁻ that is a step in m⁺, so the edge stays
inside a group only through a diagonal overlap that is generically below 1. In shell 2 every angular edge is half
crossing (hand-derived for shell 2 only; disclosed).
- Falsifier: inter-group weight ≤ 13/2. Probability 0.8.

**HG3 (open: the integer spectrum is a property of the rotation stand-in).** At least one of p₃, p₂ does not
split over ℚ, so the Hopf base spectrum has an irrational eigenvalue. Rotation's spectrum is all integers. Reason:
the weights are rationals with denominators 2, 3 and 6, and the only symmetry left is the μ-mirror, which forces
no further factorization.
- Falsifier: p₃ and p₂ both split into rational linear factors. Probability 0.75.

**HG4 (reported, not gated).** λ_max against 6 for rotation; expected smaller (the p-bundle spreads over several
group pairs); probability 0.6.

**HG5 (reported, not gated).** The alternative (Frobenius) rule: W, spectrum and zero-mode count, with the stated
convention. Reported whether or not it agrees with the primary.

## Reading if GO, stated in advance

On the same 14 labels, Paper 25's base-graph quantities split three ways.
- **Grouping-blind:** F, the total edge weight, and the group count per shell (n under both groupings).
- **Grouping-dependent, forced:** the weight profile; the Casimir sum, Tr J⁺² = B/2 under Hopf against B under
  rotation (B itself survives as Σ_g Tr P_g L², uniform per group); and the number of zero modes, 1 against 3.
- **Grouping-dependent, measured:** the fibre fraction, and whether the spectrum is integer.

The three zero modes Paper 25 attributes to "the three disconnected components inherited from the S³ graph" are
the l-blocks of the rotation grouping. The Hopf grouping on the same labels joins them. So the components and the
integer spectrum {0,0,0,1,3,6} describe the rotation stand-in, not the Hopf quotient (consistent with the
2026-10-31 Sec. III.B wording; would support one added sentence). No Paper 2 number changes.

**Caps:** n_max = 3 only (the guard's generators); one graph; one radial family for A.
