WHAT THE TABLES SHOW:
  - H (graph mode): the legacy graph model H = kappa*(D - A) has
    E_0 = kappa*lambda_max matched to -1/2 by construction
    (kappa = -1/16). Its 'Sat.def%' column is the band-edge
    saturation deficit (8 - lambda_max)/8, NOT an accuracy.
  - The 18 symbolic checks (tests/test_fock_*.py) verify the
    CONTINUUM Fock projection. The lattice's edge Laplacian
    D - A does NOT converge to the S3 Laplace-Beltrami operator
    (n_max disconnected l-blocks; a documented wall, Paper 7).
  - The GeoVac He rows used the legacy graph-kinetic model
    (retired family, 2026-09-25); physical fixed-k CI at n_max=5
    is 1.93% above exact (Paper 13).
  - Speed/sparsity figures are for the graph eigenproblem only;
    they are not an accuracy-matched comparison with PySCF.

LIMITATIONS:
  - H2 'Full CI' here is the legacy LCAO graph-concatenation
    model, superseded (FCI-M: no equilibrium geometry). Its
    ~2% figure benefits from error cancellation between the
    Mulliken V_cross and point-charge V_ee approximations.
  - No basis-set extrapolation procedure established.

VERDICT:
  This output supports no GeoVac accuracy claim. The graph-mode
  hydrogen energies are band-edge saturation, the H2 model is
  superseded, and the comparison is not at matched accuracy.
  Current GeoVac results live in the papers (e.g. Papers 12,
  13, 14), benchmarked against strong baselines there.
