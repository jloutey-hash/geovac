\begin{abstract}
The GeoVac framework produces a wide class of structural facts (the $(n,l,m)$ label set and its $\mathrm{SO}(4)$ Casimir spectrum, $\sphere{3}$ outer manifold, master Mellin engine, inner-algebra factor count, the $D_F$ moduli dimension $\dim \Mcal(\DF) = 32$ at one generation (matter-sector projection rank $16$; corrected 2026-09-03 from a degenerate-sample $128/260$;\ the factor count and this dimension hold relative to the Hopf-tower organizing reading, the adopted Upgrade~B axiom and the imported CCM representation), two-term-exact gravity, and many more), admits the gauge group $U(1) \times SU(2) \times SU(3)$ under an organizing reading plus an adopted axiom (Paper~32;\ not a derivation), and consumes specific numerical content as external input (Yukawa values, generation count, calibration cutoff moments, atomic-physics screening exponents, multi-loop QED counterterms, etc.). We catalogue both sides of this \emph{forced/free seam} across the corpus --- 40 forced entries (the gauge-group entries forced relative to the adopted Upgrade~B axiom), 4 Observations ($\kappa$, matched not forced;\ two released-axis results that lack a derivation, the decompactification front and the four-body $r_{12}$ bridge;\ and the rung rule, a pattern across the tested instances), 1 admitted-not-forced entry, 25 calibration entries and 3 walls, spanning foundations, gauge structure, periods, gravity, QED, the Standard Model inner factor, multi-focal-composition walls, chemistry / quantum computing scaling, foundational calibration and the released axes of the chemistry program:\ a second nucleus, the decompactification front, a third and a fourth centre, bending, a three-electron chain and the four-body $r_{12}$ bridge, each recorded with the number class that enters when the axis is released, where one is assigned --- weight-one logarithms at two centres, an elliptic period at three, a K3 holomorphic period for the chain. We identify a cross-domain unity pattern (the same wall appears in six independent settings, all carrying the same multi-focal-composition signature) and test five candidate principles for what discriminates the two sides: multi-focal depth, period-content classifiability, dimensional character, compactness inheritance, and packing-reachability. The fifth candidate (packing-reachability:  an entry is forced iff a witness structural derivation exists from $\{$Paper~0 + standard real-spectral-triple axioms + Hopf-tower truncation + Bertrand + Upgrade~B + CCM canonical SM representation$\}$) \emph{consolidates} the forced/free boundary rather than independently discriminating it:  its 98.3\% agreement with the F/C status (59/60, on the 2026-06-08 labelling in which $\kappa$ was counted forced) is an internal-consistency check confirming the catalogue applies its conventions uniformly --- the packing-reachable tag is the F/C label by construction --- not an independent accuracy.  The independent discriminator signal is carried by multi-focal depth, period class, and the two-family decomposition. The earlier-named two-family decomposition (multi-focal composition vs.\ inner-factor input data) is preserved as the structural failure-mode story beneath the principle:  both families share the same surface verdict (packing-unreachability) but exhibit different structural reasons for it. This paper consolidates what was previously scattered across CLAUDE.md \S 1.7 working hypotheses, dead-end records, and individual sprint memos, and adds the Sprint C2 principle hunt that closes the question Paper 57 §6.1 originally left open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: the seam and why naming it matters}
\label{sec:intro}

GeoVac is a discretization framework built on the Paper~0 packing axiom~\cite{paper0}, whose node set is exactly the label set $(n,l,m)$ of the unit-three-sphere harmonics, together with Fock's conformal equivalence (whose chain Paper~7 verifies) between the continuum $\sphere{3}$ Laplace--Beltrami operator and the Schr\"odinger problem with $-Z/r$ potential~\cite{paper7}.  (The graph's edge Laplacian is \emph{not} that operator and does not converge to it:\ it preserves $l$ and splits into $n_{\max}$ disconnected blocks, Paper~0~\S VI.) From this minimal foundation the framework has been shown to generate a large class of structural facts:  the angular sparsity theorem of Paper~22~\cite{paper22}, the chemistry Pauli count of Paper~14~\cite{paper14} (exactly linear in the qubit count across molecules at fixed basis, $N_{\mathrm{Pauli}} = 27.90 \times Q$), the spectral triple structure of Paper~32~\cite{paper32} with state-space Gromov--Hausdorff convergence (proved) of the true-window truncations to the round-$\sphere{3}$ triple for the commutative factor (Paper~38~\cite{paper38}, main theorem:\ a Fej\'er-kernel bound $(4/\pi)\log n/n$, \textbf{[INTERNAL THEOREM + MEASURED]}, and a measured $O(1/n)$ with a tapered amplitude), the master Mellin engine's tagging of the transcendentals in the census of GeoVac spectral data (a census and a reading, not a classification;\ Paper~18 \S III.7;\ Paper~32 \S VIII case-exhaustion theorem, \textbf{[CONDITIONAL]} and scoped to the first fifteen projections~\cite{paper18,paper32}), and the Forced-Count Theorem (moduli dimension $\dim \Mcal(\DF) = 32$ at one generation, matter-sector projection rank $16$) (Paper~32 \S VIII, \texttt{thm:forced\_count}).  The gauge group $U(1) \times SU(2) \times SU(3)$ is catalogued as forced only \emph{relative to the adopted Upgrade~B axiom}:\ it follows from the Bertrand $\times$ Hopf-tower organizing reading plus that axiom (Paper~32 \S VIII.B), not from a derivation.

The framework also consumes specific numerical content that it does not autonomously generate. The Yukawa values cannot be selected by the inner-factor structure (Sprint H1 non-selection theorem, empirically confirmed at 162 PSLQ cells against the inner-factor $M_1 \cup M_2$ pure-Tate basis~\cite{paper32,debug_yukawa_pslq}). The generation count $\Ngen = 3$ is packing-unreachable in the current axiom system (Direction~2 NO-GO, Read~2 NO-GO~\cite{debug_read2}). The chemistry-side inner-region correlation wall (\emph{W1e}) consumes external atomic-physics screening data (Clementi--Raimondi exponents) and cannot be closed by any framework-internal rational/algebraic modification, as verified by six independent sprint attempts (CLAUDE.md \S 3 entries F1--F6 plus Schmidt, core correlation, and basis enlargement). The multi-loop QED renormalization counterterms ($Z_2$, $\delta m$) are likewise consumed rather than generated (LS-8a wall~\cite{paper36}).

The line between what the framework forces and what it consumes is itself a structural property of the framework. It has been repeatedly named --- the \emph{structural-skeleton scope} reading of CLAUDE.md \S 1.7 WH3, the \emph{multi-focal-composition wall pattern} crystallized at six instances, the \emph{external-input three-class partition} (Calibration / Multi-focal composition / Multi-determinant correlation) --- but never consolidated. Each naming has produced a clearer statement than the previous one, but the underlying inventory of which physical inputs sit on each side, and which discriminator (if any) governs the partition, has been distributed across many sprint memos, dead-end rows, and working-hypothesis bullets.

This paper consolidates the inventory and tests, at preliminary level, four candidate discriminator principles against it. The goal is not to close the seam --- we do not yet claim to have identified \emph{the} principle that decides which side a given physical input falls on --- but to name the question sharply enough that it admits a determinate answer.

\paragraph{Why an observations paper, not a theorem paper.}
The format follows Paper~2, Paper~25, and Paper~33 (the existing observation-genre papers in the GeoVac corpus). We describe a structural pattern that the framework exhibits across multiple independent domains, we cite the underlying theorems and dead-end records that establish each entry, and we probe candidate discriminators without commitment. Where a finding has theorem-grade status it is named as such; where it is a numerical / empirical observation only, the honest scope is recorded explicitly. The companion catalogue \texttt{docs/forced\_free\_seam.md} carries the full table; this paper is the publication-grade framing.

\paragraph{What we promise to deliver in this paper.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \S\ref{sec:forced} --- forced-side inventory across nine domains (foundations, gauge, periods, gravity, QED, Standard Model inner factor, chemistry/QC scaling, foundational calibration, and --- added 2026-10-15 --- the released axes of the chemistry program, \S\ref{sec:forced_released}; the load-bearing sub-domains).
  \item \S\ref{sec:free} --- free-side inventory with non-selection theorems and documented negative searches.
  \item \S\ref{sec:unity} --- the cross-domain unity:  the multi-focal-composition wall pattern at six independent instances.
  \item \S\ref{sec:principles} --- formal probe of five candidate discriminators (Sprint C2 finding:  packing-reachability is the consolidated F/C boundary;  its 98.3\% agreement with the F/C status is an internal-consistency check, not an independent discriminator).
  \item \S\ref{sec:open} --- open questions; the ``second packing axiom'' status.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What we do NOT promise.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A theorem stating ``GeoVac forces $X$ iff $X$ has property $P$.''  No such theorem is proved or even decisively favoured in this paper.
  \item A derivation of any presently-free quantity from a single GeoVac principle.
  \item A demonstration that the present seam is the only possible seam (a ``second packing axiom'' could shift the line and remains the named long-range frontier).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Foundations / skeleton (A)}
\label{sec:forced_foundations}

The packing construction of Paper~0 motivates the labels $(l,m)$ (with $n$ and $s$ recorded there as Observations);\ through Fock's projection, the continuum $\sphere{3}$ Laplace--Beltrami operator acts on the label set $(n,l,m)$ exactly as the diagonal $SO(4)$ Casimir, with spectrum $-(n^2 - 1)$, at every cutoff (the graph's edge Laplacian does \emph{not} converge to it;\ it splits into $n_{\max}$ disconnected $l$-blocks)~\cite{paper0,paper7}. Paper~7's eighteen symbolic checks (ten limiting-case, definitional or non-discriminating) of Fock's $\sphere{3}$ conformal chain~\cite{paper7} pin the outer manifold; Bertrand's classical theorem~\cite{bertrand1873} combined with SO(4) closure forces it to be $\sphere{3}$ rather than a different homogeneous space. The matched energy scale $\kappa = -1/16$ is exact rational, but it is matched rather than forced:\ it maps the graph's $\lambda_{\max} \to 8$ (twice the maximum degree, a grid band edge carrying no $\sphere{3}$ information) onto $-1/2$, and its coincidence with the Fock value $1/\Omega^4(0)$ is an Observation (Papers~0, 7). The shell degeneracy $g_n = n^2$ is representation-theoretic. The deeper organizing observation, established across Papers~18, 22, 24, 27, 32, and consolidated in the structural-skeleton scope pattern, is that the framework's discrete content lies in the closed/compact/Peter--Weyl regime; the continuum is what remains when compactness is released. (Compactness forces discreteness, A5;\ the converse fails --- discrete spectrum is the compact-resolvent property, as for the Paper~24 oscillator on non-compact $\mathbb{R}^3$.) Two rows added 2026-10-15 make that pair explicit:\ A6 records the edge Laplacian $D-A$ as a wall (it preserves $l$, splits into $n_{\max}$ disconnected blocks with $\dim\ker L = n_{\max}$ at every cutoff, and does not converge to $\Delta_{S^3}$;\ Paper~0~\S VI, Paper~7, \texttt{tests/test\_graph\_s3\_nonconvergence.py}), and A7 records the Bargmann--Segal lattice of the harmonic oscillator as forced $\pi$-free (bit-exact rational at every $N_{\max}$;\ Paper~24~\cite{paper24}), the corpus's own instance of a discrete spectrum on a non-compact space, which is why compactness is recorded as sufficient for discreteness and not necessary.

\subsection{Gauge structure (B)}
\label{sec:forced_gauge}

The Standard Model gauge group is reached, under an organizing reading plus the adopted Upgrade~B axiom (Paper~32;\ not a derivation), from two independent structural inputs:  Bertrand's classical theorem (only $-Z/r$ and $kr^2$ central potentials produce closed orbits) and the Hopf-tower of spheres $\sphere{1} \to \sphere{3} \to \sphere{5} \to \cdots$ truncated at $n \le 3$ by the Hurwitz classification of associative real normed division algebras (Door~4d, summarized in \cite{paper32}, \S VIII.B). This produces
\begin{equation}
  \AF \;=\; \C \,\oplus\, \HH \,\oplus\, M_3(\C),
  \label{eq:af}
\end{equation}
with $\C$ at $n=1$ from the unique division algebra of $\sphere{1}$, $M_3(\C)$ at $n=3$ from the Hurwitz fallback at $\sphere{5}$, and $\HH$ at $n=2$ \emph{admitted} but not autonomously forced over $M_2(\C)$ in the J-sign-table audit (Door~4c). The admitted-not-forced status of $\HH$ is the lone admitted entry in our catalogue and is recorded as B5.

The Forced-Count Theorem~\cite{paper32} (Paper~32 \S VIII, \texttt{thm:forced\_count}), given the admitted $\mathcal{A}_F$ and the imported CCM representation, chains the standard real-spectral-triple axioms (Hermiticity, KO-dim-6 chirality, $J$-reality, order-one) into the bit-exact moduli dimension
\begin{equation}
  \dim_{\R} \Mcal(\DF) \;=\; 32 \quad (\text{one generation; matter-sector projection rank } 16),
  \label{eq:forced_count}
\end{equation}
(corrected 2026-09-03:\ under a linear $*$-representation of $\mathcal{A}_F$ the full-axiom $D_F$ moduli dimension is $32$ with matter-sector projection rank $16$, Paper~32 \texttt{thm:forced\_count}; the $128$ per generation and the full-axiom $260$ printed before were a degenerate-sample artefact). The eight free parameters per generation in the diagonal slice are exactly the Yukawa values (charged-lepton + quark sector). The theorem forces the \emph{count}; it does not force the \emph{values}.

The Higgs admission --- the structural fact that the Mexican-hat field-theory structure is allowed within the inner factor's spectral action --- is the conclusion of Sprint H1 (POSITIVE-THIN verdict, Paper~32 \S VIII.C). It is an admission of \emph{structure} (given an imposed Yukawa), paired with a non-forcing of \emph{value} via Sprint H1's Yukawa non-selection theorem.

  \item \textbf{J7, a three-electron chain (F for the K3 fibre and its elliptic-squared period;\ the content beyond them is evidence only).} The integral $\langle \rho_a\rho_b\rho_c/(r_{12}r_{13})\rangle$ over two-centre densities has two momentum integrations and three roots;\ its fibre is a K3 surface (the double cover of $\mathbb{P}^2$ branched on six lines), the holomorphic period reduces to a three-scale Bessel moment $\int K_0K_0K_0$, a sum of two products $K\,K'$ --- ``elliptic squared'' --- and the one-centre limit is rational. At equal scales the true fibre collapses to K3 periods and $\pi^3$ (evidence);\ at generic scales a PSLQ search at one mass triple finds no relation among K3, elliptic and Tate periods, in a basis that omits dilogarithms and products of elliptic periods (evidence, not proof). Pre-registered on 2026-10-13;\ five falsifiers passed;\ home Paper~59 (\texttt{sec:three\_electron}), guard \texttt{tests/test\_paper59\_three\_electron\_k3.py}.
  \item \textbf{J8, the four-body $r_{12}$ bridge (Obs, validated reduction).} The only four-electron-connected term of the explicit-$r_{12}$ energy, $f_{12}f_{34}/r_{13}$, reduces without a resolution of the identity to a finite contraction of three-body vertex kernels and, for $s$ leaves, to a two-electron Slater--Condon integral (Paper~12;\ validated against Monte Carlo, no derivation, $N \ge 5$ conjectural). \subsection{The unifying statement}
\label{sec:unity_statement}

\begin{observation}[Multi-focal-composition wall]
\label{obs:multi_focal_wall}
The framework couples discrete labels (quantum numbers $n, l, m, m_s, \kappa, m_j$, isospin, color, gauge representations) cleanly via Wigner symbols and selection rules. Single-focal-length QED on $\sphere{3}$ is handled including curvature corrections at structural level (Parker--Toms verified at 0.5\% in HF-2). \end{observation}

The G4b cross-manifold question $\Tcal_{\sphere{3}} \otimes \Tcal_{\sphere{5}}$ is the spectral-triple-level instance of the same wall. Paper~24 \S V records this as the fourth Coulomb / HO asymmetry layer~\cite{paper24}.

\subsection{Operational consequence}
\label{sec:unity_operational}

When dispatching new investigations, the catalogue affords a forward-classification:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Discrete-label couplings} $\to$ framework handles natively. Single sprint, expect positive.
  \item \textbf{Single-focal-length QED on $\sphere{3}$} $\to$ framework handles natively; curvature corrections expected (Parker--Toms 0.5\%).
  \item \textbf{Multi-focal spatial composition} $\to$ framework hits Observation~\ref{obs:multi_focal_wall}. Sprint should be diagnostic, naming which composition is missing, not implementation. Expect negative-with-clear-naming.
  \item \textbf{Renormalization counterterms} $\to$ framework hits the LS-8a wall (= Observation~\ref{obs:multi_focal_wall} sub-case).
\end{itemize}

This classification is what permits the catalogue to function as a research-direction tool rather than a static record:  candidate investigations can be pre-classified as Class A (handle), Class B (handle, light corrections), Class C (diagnostic only), Class D (out of scope at present axiom level) before resources are committed.

\section{Candidate discriminator principles: a preliminary probe}
\label{sec:principles}

This section probes four candidate principles for what distinguishes forced from free content. None of them is established as \emph{the} principle. They are tested against the catalogue and reported on; the analysis is preliminary by design.

\subsection{Candidate P1:  multi-focal depth}
\label{sec:p1}

\textbf{Principle.}  Forced iff $\mathrm{MF} = 1$; free iff $\mathrm{MF} > 1$.

\textbf{Coverage.}  Every multi-focal-composition entry (G1--G6, E6, E7, E8, F1, F4, F5, F6, F7, D5, D6, I1) sits at $\mathrm{MF} > 1$, correctly placed as free. Every forced entry except C9 (the two-loop $S_{\min}$ period, tagged $\mathrm{MF} > 1$) sits at $\mathrm{MF} = 1$.

\textbf{Misclassifications.}  Eight in all (Table~\ref{tab:p_synthesis}).  Seven entries break P1 in the forced direction: F2 ($\Ngen$), F3 (KO-dim), B8 (gauge lower bound), H6, H7 (chemistry calibration), I2 (Born rule), I3 (Higgs direction) --- all $\mathrm{MF} = 1$ but free;\ the eighth is C9, forced at $\mathrm{MF} > 1$. The seven are precisely the entries of the \emph{inner-factor input data} family (Observation~\ref{obs:two_families}, item 2). P1 catches the multi-focal composition family cleanly but is blind to the inner-factor input data family.

\textbf{Verdict.}  P1 is a one-family discriminator (it discriminates the multi-focal composition family) but does not generalize. The empirical evidence is consistent with the two-family structure of Observation~\ref{obs:two_families}: multi-focal depth is the right axis \emph{for} the multi-focal composition family, full stop.

\subsection{Candidate P2:  period-content classifiability}
\label{sec:p2}

\textbf{Principle.}  Forced iff the entry is classifiable in the master Mellin engine $M_1 / M_2 / M_3$ ring; free iff it sits outside.

\textbf{Coverage.}  The multi-focal walls sit at ``outside,'' correctly placed as free, except E6 and I1 (the $\alpha$ entries, period class M1$+$M2). The forced spectral / period entries C1--C8 sit at $M_1/M_2/M_3$ or ``meta,'' correctly placed as forced;\ C9 is tagged ``outside''.

\textbf{Misclassifications.}  Eight (Table~\ref{tab:p_synthesis}):\ C9 (forced but tagged ``outside''), E6 and I1 (free but inside the ring, M1$+$M2), and B8, F2, F3, I2, I3 (free with period class ``none'' or N/A, so not ``outside'').  The predicate as computed reads only ``outside'' as free, so the gauge / counting forced entries (A1--A5, B1--B7), which mostly sit at period class ``none,'' are classified correctly by it, but for the wrong reason:\ they are forced not because they live in the period ring but because they are non-period combinatorial / representation-theoretic facts, and ``period class none'' applies equally to the free inner-factor entries F2, F3.

\textbf{Verdict.}  P2 catches the multi-focal composition family but is the wrong axis for the rest of the forced inventory and for the inner-factor input data family.

\subsection{Candidate P3:  dimensional character}
\label{sec:p3}

\textbf{Principle.}  Forced iff dimensionless; free iff dimensionful.

\textbf{Coverage.}  Most forced entries (skeleton, gauge, periods, chemistry/QC scaling) are dimensionless ratios. Most multi-focal-wall free entries are dimensionful scales (cutoff $f$, $\Lambda_{cc}$, $v$, etc.).

\textbf{Misclassifications.}  D2 ($S_{\mathrm{BH}}$, $G_N$) is forced and dimensionful. F1 (Yukawa values), F2 ($\Ngen$), F3 (KO-dim), F5 (CKM), F6 (PMNS), B8 (gauge lower bound) are all free and dimensionless. Cell by cell the count is $14$ misclassifications (Table~\ref{tab:p_synthesis};\ the list above is the representative subset).

\textbf{Verdict.}  P3 catches a correlation but not a principle. The correlation reflects that dimensionful objects often require a scale-supplying calibration step, but it does not capture the structural source of the free / forced split.

\subsection{Candidate P4:  compactness inheritance}
\label{sec:p4}

\textbf{Principle.}  Forced iff derivable from the compact / Peter--Weyl regime of the Paper~0 packing axiom; free iff requires continuum (un-compact) data.

\textbf{Coverage.}  The skeleton / spectral / Mellin-engine forced entries (A, C) derive from Peter--Weyl on compact $\sphere{3}$, correctly placed as forced. The cutoff function $f$, the Higgs VEV $v$, the multi-loop counterterms are continuum-side data, correctly placed as free.

\textbf{Misclassifications.}  As stated informally, P4 shares the blind spot of P1 / P2:\ the inner-factor input data family (F2, F3, H6, H7, I2) is not obviously continuum data, yet it is free, and the boundary between ``derivable from Peter--Weyl on compact $\sphere{3}$'' and ``derivable from compact data, broadly'' is not sharp enough to test cell by cell.  The refinement used for Table~\ref{tab:p_synthesis} (forced iff packing-reachable and not both multi-focal and period-outside) misclassifies only C9 and I3, but only because it imports packing-reachability, the criterion of P5;\ its table score is therefore not independent of P5.

\textbf{Verdict.}  P4 captures the structural-skeleton scope reading of CLAUDE.md \S 1.7 WH3 cleanly for the multi-focal composition family (continuum data = un-compact = free), but fails on the inner-factor input data family and needs sharpening to become a formal cell-by-cell predicate.

\subsection{Candidate P5:  packing-reachability}
\label{sec:p5}

The four candidates above all fail on the inner-factor input data family. Sprint C2 (2026-06-08, \texttt{debug/sprint\_c2\_principle\_hunt\_memo.md}) introduces a fifth candidate that does not.

\textbf{Principle.}  An entry is \emph{packing-reachable} iff there exists a witness structural derivation from the axiom set
\begin{equation*}
  \Bigl\{ \text{Paper~0 packing axiom; standard real-spectral-triple axioms (Hermiticity, } J\text{-reality, KO-dim chirality, order-one);}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
  \text{Hopf-tower truncation (Hurwitz); Bertrand's classical theorem; Upgrade~B (sphere-Lie-group axiom adopted);}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
  \text{CCM canonical SM representation on } \Hcal_F \Bigr\}.
\end{equation*}
Predict forced iff packing-reachable.

\textbf{Coverage.}  Every forced entry in the catalogue carries a witness in the corpus:  Paper~32~\S VIII for the Forced-Count Theorem (a derivation), and for the gauge group an organizing reading plus the adopted Upgrade~B axiom, not a derivation~\cite{paper32}; Paper~18 for the master Mellin engine~\cite{paper18}; Paper~28 for the QED structural theorems~\cite{paper28}; Paper~38 for GH convergence~\cite{paper38}; Paper~51 for the gravity closed forms~\cite{paper51}; Papers~22, 14, 23 for the chemistry / QC / nuclear scaling structurals~\cite{paper22,paper14,paper23}. Every calibration entry carries either an explicit non-selection theorem (Sprint H1's Yukawa non-selection; the W1e period-class diagnostic~\cite{debug_w1e}) or a documented no-go / negative-search memo (Direction~2 and Read~2 NO-GOs on $\Ngen$ and inner KO-dim~\cite{debug_seam_packing,debug_read2}; LS-8a renormalization wall~\cite{paper36}; 162-cell Yukawa-PSLQ clean negative~\cite{debug_yukawa_pslq}).

\textbf{Misclassifications.}  One entry, I3 (Higgs direction $\hat n \in S^2$), is tagged \emph{conditional}:  the Boyle--Farnsworth Higgs unit vector could in principle be identified with a GeoVac $S^2$ (the Hopf base, whose volume enters the $M_1$ slot only as a reading), an open follow-on flagged but not pursued.  Treating conditional as forced gives one false positive (I3 predicted forced, actual calibration).  The misclassification is precisely the open identification, not a defect in P5.

\textbf{Verdict.}  P5 is the \emph{consolidated statement of the forced/free boundary itself, not an independent discriminator of it}. An entry is tagged packing-reachable exactly when the corpus carries a forcing derivation, and packing-unreachable exactly when it carries a non-selection theorem or documented negative search --- but ``forcing derivation exists'' is the \emph{definition} of Forced and ``non-selection theorem / negative search'' is the definition of Calibration, so the packing-reachable tag is, by construction, the F/C label under another name. The 98.3\% agreement with the F/C status (59/60 on the 2026-06-08 labelling, $\kappa$ then counted forced; the lone exception I3 is deliberately tagged \emph{conditional} to encode an open identification) is therefore an \emph{internal-consistency check} --- it verifies the catalogue applied its own F/C conventions uniformly across 60 entries --- not a discovered structural predictor, and we do not present it as one. The genuine discriminator content is carried by multi-focal depth (P1), period class (P2), and the two-family decomposition (Observation~\ref{obs:two_families}), all tagged independently of the F/C status; P5 organizes that content rather than predicting it (see \S\ref{sec:open_family2}).

\textbf{What P5 unifies.}  P5 absorbs P1, P2, and P4 as special cases.  Family-1 entries fail P5 because the framework lacks a multi-focal composition theorem (MF > 1 → no closure → packing-unreachable).  Family-2 entries fail P5 because their values live in a categorical-external register on which the outer-factor mechanism is structurally silent ($\eta$-trivialization on the SM side, Paper~18~\S IV.6; chemistry-side analog at the inner-factor input-data tier).  The two families exhibit different reasons for failing P5, but both fail it.

\subsection{P5 and the tautology question (resolved)}
\label{sec:open_family2}

Sprint C2 (\S\ref{sec:p5}) introduced packing-reachability and left open whether it is a structural principle or is definitionally equivalent to the F/C label.

\paragraph{The tautology concern.} The packing-reachable tag for each entry was determined by examining the corpus for witness derivations (forced entries) and explicit non-selection theorems / negative-search memos (calibration entries). \paragraph{A direct check settles it.} We attempted to determine packing-reachability by an \emph{F/C-blind} criterion --- ``does the corpus carry a cited theorem or bit-exact test for this entry?'' --- applied identically to every entry. This does not weaken the catalogue --- the structural discovery is the two-family decomposition and its independent multi-focal-depth / period-class signatures (\S\ref{sec:principles}), which P5 consolidates.

\paragraph{The sharpened open question.} The open question is correspondingly sharpened:  not ``is P5 tautological'' (it is), but whether the packing-reachable boundary admits a \emph{meta-theorem} --- a structural characterization deciding forcedness \emph{without} first locating the forcing-or-non-selection witness. It is long-range, NCG-research-grade, and named here as the sharpest follow-on of the Sprint C2 finding.  A first answer --- covering the skeleton, the inner-factor family, the admitted value, and, at mechanism level on both corpus-faithful instances, the multi-focal family --- is the Bohr-site dichotomy of \S\ref{sec:open_bohr}.
